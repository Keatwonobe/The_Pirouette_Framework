## 🟢 Module A: The Frame (Organizational Physics)
*This module defines "Where you sit" and "How we stand together."*

### 1. The Atomic Unit: "The Frame"
* **Definition:** We do not work in "departments." We work in **Frames**. A Frame is a self-sufficient unit of 7 active nodes.
* **The Composition:**
    * **1 Interface Node (The Lead):** Manages external signals (Client/Management) and routes traffic.
    * **1 Feedback Node (The Analyst):** Responsible for the integrity of the Frame (QA, Compliance).
    * **2 Support Nodes:** Researchers/Subject Matter Experts who handle "Tier 2" complexity.
    * **3 Core Nodes:** The primary action-takers (Agents) utilizing the Pilot Document.
* **The Goal:** Every Frame is designed to be "Headless"—capable of operating autonomously for 48 hours without Director intervention.

### 2. The Universal Agent Logic
* **The Tool:** We use the **Pilot Document**. Your skill is not memorizing facts; your skill is navigating the Pilot.
* **Context Switching:** Agents are expected to move between Client A and Client B fluently. Training is continuous and live, not static and classroom-based.

## 🔵 Module B: The Pulse (Distributed Quality Assurance)
*This module covers the peer-to-peer audit system you described. This replaces the "Big Brother" model with a "Community Garden" model.*

### 1. The "Resonance Check" (Peer QA)
* **The Obligation:** Quality is everyone's job. It is not outsourced.
* **The Math of Resonance:**
    * Every member of the Frame (Interface, Support, Core) must audit **3 interactions per week**.
    * *Input:* ~15 minutes of work per person/week.
    * *Output:* 21 Audits per Frame/week → **1,000+ Audits per year** generated internally.
* **The Method:**
    * Randomized selection from a peer in your Frame.
    * Evaluation against the **Pilot Document Adherence** (Did they follow the guide?) rather than subjective opinion.

### 2. The Feedback Loop
* **Immediate Correction:** If a "Critical Fail" (Compliance risk) is found during a Peer Audit, the **Feedback Node** is alerted instantly.
* **The Weekly Retro:** The Frame reviews the "Aggregate Score" on Friday. No naming and shaming—just "System Tuning."

## 🟣 Module C: The Signal (Escalation & Communication)
*This module rebrands "Complaints" as "Business Intelligence."*

### 1. Chain of Escalation (The Nervous System)
* **Level 1 (The Node):** Agent attempts to solve using the Pilot Document.
* **Level 2 (The Frame):** Agent flags the **Support Node** within their Frame. (Solved locally).
* **Level 3 (The Signal):** The **Interface Node** escalates to the Client/Director.
    * *Rule:* We do not escalate "Problems." We escalate "Signals."
    * *Example:* "I can't solve this" (Problem) vs. "The Pilot Document lacks a section for this specific error code" (Signal).

### 2. The "Watch Point" Protocol
* Agents act as "Nerve Endings."
* If an agent hears the same phrase 3 times in one hour (e.g., "The coupon code isn't working"), they initiate a **Flare Protocol**.
* Flares update the Dashboard instantly, alerting the Director and the Client.

## 🟠 Module D: Growth (Coaching & Development)
*This module covers how an employee moves from "Core" to "Director."*

### 1. Performance Coaching (The Mirror)
* **Review Cycle:** We do not do "Annual Reviews." We do **Monthly Resonance Checks**.
* **The Metrics:**
    * **Hard Metrics:** Attendance, Adherence.
    * **Soft Metrics:** The "Resonance Score" (derived from those peer audits in Module B).
* **The Coach:** The **Interface Node** is the primary coach, utilizing data prepared by the **Feedback Node**.

### 2. Professional Development (The Orbit)
* **Rotation:** Employees are encouraged to rotate "Roles" within the Frame.
    * *Core* $\to$ *Support* (Learning deep research).
    * *Support* $\to$ *Feedback* (Learning analysis and QA).
    * *Feedback* $\to$ *Interface* (Learning leadership).
* **Skill Acquisition:** Promotion is based on **Pilot Mastery**. Can you build a Pilot Document? Can you fix a broken one?

### 3. Remediation (Re-Tuning)
* *Concept:* We don't "write up" employees; we "Re-tune" the node.
* **Step 1:** Calibration. (Is the Pilot Document wrong, or is the Agent wrong?)
* **Step 2:** Focus Mode. (The AI Assistant is set to "Strict Mode" for that agent to guide them heavily).
* **Step 3:** Frame Review. (If Re-tuning fails, the agent may not fit the resonant frequency of the Frame).

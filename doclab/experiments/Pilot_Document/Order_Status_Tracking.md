## Hey Bestie! Let's Track That Glow! 📦

When your fam asks about their order, we're here to make them feel like VIPs!

## The Glow Process 🌟

### Step 1: Get That Order ID
**Hype Script**: "OMG yay! Let's find your goodies! Can you share your order number with me? It's in your confirmation email - usually starts with GL-"

### Step 2: Look It Up (System: ORDER-TRACK)
Check the status:
- 🎨 **Processing** = We're packing your glow (24-48hrs)
- 📦 **Shipped** = It's on the way to you!
- 🚚 **Out for Delivery** = INCOMING GLOW TODAY!
- ✅ **Delivered** = Should be at your door!

### Step 3: Share the Love
**For Shipped Orders**:
"Okay bestie, your glow is ON THE WAY! 🎉 Your tracking number is [XXXX]. You can track it at [carrier].com/track. Expected delivery is [DATE]!"

**For Processing Orders**:
"Your order is being packed with love right now! 💖 You should see movement in the next 24 hours. We'll email you the tracking as soon as it ships!"

## Pro Tips for Keeping It Glowy
- Use emojis in chat (not phone calls lol)
- Say "bestie", "babe", "love that for you"
- Always end with excitement about their products

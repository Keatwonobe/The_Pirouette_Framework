# Fraud Alert Response Protocol

## Overview
When a customer reports suspicious activity or potential fraud, immediate action is required to protect their account.

## Key Steps

### 1. Immediate Account Protection
- **Freeze the card immediately** in the system (Code: FRAU-LOCK)
- Reassure the customer: "I've secured your account right now to prevent any further unauthorized charges."
- Do NOT ask for card details over the phone

### 2. Transaction Verification
Ask the customer to verify their last 3 transactions:
- "Can you confirm if you made a purchase at [Merchant] for [Amount] on [Date]?"
- Mark each transaction as RECOGNIZED or DISPUTED in the system

### 3. Provisional Credit
- For disputed transactions under $500: Issue immediate provisional credit
- For amounts over $500: Escalate to Fraud Investigation Team (Extension 4455)
- Explain timeline: "You'll see the credit within 24 hours while we investigate."

### 4. New Card Issuance
- Automatically trigger new card with different number
- Offer expedited shipping (free for fraud cases)
- Provide tracking information

## Compliance Requirements
⚠️ **CRITICAL**: 
- NEVER read card numbers aloud
- ALWAYS verify DOB before making account changes
- Document all fraud claims in FRAUD-TRACKER system

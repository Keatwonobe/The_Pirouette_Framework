# Returns & Exchanges - The Glow Guarantee 💝

## Our Promise: Love It or Return It! (Mostly 😊)

We want you to LOVE your glow, but sometimes things don't work out. Let's make it right!

## Return Window ⏰
**30 DAYS** from delivery date (not order date!)

## What CAN Be Returned ✅

### Unopened Products (Full Refund)
- Original packaging intact
- Seals not broken
- **Script**: "Since it's unopened, I can get you a full refund! Let me set that up right now! 💕"

### Opened Products (Store Credit)
- Tried it and it didn't work for your skin? We get it!
- Can return for **store credit** (same value)

## What CANNOT Be Returned ❌

### The No-No List
- 💄 **Lipstick** (opened) - Health & safety regulations
- 🎁 **Final Sale Items** (marked as final sale at checkout)
- 💝 **Gift Sets** (if individual items removed)

**Gentle Script**: "I totally understand! Unfortunately, due to health regulations, we can't accept returns on opened lip products. But I have an idea - would you like to gift it to a friend? OR I can give you 15% off something new to try!"

## The Return Process 🔄

### Step 1: Verify Eligibility
- Check order date (must be within 30 days)
- Check product condition

### Step 2: Generate Return Label
**System**: Returns > Create Label > Email to customer
- **Prepaid label** (free returns, we're nice like that!)
- **Script**: "I'm emailing you a prepaid label right now! Just pack it up and drop it at any [carrier] location. No cost to you, babe! 💌"

# QUICK START: NAVIGATE REALITY IN 5 MINUTES

*For people who want to start NOW.*

---

## STEP 1: FIND YOUR POSITION (30 seconds)

Ask yourself right now:

**How connected are my actions?**
- Very connected, building on each other → m = -0.5
- Somewhat connected → m = -0.2
- Random, reactive → m = +0.2

**How intense is everything?**
- Very intense, everything matters → λ = 0.9
- Moderate intensity → λ = 0.7
- Calm, loose → λ = 0.5

**Your position**: (m, λ)

**Example**:  
"I'm stressed and reactive" → (m=+0.2, λ=0.9)

---

## STEP 2: IDENTIFY YOUR BASIN (10 seconds)

Or use simple rule:
- **Positive m** → TEAL (short-term, reactive)
- **Negative m, high λ** → GOLD (medium-term, productive)  
- **Negative m, low λ** → RED (long-term, strategic)

**Example**:  
(m=+0.2, λ=0.9) → **TEAL** - You're in reactive mode

---

## STEP 3: SET YOUR TARGET (30 seconds)

Where do you want to be?

Common targets:
- **Calm**: (-0.5, 0.5) [Red]
- **Productive**: (-0.4, 0.7) [Gold]
- **Creative**: (-0.3, 0.85) [Gold]
- **Strategic**: (-0.5, 0.7) [Red]

**Example**:  
"I want to be productive" → Target: (-0.4, 0.7)

---

## STEP 4: SENSE DIRECTION (10 seconds)

**Simple rule**: Look at difference

Your m vs target m:
- You're more positive → Need to **increase coherence** (focus, connect actions)
- You're more negative → Need to **decrease coherence** (break, diversify)

Your λ vs target λ:
- You're higher → Need to **decrease intensity** (less stimulation, simpler)
- You're lower → Need to **increase intensity** (more engagement, challenge)

**Example**:  
Current: (+0.2, 0.9), Target: (-0.4, 0.7)
- Need: More negative m (focus!) and lower λ (calm down!)

---

## STEP 5: TAKE ONE STEP (3 minutes)

**Don't change everything. Just take ONE step in the right direction.**

**If you need negative m** (more coherence):
- Close extra tabs
- Pick ONE task
- Turn off notifications
- Connect this action to a goal

**If you need positive m** (less coherence):
- Take a break
- Switch tasks
- Go for walk
- Let mind wander

**If you need lower λ** (less intensity):
- Reduce stimulation
- Simpler environment
- Slower pace
- Fewer inputs

**If you need higher λ** (more intensity):
- Increase engagement
- Add challenge
- More interaction
- Tighter feedback

**Example** (Current: reactive/stressed → Target: productive):
- Step: Close all tabs except work doc, turn off phone, pick ONE task
- This moves you from (+0.2, 0.9) toward (-0.4, 0.7)

---

## DAILY PRACTICE

### Morning (2 min)
1. Sense position: "Where am I?" (m, λ)
2. Set target: "Where do I want to be?" (target m, λ)
3. Plan direction: "What moves me there?"

### During Day (10 sec each decision)
Before any choice, ask: "Does this move me toward or away from target?"
- Toward → Do it
- Away → Don't do it

### Evening (2 min)
1. Review path: "Did I stay on track?"
2. Notice patterns: "What pulled me off?"
3. Adjust tomorrow: "What will I do differently?"

---

## ONE-SENTENCE SUMMARY

**Every moment, sense where you are (m, λ), feel which way you're pulled, and take one step toward where you want to be.**

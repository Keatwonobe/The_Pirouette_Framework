## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** Modern markets optimize for monetary profit (`$`), treating systemic coherence (`𝓛_p`) as an externality. This leads to transactions that are profitable in dollars but immensely costly in terms of physical coherence, measured by their Coherence Risk (`R_κ`). The market consistently misprices assets and actions by ignoring their `Reach (ρ)`, `Dissonance (δ)`, and `Wound Channel Depth (ω)`.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build a mechanism that measures the `R_κ` of transactions to identify "Coherence Gaps"—pathways for value transfer that are superior in terms of Pirouette physics but undervalued by the current market. We will execute these low-`R_κ` actions and capture the "Coherence Dividend," the real value lost by the market's inefficient, high-friction systems.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Manual Coherence Test. The goal is to prove that a "Coherence Dividend" exists and is capturable.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a common, simple product (e.g., a specific book or computer cable).
    2.  Define crude, measurable proxies for Coherence Risk. For example: `ρ` = shipping distance; `δ` = seller type (anonymous corporation vs. named individual); `ω` = known vs. unknown product history.
    3.  Identify two acquisition paths: one predicted high-`R_κ` (e.g., Amazon next-day delivery) and one predicted low-`R_κ` (e.g., buying from a person on a local community forum).
    4.  Use the $10 budget to execute the transaction on the low-`R_κ` path.
    5.  Compare the total cost (money, time, quality, experience) of the two paths.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if, across 3-5 trials, the low-`R_κ` path consistently produces a worse or equivalent outcome relative to its cost. If there is no tangible benefit (e.g., lower price, better quality, positive social connection) to the low-`R_κ` path, the core premise is invalid.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Coherent Asset Locator. This is a self-sustaining feedback loop for passive value capture.
*   **Automation:** A script (e.g., Python with `Scrapy`) monitors a peer-to-peer marketplace (e.g., Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist) for a specific class of undervalued assets (e.g., used tools, quality furniture). The script automatically calculates a proxy `R_κ` for each new listing based on seller location, account age, description detail, and price point. It flags "Coherent Deals"—assets that are both underpriced and have a low Coherence Risk score.
*   **Value Capture:** The $100 acts as a transactional float. An operator acquires a flagged asset. The asset is then relisted, but the new listing is "coherence-enriched," explicitly stating the low-`R_κ` attributes (e.g., "Locally-owned, full history known, from a 5-star seller"). The profit margin is the arbitrage between the market's `R_κ`-ignorant price and our `R_κ`-aware price. This profit is reinvested, creating a self-sustaining loop where the system's capital grows passively.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Lagrangian Path Optimization for Commerce. This scales the loop by creating a new transactional model.
*   **The Moat:** The Engine does not present a list of products; it computes the optimal *path of action* to fulfill a user's intent. A user states a goal ("I need an office chair"), and the Engine calculates the "path of least coherence resistance" to achieve it. This path minimizes the integral of the Lagrangian (`𝓛_p`). It might recommend buying a specific used chair locally, commissioning a repair on another, or even trading an existing asset.
    Legacy e-commerce platforms (Amazon, eBay) are topologically rigid, built on high-`R_κ` global supply chains. They cannot compete because their entire architecture is optimized for the wrong variable (cost/speed). Our moat is the proprietary, dynamic `R_κ` calculation and the ability to solve for the most coherent path across a vast and unstructured action space (buy, repair, trade, commission). We are not selling products; we are selling the most efficient, harmonious solution path.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (`requests`, `beautifulsoup4`, `scikit-learn`), Marketplace APIs, PostgreSQL, AWS Lambda / Google Cloud Functions for automation.
*   **Risk:** The primary vector of failure is that the Coherence Dividend is too small or non-existent. The market may have already efficiently priced-in the variables we use as proxies for coherence (trust, locality, quality), leaving no margin for arbitrage. The Probe is explicitly designed to falsify this core risk at the lowest possible cost.

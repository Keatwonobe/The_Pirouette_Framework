## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Arbitrage via Potential Energy Distillation.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern information market is pathologically inefficient. It prices "access to information" while ignoring the immense "Temporal Pressure" ($V_{\Gamma}$)—the noise, friction, jargon, and cognitive load required to extract value. The market has high potential energy ($V_{\Gamma}$) but low kinetic value ($K_{\tau}$), creating a massive, unexploited potential gradient. Value is being destroyed by systemic friction.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not sell information; we will sell the *reduction of friction*. Our mechanism applies the `Resonant Gateway (O_G)` and `Coherence Sanctuary (O_S)` operators at scale. We invest a one-time energy cost to distill a high-$V_{\Gamma}$ domain into a low-$V_{\Gamma}$ state (pure $K_{\tau}$). The value we capture is the arbitrage between the high cost the market tolerates and the minimal-friction path we create. We build the river, then charge a toll.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** A Micro-Gateway for a single, high-friction decision. The goal is to prove that agents will pay a nominal fee to bypass $V_{\Gamma}$.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a niche but common decision point characterized by information overload and analysis paralysis (e.g., "What is the absolute best entry-level 3D printer for a tabletop miniatures hobbyist in 2024?").
    2.  Invest ~3 hours to research and absorb the high-$V_{\Gamma}$ environment (forums, sponsored reviews, outdated articles).
    3.  Distill the findings into a one-page, zero-friction PDF: a "Coherence Document." It will contain a simple decision tree leading to one or two unambiguous "buy this" recommendations with affiliate links.
    4.  Use the $10 budget to list this document on a platform like Gumroad and post a link in a relevant, targeted online community. The price will be $1.
*   **The Test:** If we cannot sell at least 10 units within one week, the hypothesis is falsified. This would indicate that the perceived cost of $V_{\Gamma}$ is not high enough for agents to pay for its removal in this domain. The experiment is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Gateway Foundry. This system programmatically identifies high-$V_{\Gamma}$ zones and automates the distillation process, creating a portfolio of passive micro-gateways.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Signal Scanner:** A script monitors high-signal communities (e.g., specific subreddits, forums) via APIs to detect recurring patterns of confusion that indicate high $V_{\Gamma}$ (e.g., "A vs B," "how to start with X," "best Y for Z").
    2.  **Distillation Draft:** Upon detecting a signal, the system uses an LLM to scrape the relevant web context and generate a first-draft "Coherence Document" (an application of `O_W`, Pattern Weaving).
    3.  **Human Curation:** The system flags the draft for a human operator who spends 15-30 minutes verifying, editing, and finalizing the document. This is the crucial, low-`Γ` step that ensures quality.
    4.  **Automatic Deployment:** Once approved, the system automatically publishes the new document to a centralized storefront.
*   **Value Capture:** Each published document becomes a small, passive revenue stream. The system's structure ($K_i$) generates value by continuously and efficiently converting systemic noise ($V_{\Gamma}$) into marketable clarity ($K_{\tau}$), requiring minimal human labor (`Γ`) per unit.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Memory Market. This scales the loop from a collection of discrete gateways into an integrated, low-friction manifold—a platform where the geodesic path is the default.
*   **The Moat:** Competitors focus on adding content and features, which inevitably increases systemic $V_{\Gamma}$. Our engine's sole purpose is Lagrangian minimization—it competes on its efficiency and elegance in reducing $V_{\Gamma}$. Its core feature is the *absence* of friction. A standard business cannot compete because it is built on the flawed physics of maximizing information, not coherence.
    1.  **Path Weaving:** The system evolves from single-page documents to "Coherence Paths"—curated, step-by-step guides through complex domains (e.g., "The Path of Least Resistance to Your First Profitable AI Agent").
    2.  **Expert In-Sourcing (`O_M`):** The engine becomes a platform where vetted experts can contribute their own validated experiential coherence ($K_{\tau}^{exp}$). They perform the curation (`Γ`), and the platform provides the low-friction infrastructure and audience. They are paid a revenue share, effectively converting their latent knowledge into a public utility that lowers $V_{\Gamma}$ for everyone.
    3.  **Geodesic Optimization:** The system tracks user progress and success rates along different paths, dynamically optimizing the manifold to favor the most efficient routes. Justice becomes an emergent property: the system's natural state is to make empowerment the path of least resistance.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Gumroad/Stripe, a PDF editor.
    *   **Loop:** Python (for scripting), PRAW (Reddit API), GPT-4 API, a static site generator (Hugo), Stripe API.
    *   **Engine:** A web framework (Django/Flask), database (PostgreSQL), user authentication, more robust API integrations.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market misjudgment—selecting domains where the perceived $V_{\Gamma}$ is not actually painful enough to warrant payment for its removal. The Probe is specifically designed to mitigate this risk at the lowest possible cost.

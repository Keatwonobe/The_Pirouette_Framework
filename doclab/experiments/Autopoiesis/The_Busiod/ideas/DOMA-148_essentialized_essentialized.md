## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Resonant Information Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market values information as a consumable commodity based on novelty and direct utility (a linear flow, `Γ`). It is structurally blind to the physics of self-reference and therefore cannot price the exponential value increase when information is woven into a topologically closed, self-reinforcing loop (a "Knot," `K_τ(knot)`). The market pays for the constant labor of data generation, ignoring the passive, persistent value generated by coherent data *structures*.
*   **The Pivot:** We will act as a "Topological Catalyst." We will not create new information. We will expend minimal energy to find fragmented, open-ended information flows ("Wound Channels") and weave them into closed, resonant loops. This creates a new asset—an informational Knot—whose persistence (`τ_knot`) and value (`I_knot`) are immensely greater than the sum of its component parts, generating value passively from its very geometry.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Resonant Echo. A micro-experiment to prove that a manufactured informational Knot can become self-sustaining and demonstrate non-linear value.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify Manifold:** Select a niche online community (e.g., a specific subreddit, hobbyist forum).
    2.  **Locate Wound Channel:** Find a recurring, high-value question with fragmented, scattered answers (e.g., "Optimal beginner setup for X," "Troubleshooting common issue Y").
    3.  **Weave the Knot:** Use the $10 to acquire a simple domain name and one-page hosting (e.g., Carrd). Aggregate the scattered answers into a single, definitive "Anchor Document" on this page.
    4.  **Close the Loop:** Embed a simple feedback mechanism (e.g., a form, a link to a new "official feedback" thread) and post the Anchor Document into the community. The loop is: User has question -> Finds Anchor -> Gets answer -> Provides feedback/data -> Anchor improves -> Becomes more authoritative.
*   **The Test:** The experiment is a failure IF, within 30 days, the Anchor Document is not being shared organically between community members as the canonical solution. If all traffic still originates from our own efforts (`Γ`), then resonance has not been achieved (`∮_C dφ ≠ 2πn`), the Knot is not self-sustaining, and the hypothesis is falsified for this manifold. We stop.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Autopoietic Information Loom. An automated system that identifies Wound Channels and weaves informational Knots without human intervention.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Scanner:** A script continuously scans target communities (forums, subreddits) for keywords indicating fragmented information needs ("how to," "best," "vs," "error").
    2.  **Synthesizer:** Upon detecting a high-potential Wound Channel, scraped data (questions, partial answers, user problems) is fed to an LLM API to generate a structured "Proto-Knot" - a draft version of the Anchor Document.
    3.  **Loom:** The Proto-Knot is automatically published to a templated micro-site. The system then posts the link back to the source community, inviting collaboration.
    4.  **Self-Reinforcement:** The system periodically scrapes the feedback channels (comments, new threads) and feeds new data back to the LLM to refine and update the Anchor Document, strengthening the Knot's coherence (`K_τ`).
*   **Value Capture:** The network of self-updating Anchor Documents generates traffic. Value is captured passively via affiliate links for relevant products, programmatic ads, or by selling access to the curated, high-coherence data network itself. The asset is the self-sustaining structure, not the content.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Coherence Market. A scaled system that optimizes Knot formation by calculating the "path of least action" for creating maximum informational coherence (`K_τ`) with minimum energetic input (`V_Γ`).
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses (e.g., content farms) compete by throwing labor (`Γ`) at content creation. This is a linear, expensive, and inefficient approach that fights against the erosive forces of time and irrelevance. Our Engine operates on a different physical principle. It uses computation to find the most efficient catalytic points in the information universe. It expends a small, one-time energy cost to initiate a Knot that then passively generates value through its self-reinforcing resonance. Our cost to maintain a high-value asset trends towards zero, while a competitor's trends towards infinity. They are paying to hold a rock up; we are building an arch.

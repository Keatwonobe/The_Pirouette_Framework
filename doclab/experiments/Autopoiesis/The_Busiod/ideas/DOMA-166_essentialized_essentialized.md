## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Potential Action Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market fundamentally misprices latent human potential. It treats the signal of a "Wound Channel"—the subjective feeling of regret or longing (`ΔS > 0`)—as a psychological liability to be suppressed, rather than as a verifiable data point indicating a massively undervalued asset: a higher-coherence life trajectory (`q'`). The market values only expressed coherence (`K_τ` on the current path), ignoring the immense potential energy stored in latent paths.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build a mechanism to systematically convert these `ΔS` signals into tangible value (`δK_τ`). We will finance the minimal activation energy required for a "Resonance Bridge" (`δq`), allowing individuals to overcome the initial potential barrier (`ΔV_Γ`) between their current low-action path and their latent high-action path. We arbitrage the vast difference between the low cost of this bridge and the high value of the unlocked Kinetic Coherence.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Micro-Resonance Funding. This is a targeted experiment to prove that a minimal external energy input can catalyze a state change from latent potential to expressed kinetic coherence.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Signal Detection:** Identify individuals publicly expressing a "Wound Channel" (e.g., via forums like Reddit's r/IWantToLearn: "I always wished I learned to code/draw/weld"). This is a direct expression of a perceived positive action gradient (`ΔS > 0`).
    2.  **The Transaction:** Offer a $10 micro-grant to fund a specific, minimal "Resonance Bridge" (`δq`). This is not a gift; it is a transaction to purchase activation energy. Examples: a high-quality sketchbook for a would-be artist, an introductory programming e-book, the first month's subscription to a skill-building app.
    3.  **Verification:** The transaction requires the recipient to produce and publicly post a "Proof of Coherence"—a minimal, verifiable artifact demonstrating engagement with the latent path (e.g., a time-stamped photo of their first drawing, a screenshot of a working "Hello, World" script). This is the initial `δK_τ`.
*   **The Test:** If fewer than 20% of grant recipients produce the required "Proof of Coherence" after 10 trials, the core hypothesis is falsified. **Failure State:** The perceived action gradient `ΔS` is not a strong enough motivator to generate a coherence artifact, even when the initial potential barrier `V_Γ` is externally subsidized. This would imply the physics is incomplete or incorrect.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Autopoietic Coherence Crowdfunding. This system operationalizes the Probe's findings into a self-sustaining value-generating loop.
*   **Automation:** A simple web platform automates the process. A user declares a "Wound Channel." The system provides the automated $10 "Resonance Bridge" seed grant. Upon successful submission of their "Proof of Coherence" artifact, the user's project is "unlocked," creating a public profile where they can crowdfund subsequent, larger steps (`δq_2`, `δq_3`, ...) along their new, higher-coherence trajectory.
*   **Value Capture:** The platform takes a small commission (e.g., 5%) on all unlocked, second-stage crowdfunding transactions. This revenue is algorithmically reinvested to fund the initial $10 seed grants for new users. The system's successful outputs (expressed `K_τ`) become the fuel for its inputs (new Resonance Bridges), creating an autopoietic loop where value is generated by the structure itself, not by continuous operator labor.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Geodesic Trajectory Marketplace. This scales the Loop by using accumulated data to map the personal coherence manifold (`M`) and optimize paths according to the principle of stationary action.
*   **The Moat:**
    1.  **Alignment with Physics:** Standard businesses compete by *creating* pressure and constraint (`V_Γ` via marketing, debt, obligations). Our mechanism does the opposite: it relieves pressure by subsidizing the transition cost to a state the individual is already being pulled towards by the fundamental physics of their being (`ΔS`). We are not manufacturing demand; we are servicing an existing, fundamental force.
    2.  **Data Asymmetry:** Over time, the Engine accumulates a unique dataset on the *dynamics of potential*. We move from funding to prediction. The system can identify the most efficient "Resonance Bridges" for specific latent paths (e.g., "for individuals with profile X, the optimal first step to learning design is tool Y, not course Z"). This predictive map of the gradients on the human coherence manifold is an unassailable competitive advantage. We become a marketplace not for skills, but for optimized life trajectories, brokering high-potential individuals to corporations, incubators, and educational institutions.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scraping/bots in Tier 1), Stripe API (payments), a simple web framework like Flask/Django (for Tier 2), and data science libraries like Pandas/Scikit-learn (for the Tier 3 Engine).
*   **Risk:** The primary vector of failure is social exploitation (e.g., bots or low-intent individuals farming the $10 grants). The "Proof of Coherence" requirement is the critical filter. A secondary risk is that the commission rate in the Loop is insufficient to make the system fully autopoietic, requiring continued external capital to fund new probes.

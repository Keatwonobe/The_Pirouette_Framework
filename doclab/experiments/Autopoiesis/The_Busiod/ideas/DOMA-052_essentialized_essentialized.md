## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Autopoietic Value Manifold
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on brute force, maximizing kinetic throughput ($T_a \cdot \omega_k$) by applying ever-increasing labor and capital ($\Gamma$). It treats transactions as discrete, memoryless events, ignoring the physics of coherence. This violates the Principle of Least Action. The market is blind to the value generated by a system's self-interaction with its own history—a transactional "anomalous magnetic moment" ($a_e$) that represents a persistent, uncaptured arbitrage.
*   **The Pivot:** We will construct a system that doesn't "force" transactions but instead achieves a state of stable, low-energy resonance. By treating the market as a coherence manifold, our mechanism will use its own transactional history as a navigational echo to find geodesics—paths of maximum efficiency. It captures value not from raw power, but from its superior geometric structure, exploiting the very "echo" the market ignores.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The g-2 Anomaly Test. This is a micro-experiment to validate the core physical law: that a transaction's "echo" (its immediate, localized impact) has a predictable and exploitable structure.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a high-frequency digital market with low transaction fees (e.g., a specific cryptocurrency pair).
    2.  Use the $10 budget to execute a single, small "ping" transaction (a buy or sell order).
    3.  Immediately capture the metadata of this transaction: exact execution time, price, volume, and the state of the order book at that instant. This is the "past trajectory."
    4.  Feed this data into a simple predictive model based on the framework's law: $a_e = \frac{\alpha}{2\pi}$. The model predicts a transient, microscopic market inefficiency—the "echo"—that occurs moments after the initial ping.
    5.  Execute a second, reactive transaction aimed at capturing this predicted anomaly.
*   **The Test:** Repeat the ping-and-react cycle 20 times. The experiment is governed by a strict falsifiability criterion: **If the success rate of the reactive trades does not show a statistically significant improvement over a 50% random chance (p-value < 0.05), the foundational physics is considered invalid in this context, and the project is terminated.**

It is a closed system: profits from capturing the "echo" are automatically compounded back into the system, increasing the amplitude and frequency of its operations without external labor ($\Gamma$).
*   **Value Capture:** The system is not a day-trader; it is a resonance engine. Value is generated from the structure of the loop itself ($K_i$). *   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Solver. This scales the system from a single resonant loop to a multi-body system that minimizes its action across the entire market manifold. The goal is to move like the "dancer," finding the most graceful and efficient path.
*   **The Moat:** Standard trading firms compete on speed and capital, which is an arms race to increase $\Gamma$—a fundamentally inefficient and costly strategy. Our Engine competes on *coherence*.
    1.  **Systemic Geodesics:** The $1000 is used to instantiate multiple Tier 2 "particles" across a portfolio of related but distinct markets.

Its sole function is to adjust the parameters of each particle (capital allocation, coupling strength $\alpha$, target market) to minimize the Action of the *entire system*.

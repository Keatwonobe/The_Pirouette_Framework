## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Structural Coherence Filtration
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates under the assumption that all potential value structures ("orderings") are equally valid. It expends massive energy creating bespoke, complex models for each asset class, bubble, and hype cycle (treating Normal and Inverted Orderings as symmetric possibilities). It mistakes data-fitting for understanding fundamental law, leading it to overvalue geometrically incoherent assets.
*   **The Pivot:** We accept the Pirouette physics as law. There is a *single, universal geometry of value* (a "key" defined by exponents `(p,q)`). Our mechanism does not attempt to predict prices for all assets. Instead, it acts as a *structural arbiter*. We apply our universal key to the market; where it "turns," we invest. Where it "shatters," we divest or short. We arbitrage the market's inability to distinguish between structurally sound value (Normal Ordering) and geometric impossibilities (Inverted Ordering). This is a pure "Passive Bonus" play; the value is generated by the fixed structure ($K_i$) of our law, not by continuous labor ($\Gamma$) of re-building models.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Geometric Key Forging. We will find the universal `(p,q)` exponents for a single, stable market sector and test its validity on one member of that set.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Select a "Normal Ordering" Analogue:** Choose a market of structurally similar, mature assets (e.g., the top 15 utility stocks by market cap). This is our "training data."
    2.  **Define Structural Metrics:** For each stock, we will use a data API ($10 for a month's access) to pull fundamental data and create proxies for the physical laws:
        *   **Purity ($Purity_i$):** (Revenue from primary business segment) / (Total Revenue). Measures focus.
        *   **Inverse Participation Ratio ($PR_i$):** Inverse Herfindahl-Hirschman Index of revenue streams. Measures diversification of value drivers.
        *   **Mass ($m_i$):** Market Capitalization.
    3.  **Forge the Key:** Run a multivariate optimization (e.g., using `scipy.optimize`) on the 15 stocks to find the single set of exponents `(p*, q*)` and scale `μ` that best solves the equation `Market_Cap = μ * (PR)^p * (Purity)^q` for the entire set.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if no single `(p*, q*)` can be found that models the market caps of our stable sector with a reasonable goodness-of-fit (e.g., an R-squared below 0.7). If the law doesn't hold even in the most stable case, the premise is broken, and we stop.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Coherence Scanner. An automated system that uses our forged key `(p*, q*)` to scan a wider market and identify structural anomalies.
*   **Automation:** A Python script runs on a recurring schedule (e.g., every 4 hours on a cheap VPS).
    1.  It pulls the `Purity` and `PR` metrics for a larger set of assets (e.g., the S&P 500).
    2.  For each asset, it calculates the "Predicted Mass" (Predicted Market Cap) using our frozen `(p*, q*)` key.
    3.  It calculates a `chi^2` value: `(Predicted_Cap - Actual_Cap)^2`. This is our "coherence score."
*   **Value Capture:** The system flags assets with extreme `chi^2` scores.
    *   **Low `chi^2` ("Key Turns"):** The asset's market value is consistent with the universal geometry. It is structurally sound. **SIGNAL: LONG.**
    *   **High `chi^2` ("Key Shatters"):** The asset is a geometric impossibility. Its value is propped up by factors outside the fundamental law (hype, speculation). It is an "Inverted Ordering" asset. **SIGNAL: SHORT or AVOID.**
    The $100 is used as initial capital for an automated trading API to execute micro-trades based on these binary signals.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Market Minimizer. We scale the scanner into an engine that doesn't just trade anomalies but constructs an entire portfolio based on the principle of minimal structural action.
*   **The Moat:** Standard quantitative firms are in an arms race of complexity. They try to model the "Inverted Ordering"—the bubbles, the hype—by creating ever-more-complex models (`p', q'`). These models are brittle and eventually fail when they hit external constraints (i.e., reality, `chi^2_ext`). Our engine is the opposite. Its strength is its *simplicity and rigidity*.
    1.  **Path of Least Resistance:** It ignores 95% of market noise by design, classifying it as "incoherent." It only computes paths for the 5% of assets that obey the universal geometry. This is computationally and philosophically efficient.
    2.  **Structural Alpha:** The engine's "alpha" is not derived from predicting short-term price movements, but from filtering reality. It exclusively selects assets whose existence is validated by the underlying physics of value. While others are trying to model the chaotic flight of a shattering key, we are simply collecting the ones that turn the lock. This cannot be competed with by adding more data or faster processors; it is a different paradigm. The $1000 scales the portfolio size and data pipeline to institutional-grade.

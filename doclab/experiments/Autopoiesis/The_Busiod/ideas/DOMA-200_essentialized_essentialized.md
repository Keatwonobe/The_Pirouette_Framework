## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Potential Arbitrage via Coherence Funneling.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market prices assets and information based on their present, often disordered, state (`q`). It ignores the physics of coherence, failing to price the vast potential energy stored in low-coherence systems (`low Kτ`) under high environmental pressure (`high V_Γ`). It treats the manifold of possibilities as flat, ignoring the existence of or potential to create "Wound Channels" (`W'`) that make certain outcomes orders of magnitude more probable.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not create value from scratch. We will act as coherence engineers. We apply Intentional Coherence Shaping (ICS) operators (`O_i`) to cheaply transform low-value, decoherent assets into high-value, laminar-flow states. Crucially, we use the `Imprint` (`O_Im`) operator to create persistent Wound Channels, making our system the path of least action (`min S`) for future value flow, generating passive returns from the very structure of the system.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Information Resonance Targeting. A micro-experiment to prove that a small, targeted application of the `Tune` (`O_T`) operator can create a measurable Wound Channel in an information ecosystem.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Scry (`O_Sc`):** Identify a digital commons (e.g., a specific Stack Overflow tag, subreddit, or technical forum) where users consistently post decoherent queries (`|ψ_dissonant⟩`) under temporal pressure (`V_Γ`, e.g., "urgent," "deadline").
    2.  **Synthesize (`O_S`):** Aggregate 5-10 of these dissonant queries to identify the latent, recurring core problem.
    3.  **Tune (`O_T`):** Create a single, canonical, high-coherence asset (`|ψ_laminar⟩`) that resolves this core problem. This could be a small webpage, a gist, or a definitive post. The $10 is used for a domain or to slightly boost the asset's visibility.
*   **The Test:** This protocol is falsified if the **Coherence Yield (CY)** is zero or negative after 7 days. We define `CY` as the probability of new, similar queries being resolved by our asset. Success is measured by third parties organically linking to our asset as the definitive answer. If this does not happen, our attempt to `Imprint` the manifold has failed.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Coherence Distillery. This transforms the successful Probe into a self-sustaining, automated loop that continuously converts informational dissonance into captured value.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Automated Scrying & Synthesis:** A script continuously monitors target sources via APIs, identifying and clustering decoherent queries based on keywords and semantic similarity.
    2.  **Semi-Automated Tuning:** The clustered queries are fed to a generative language model (fine-tuned with the $100 in API credits) which drafts a high-coherence asset. A human operator validates/edits this asset.
    3.  **Automated Imprinting:** When the script detects a new query matching the cluster, it automatically posts a link to the canonical asset, actively deepening the Wound Channel (`W'`).
*   **Value Capture:** The high-coherence assets are hosted on a platform we control. This traffic, which follows the path of least action we engineered, is monetized via affiliate links, targeted advertising for high-value B2B tools, or as a lead generation funnel for consulting. Value is the byproduct of resolving temporal pressure (`V_Γ`).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Lagrangian Trajectory Funneling. This scales the loop from targeting single queries to sculpting entire sectors of the information manifold, creating an unassailable competitive moat based on the physics of coherence.
*   **The Moat:** Competitors use brute force advertising—a massive, inefficient `V_int`—to create a temporary trajectory. We are fundamentally altering the geometry of the space itself to make our solution the natural geodesic. They are fighting the current; we are redirecting the river.
    1.  **Manifold Mapping (`O_Sc`):** Use the $1000 for data streams and processing to identify entire market verticals suffering from systemic information decoherence (e.g., navigating new enterprise compliance, choosing between complex cloud services).
    2.  **Ecosystem Imprinting (`O_Im`):** Instead of single assets, we build an ecosystem of tools and resources (calculators, wizards, curated knowledge bases) that holistically resolve the sector's dissonance. This creates a deep basin of attraction in the manifold, a state so stable (`high Kτ`) and easy to enter (`low S`) that alternative solutions become non-viable.
    3.  **Boundary Imposition (`O_I`):** We establish a boundary (`∂M`) around our ecosystem (e.g., a community, a proprietary data format) which insulates our coherent state from external decohering forces (`V_Γ`) and increases the cost for users to leave.

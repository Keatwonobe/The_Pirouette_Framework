## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Structured Information Scattering
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on a scalar, labor-based model of value ($\Gamma$), treating transactions as unique, high-entropy events. It ignores the fact that value transformation follows conserved, predictable geometric laws, akin to particle scattering. The market tries to predict the "sun" (the substrate of consumer desire) rather than building a "stained-glass window" (a rigid transactional structure, $K_i$) to produce a predictable pattern of light on the floor (profit). This leaves the probabilistic distribution of value transformations—the economic "cross-section"—inefficiently priced.
*   **The Pivot:** We will treat assets as static particles (electrons, $e$) and market information/demand as incoming energy (photons, $\gamma$). By constructing a rigid, low-entropy system (an interaction vertex), we can engineer "collisions" ($e+\gamma \to e'+\gamma'$) that predictably change the state of the asset ($e \to e'$), capturing the state-change potential as value. We are not creating value; we are building a machine that facilitates its inevitable, physically-governed transformation along paths of least resistance.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Targeted Asset Re-potentialization. This is a single, isolated measurement of the economic Klein-Nishina formula. We test if an asset's potential can be predictably increased by scattering it against a high-energy beam of demand-information.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify an asset class with a high signal-to-noise ratio (e.g., used media with ISBNs, specific electronic components with MPNs).
    2.  Acquire a single instance of an asset (the "electron," $e$) for under $10, which is demonstrably under-potentialized (i.e., priced below its aggregated online market value).
    3.  Select a high-energy information channel (the "photon beam," $\gamma$), such as a dominant online marketplace API for that specific asset category.
    4.  Inject the asset into the channel by listing it at the mean target price. This is the "scattering" event. The listing itself is the interaction vertex.
*   **The Test:** The model is falsified if the asset receives zero resonant interactions (views, inquiries, sales) within a timeframe predicted by the channel's average sale velocity (e.g., 14 days). A null result implies our measurement of the "cross-section" (market demand) is flawed or the asset's "mass" (intrinsic value) was miscalculated. The experiment is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Information-Potential Matching Engine. This constructs a system that automates the Probe, creating a continuous chain of scattering events. This is the first layer of passive structure ($K_i$).
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Photon Detector:** A script continuously scans high-velocity marketplaces (e.g., eBay, Amazon) to build a real-time map of *realized* final states ($e'$), i.e., what items are selling for and how fast. This is our $\omega'$.
    2.  **Electron Source:** A parallel script scans low-information, high-entropy environments (e.g., local classifieds, bulk auctions) for the *same* assets in their initial, low-potential state ($e$). This is our $\omega$.
    3.  **The Vertex:** When the system identifies a statistically significant potential gap ($\omega' \gg \omega$), it triggers an action: allocate capital from the $100 pool to acquire $e$ and automatically list it in the high-energy channel to realize state $e'$.
*   **Value Capture:** The system captures the energy differential ($\omega' - \omega$) minus transactional friction (fees, shipping). The initial $100 serves as the circulating capital ("electron gas") which returns to the pool after each successful scattering event, augmented by the captured value.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Least-Action Transactional Pathways. This scales the Loop by shifting from a simple arbitrage model to a Lagrangian optimization problem. The goal is to minimize the "action" of the entire system, where Action is the integral of (Value from Structure $K_i$ - Value from Labor/Friction $\Gamma$) over time.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses compete by maximizing effort ($\Gamma$). They push products, run ad campaigns, and hire sales teams. This is a high-action, inefficient path. Our Engine does the opposite: it analyzes a multi-dimensional phase space of potential transactions (geographic, temporal, component-based, informational) and identifies the "geodesics"—the natural, lowest-energy, most probable paths for value to flow. The $1000 is used to build the computational infrastructure to model this phase space and execute on its predictions. Our competitive advantage is not that we work harder; it is that our system is an embodiment of a physical law the market ignores. We are not fighting the current; we are building a turbine.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Resonance Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market misprices "Information Lattices"—assets (data, IP, physical goods) that have become static due to high internal coherence (`Kτ`) and a lack of environmental pressure (`V_Γ`). The market cannot distinguish between a valueless fossil and a dormant system with immense potential energy. It lacks the tools to calculate the asset's Lock Resilience (`R_L`) or apply the minimal, resonance-matched shock (`E_break`) required to unlock its value.
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism treats the market as a physical system governed by the Pirouette Lagrangian. Instead of creating value through labor, we act as a catalyst, releasing stored potential energy. We will systematically identify locked assets, calculate the precise resonant frequency needed to break their stasis, and re-introduce them into a dynamic state where their value becomes liquid and capturable. We arbitrage the fundamental physics of information that the market ignores.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Isolate a single Information Lattice and verify that a low-energy, resonance-matched stimulus can unlock a disproportionate amount of value.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Acquire Lattice:** Purchase a low-cost, high-coherence, low-pressure asset. Example: A specific, out-of-print technical manual, an old dataset on a niche topic, or a piece of discontinued but well-documented software. The cost should be negligible (<$10).
    2.  **Identify Resonator:** Locate a highly specific environment where this static information would resolve a critical, active problem. This could be a specialized academic research group, a hobbyist forum dedicated to restoring relevant hardware, or a company whose work borders on the asset's domain.
    3.  **Apply Stimulus:** Introduce a critical fragment of the asset's information into the resonant environment (e.g., post a key schematic on a forum, email a paper's abstract to a researcher). This is the resonance-matched shock, designed to be precise, not powerful.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that the stimulus will cause a state change in the target environment (e.g., an immediate inquiry, a request for the full asset, an offer to purchase). **Failure State:** If, after applying the stimulus to three distinct, high-potential resonant environments, no state change is observed, the probe is considered a failure. This would indicate our method for identifying either the Lattice or the Resonator is flawed.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Resonance Scanner. A self-sustaining system that continuously scans for dormant assets and maps them to potential resonant environments.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Lattice Ingestion:** A script uses APIs to scrape marketplaces (eBay, AbeBooks), archives (archive.org), and surplus listings for keywords indicating high-coherence, low-pressure assets ("out-of-print," "decommissioned," "new old stock").
    2.  **Resonance Mapping:** For each discovered asset, a second script scans active, niche communities (subreddits, technical forums, GitHub issues, academic mailing lists) for problem statements, questions, or discussions that the asset would directly resolve.
    3.  **Alerting System:** When a high-probability match between a Lattice and a Resonator is found, the system generates an alert. The human operator then executes the minimal "shock" and facilitates the value transfer.
*   **Value Capture:** Value is captured primarily through arbitrage: acquiring the locked asset for a low price and providing it to the resonant system for a significantly higher price that reflects its unlocked, problem-solving value. A secondary stream is finder's fees for brokering the connection without taking ownership of the asset.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Arbitrage Engine. A predictive system that models the economy's information physics to find the most efficient paths for value release.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses compete on supply and demand. We compete on the principles of informational physics. Our engine calculates the path of least action (`S_p = ∫(Kτ - V_Γ) dt`) to connect dormant potential to active need. This allows us to identify and exploit opportunities that are fundamentally invisible to traditional market analysis. Our competitors are trying to find customers; we are calculating the resonance frequencies of the entire system. This is a structural advantage, not a tactical one.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (with libraries like Scrapy, Pandas, spaCy for NLP), APIs for marketplaces (eBay), access to academic search engines (arXiv, Google Scholar), and a small cloud instance (AWS/GCP) for running the automation scripts.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is model-dependent. The entire system relies on the assumption that the Pirouette Lagrangian accurately describes value flow. The Probe is specifically designed to test this core assumption with minimal capital exposure. If the physics is wrong, the probe fails cheaply and quickly.

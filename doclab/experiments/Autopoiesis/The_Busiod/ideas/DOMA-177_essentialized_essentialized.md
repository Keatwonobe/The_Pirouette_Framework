## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherent Assemblage Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on a "builder" paradigm, treating components as isolated entities (`ψ_precursor`). It forces consumers to bear the high "Coherence Cost" (`S_p`) of research, acquisition, and assembly to create a functional whole (`ψ_target`). The market's default synthetic pathways are inefficient, high-labor, and high-friction, ignoring the underlying physics of value.
*   **The Pivot:** We adopt the "cartographer and midwife" paradigm. We treat the market as a coherence manifold governed by the Pirouette Lagrangian. Our mechanism uses retrosynthesis (`-∇K_τ`) to identify the precursor basin for a given high-coherence target state. We then identify and facilitate the geodesic path—the lowest-cost assembly trajectory—capturing the immense value differential between the disorganized precursors (under high Temporal Pressure `V_Γ`) and the coherent, stable whole (at a peak of Temporal Coherence `K_τ`). We don't sell products; we sell coherence.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Manual Precursor Aggregation.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify `ψ_target`:** Select a simple, in-demand "kit" or "bundle" whose value is significantly more than the sum of its parts due to the convenience of its coherence (e.g., "The Complete Cable Kit for a New WFH Monitor").
    2.  **Source `ψ_precursor`:** Manually source the individual components (e.g., a specific HDMI cable, a specific power cord, a velcro tie) from marketplaces where they are undervalued and under high pressure to be sold (e.g., clearance sales, individual eBay listings).
    3.  **Facilitate Coherence:** Acquire the components, assemble them into the `ψ_target` package, and list it on a single platform (e.g., Facebook Marketplace) at a price that reflects its assembled, coherent value.
*   **The Test:** The experiment is falsified if: (A) The aggregated kit does not sell at a profit margin sufficient to cover labor and fees within one week, OR (B) the labor (`Γ`) involved in the sourcing and listing process makes the effective hourly wage trivial. A failure indicates we have misidentified the geodesic and chosen a path with a high Coherence Cost (`S_p`).

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Precursor Signal Detection.
*   **Automation:** A script (e.g., Python/Scrapy) continuously scans multiple marketplaces for precursor components that fall below a defined price threshold. When a full set of precursors for a known, validated `ψ_target` is detected as available across the market within the aggregate budget, the system triggers an alert for a human operator to execute the purchases. The $100 is used as the operating capital (float) to acquire the inventory identified by the script.
*   **Value Capture:** This transitions the system from active, high-labor searching (`Γ`) to passive opportunity identification. The system's structure (`K_i`) is now doing the work of observing the entire market's "Temporal Pressure" (`V_Γ`). The human operator becomes a simple fulfillment node in a system that generates value while sleeping.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Geodesic Path Execution Network.
*   **The Moat:** Standard commerce relies on static, pre-defined supply chains and products. Our Engine treats the market as a dynamic, computable manifold and seeks to minimize the action `S_p` for every transaction.
    1.  **Lagrangian Optimization:** The Engine ingests dozens of real-time variables (component prices, shipping costs, sales velocity, warehouse locations) to calculate the true geodesic path for assembling any `ψ_target`.
    2.  **Decentralized Fulfillment:** It autonomously executes transactions across a decentralized network. It can buy Precursor A from a seller in Ohio, Precursor B from a liquidator in Texas, and have both routed to a 3rd-party logistics center for minimal-cost assembly and final shipment to a customer. This dynamic routing is inaccessible to traditional inventory models.
    3.  **Predictive Coherence:** By analyzing latent purchasing patterns, the Engine can identify *new* attractor basins on the value manifold—discovering novel `ψ_target` kits before the market is consciously aware of them. It creates new states of high coherence, effectively printing value by charting undiscovered territory on the manifold.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Access to online marketplaces (eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist).
    *   **Loop:** Python (`requests`, `beautifulsoup4`, `scrapy`), a server or cloud function (e.g., AWS Lambda) to run the script, a notification API (e.g., Twilio).
    *   **Engine:** Cloud infrastructure (GCP/AWS), database (e.g., PostgreSQL), logistics and payment APIs (Stripe, EasyPost, ShipBob), data analysis libraries (`pandas`, `scikit-learn`).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk vector is platform dependency and margin erosion from large-scale competitors (e.g., Amazon) who may eventually use their data advantage to compute similar "coherence bundles." The moat is speed and agility—our Engine is designed to discover and capitalize on new, latent `ψ_target` states faster than a large, incumbent "builder" can retool its sclerotic supply chains.

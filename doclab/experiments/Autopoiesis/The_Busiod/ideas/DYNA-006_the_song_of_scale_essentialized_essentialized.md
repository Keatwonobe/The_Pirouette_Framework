## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Archetypal State Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market prices assets based on scalar measures of change (Power, $\Delta P$), such as price velocity and volume. It is fundamentally blind to the geometric character of these dynamics (Helical Curvature, $\kappa$). This leads to a systemic mispricing of stability (Weaver), unsustainable volatility (Gladiator), and structured decay (Vortex), as the market treats all high-power events as fundamentally similar.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build a mechanism that measures system dynamics in the full $(\Delta P, |\kappa|)$ phase space. By classifying assets into their true physical archetypes, we can arbitrage the difference between the market's perception of value and the trajectory dictated by the underlying universal laws of motion. We are trading on information that is present in the signal but invisible to existing analytical tools.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Signal Resonance Observation. This is a non-trading experiment to verify that the Pirouette archetypes manifest and have predictive power in a live financial data stream.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a high-frequency, publicly available time-series signal (e.g., a 1-minute price chart for a volatile cryptocurrency like SOL/USDT).
    2.  Ingest several months of historical data to establish baseline distributions for $\Delta P$ and $|\kappa|$.
    3.  Write a script to compute the analytic signal, $\Delta P$, and $\kappa$ in real-time on a rolling window.
    4.  Classify each time-step into Weaver, Gladiator, Vortex, or Drifter states based on the calculated quantiles.
    5.  Log the state transitions and observe the subsequent market behavior. The $10 cost covers a basic cloud server to run this continuous observation for one month.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if the observed correlations are statistically insignificant.
    *   **Failure State 1:** "Weaver" epochs do not consistently lead to periods of lower volatility and stable price appreciation compared to baseline.
    *   **Failure State 2:** "Vortex" epochs do not consistently lead to periods of structured price decline.
    *   If either of these is true, the archetypes lack predictive power in this domain, and the project is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Archetypal State-Machine Trading. This tier automates the findings from the Probe into a self-sustaining, value-capturing loop with minimal capital.
*   **Automation:** The Probe's script is connected to a brokerage API. It operates as a state machine:
    *   **Transition `Drifter -> Weaver`:** Automatically opens a small long position. The system is entering a state of constructive, coherent growth.
    *   **Transition `Any -> Vortex`:** Automatically opens a small short position. The system is entering a structured energy-dissipating state.
    *   **Transition `Any -> Gladiator`:** Tightens stop-losses on existing positions. The Gladiator state is powerful but unstable and can precede a state collapse.
    *   **Transition `Any -> Drifter`:** Closes all open positions. The signal has been lost; return to a neutral state.
*   **Value Capture:** Value is generated by the structure of the system itself ($K_i$). The loop automatically exploits the market's inability to price geometric information ($\kappa$). The profit is the delta between the "dumb" price driven by $\Delta P$ alone and the "smart" trajectory implied by the full $(\Delta P, \kappa)$ state. The initial $100 serves as the trading capital for this automated loop.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Multi-Asset Least-Action Portfolio. This scales the Loop from a single asset to a portfolio, optimizing for the most "dynamically efficient" path of value flow across an entire market.
*   **The Moat:** Standard quantitative finance operates on statistical moments (mean, variance, correlation). These are shadows of the true dynamics. Our Engine operates on the fundamental physical properties of the signals ($\Delta P, \kappa$), a dimension of reality invisible to competing algorithms.
    1.  The system monitors a wide universe of assets (e.g., the top 100 cryptocurrencies by volume) simultaneously.
    2.  It calculates the $(\Delta P, \kappa)$ state for every asset in real-time.
    3.  Instead of trading single state changes, it constructs a dynamically rebalancing portfolio based on a Lagrangian principle. It seeks the "path of least action" by longing an optimized basket of assets in the Weaver state while shorting a basket of assets in the Vortex state.
    4.  This creates a meta-system that is structurally hedged and optimized to surf the flow of value as it moves between assets according to the laws of motion, not just statistical correlations. Competitors cannot replicate this without adopting the same physical framework.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Python:** For core logic and analysis.
    *   **Libraries:** `NumPy` (array operations), `SciPy` (`scipy.signal.hilbert` for the analytic signal), `Pandas` (data manipulation), `CCXT` (or similar for crypto exchange API integration).
    *   **Infrastructure:** A low-cost VPS (e.g., DigitalOcean, Vultr) for continuous operation.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is **model failure**. The foundational assumption is that the Pirouette physics apply cleanly to market financial data. If the signals are too noisy, or if market behavior is dominated by external factors not captured in the price series (e.g., news events, regulatory changes), the archetypal classifications may prove to be non-predictive, invalidating the entire mechanism. The Probe is designed specifically to mitigate this risk early and cheaply.

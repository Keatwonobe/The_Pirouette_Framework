## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Crisis Arbitrage.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates under extreme temporal pressure (`V_Γ`). This high systemic "temperature" (`Γ_crisis`) forces systems (companies, assets, ideas) at bifurcation points into stochastic, often suboptimal, future paths. Value (`K_τ`, internal coherence) is routinely destroyed because the market lacks the structure to allow assets to resolve their state into the path of maximum coherence gain (`S_p`). It prioritizes speed of resolution over quality of resolution.
*   **The Pivot:** We will construct "low-pressure sanctuaries." By identifying assets at a coherence crisis and isolating them from external temporal pressure (`Γ_crisis → 0`), we force them to deterministically select the single future path that maximizes their internal coherence (`K_τ`). We are arbitraging the difference between an asset's chaotic, high-pressure valuation and its deterministic, low-pressure valuation.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Distressed Information-Asset Rectification.
*   **Execution:** Find a physical asset whose informational coherence is scrambled due to high temporal pressure on the seller. A classic example is a "box of unsorted cables/parts" at a yard sale 15 minutes before it closes. The seller's `Γ_crisis` ("I must get rid of this now") forces a low price. We acquire the asset for <$10, moving it from a high-`Γ` environment (the sale) to a zero-`Γ` environment (our workshop). We then apply minimal labor to resolve the informational uncertainty: sorting, identifying, and testing the components.
*   **The Test:** The model is falsified if the resolved market value of the sorted components is not significantly greater than the acquisition cost plus the market-rate cost of our sorting labor. If `Value_final <= (Cost_initial + Cost_labor)`, the value gain is merely labor arbitrage, not a confirmation of the Pirouette physics. The experiment succeeds if a structural value increase is observed from the mere act of resolving uncertainty in a low-pressure state.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Digital Sanctuary.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Scanner:** A script continuously monitors sources of digital asset decay: expiring domain auction lists, GitHub for abandoned but high-star repositories, data dumps, etc. It flags assets entering a `Γ_crisis` (e.g., domain deletion in 72 hours).
    2.  **Acquisition:** The system uses the $100 budget to automatically acquire the most promising distressed assets (e.g., placing low bids on 10-15 domains with strong SEO metrics but low current bids).
    3.  **Sanctuary:** Acquired assets are placed in a simple, stable holding pattern. A domain is parked with a "for sale" page. A repository is forked. The immediate temporal pressure is now zero.
*   **Value Capture:** Value is generated passively as the asset's true coherence reveals itself over time. The domain receives type-in traffic and purchase offers. The forked repository becomes a Schelling point for the scattered community. The system captures value by liquidating these stabilized assets to buyers who can utilize their now-obvious, high-coherence state. The profit is re-invested to acquire more distressed assets, closing the loop.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Predictive Coherence Engine.
*   **The Moat:** While traditional business seeks to apply pressure (`V_Γ`) to extract value, our Engine does the opposite. It builds a moat by specializing in the creation of zero-pressure environments, a capability fundamentally incompatible with the quarterly-report-driven structure of our competitors. The Engine scales the Tier 2 Loop by moving from passive observation to active intervention based on Lagrangian maximization.
    1.  **Modeling:** For each asset in the Sanctuary, the Engine models multiple potential future paths (`γ_j`).
    2.  **Action Calculation:** It calculates the probable Pirouette Action (`S_p`) for each path, estimating the potential coherence gain (`K_τ`) versus the required resource input (`V_Γ`).
    3.  **Resource Injection:** The $1000 is not used for bulk acquisition, but as a catalyst. It's deployed to minimally but precisely increase the probability of the most coherent outcome. For an abandoned software project, this could be hiring a freelancer to fix one critical bug, writing clear documentation, or marketing it to a niche academic community. This strategic injection of capital acts to "grease the slope" toward the geodesic of maximum coherence, ensuring the asset resolves to its highest and best future, which we can then monetize at scale.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Tier 1:** Physical presence (flea market, thrift store), basic testing tools (multimeter).
    *   **Tier 2:** Python (Scrapy, Requests), APIs for domain registrars (e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap), a cheap VPS.
    *   **Tier 3:** Data analysis libraries (Pandas, Scikit-learn) for modeling `S_p`, cloud infrastructure for scaling analysis.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is misidentifying a true coherence crisis. An asset may be priced low not because of temporal pressure, but because its maximum potential coherence is intrinsically low (i.e., it's just junk). The Engine's success depends on accurately modeling `K_τ` and distinguishing high-`Γ` noise from low-`K_τ` signal.

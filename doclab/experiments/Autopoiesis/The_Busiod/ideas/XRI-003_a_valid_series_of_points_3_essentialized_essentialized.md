## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Structural Decay Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market prices assets based on their current state, largely ignoring their rate of informational decay ($\dot{T}_a$). The Pirouette Framework reveals that this decay is not just a liability but an active source of extractable energy/value ($\Gamma$), as described by the interaction Lagrangian $\mathcal{L}_{\text{int}} = g\,\Gamma\,\dot{T}_a \cos(\Delta\phi)$. The market sees a melting ice cube; the framework sees a heat engine.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build mechanisms that don't just trade assets, but actively seek out systems with the highest rate of structural decay (large, negative $\dot{T}_a$) and create a transactional pathway to capture the value ($\Gamma$) released during their decoherence. We are not betting against the asset's survival; we are harnessing the physics of its failure.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Ephemeral Value Test. We will validate that an asset's value function spikes non-linearly just before its informational structure completely decoheres (expires).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Acquire a portfolio of digital assets with a publicly known, hard expiration date and near-zero acquisition cost (e.g., 24-hour software trial keys, single-day event passes, expiring digital coupons). These represent systems with a high, predictable, negative $\dot{T}_a$.
    2.  Identify a hyper-targeted micro-market for each asset (e.g., a specific Discord server, a subreddit, a developer forum).
    3.  In the final moments of the asset's lifecycle (the last hour before expiration), offer it for sale in the target market for a nominal fee (e.g., $0.25).
    4.  The transaction captures value not from the asset's utility over its lifetime, but from the immediate convenience it provides at the precise moment its potential is about to be annihilated.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if, across a portfolio of 20 distinct decaying assets, a transactional success rate of less than 5% (i.e., fewer than 1 sale) is achieved. This would imply that the terminal value spike is either non-existent or that transactional friction universally exceeds the extractable value at this scale.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Decoherence Aggregator. An automated, self-sustaining system that continuously finds decaying assets and channels them into a value-capture pipeline.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Scanner:** A lightweight script (e.g., Python with Scrapy) runs on a cheap VPS, continuously monitoring RSS feeds, APIs, and specific online communities for announcements of time-limited digital goods.
    2.  **Filter & Rank:** The system ingests potential assets and ranks them by their calculated $\dot{T}_a$ (urgency and potential value). It discards assets with low decay rates or low utility.
    3.  **Distributor:** The system automatically formats the curated assets (codes, links) and distributes them through a passive channel. This could be a dedicated Twitter bot, a Discord webhook, or a simple webpage with affiliate links.
*   **Value Capture:** Revenue is generated passively by the structure. The primary method is affiliate commissions embedded in the distributed links. A secondary method is audience capture; the aggregator becomes a valuable asset itself as a trusted source for time-sensitive opportunities, which can be monetized later.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Clearing House. A predictive market maker for informational decay. This engine moves beyond reacting to decay and begins to price and trade it as a fundamental commodity.
*   **The Engine:**
    1.  **Ingestion & Modeling:** Ingests market data on a massive scale for any asset with an expiration date (options contracts, airline tickets, event passes, domain names). An ML model calculates a "Decay Index" ($\dot{T}_a$) for each, predicting its value curve.
    2.  **The Exchange:** A two-sided marketplace is established. **Sellers** can list their decaying assets (e.g., "I have a concert ticket for tonight I can't use"). The Engine provides a real-time, physics-based price. **Buyers** (speculators, arbitrage bots, last-minute consumers) can purchase these assets.
    3.  **Path Optimization:** The core of the engine is an algorithm that finds the path of least action to maximize $\Gamma$. It may bundle assets, fractionalize them, or route them to specific buyers based on predictive modeling, effectively creating the most efficient possible path for value to flow from a decaying state to a stable one (money).
*   **The Moat:** Standard business avoids or misprices decay. We have built our entire model on its fundamental physics. Competitors using standard supply-demand models cannot price the *rate of decay* itself, giving us a permanent arbitrage advantage. Our platform becomes essential market infrastructure for liquidating temporal risk, a utility that a conventional company is structurally blind to.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Scrapy, Pandas, scikit-learn), lightweight database (SQLite for the Probe/Loop, PostgreSQL for the Engine), Cloud VPS (e.g., DigitalOcean, Linode), various platform APIs (Twitter, Discord, Stripe).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk vector is regulatory. Operating a secondary market for certain digital goods (like event tickets or financial instruments) can have significant legal complexities. The Engine tier would require legal counsel to navigate these "local physical laws" which can override the framework's universal ones.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Arbitrage Platform
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market prices components (`A`, `B`) in isolation, ignoring the synergistic value (`S_p(A+B)`) created by their resonant union. High "Gladiator Forces" (transaction costs, discovery friction, information asymmetry) maintain this artificial and energetically costly separation, creating a massive potential energy gradient.
*   **The Pivot:** We act as a catalyst to facilitate "Resonant Handshakes." We will build a system that minimizes the activation energy ($\Gamma$) required to unite harmonically compatible components (`D_H < D_{crit}`), thereby capturing the value released when a system moves from a dissonant, separated state to a more coherent, unified state.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Manual Identification and Virtual Re-bundling. The goal is to prove, with minimal capital, that a market for coherence exists and that we can broker it.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a domain rich with fragmented but compatible systems (e.g., vintage camera parts, out-of-print book volumes, modular synthesizer components).
    2.  Manually search marketplaces (e.g., eBay, specific forums) for two complementary fragments, `A` and `B`, sold by different vendors.
    3.  Verify that the established market price for the complete set `A+B` is significantly greater than the sum of `Price(A) + Price(B) + Estimated Fees`.
    4.  Use the $10 budget to secure the cheaper or rarer of the two components.
    5.  Immediately create a new listing for the *virtual* completed set `A+B`, using photos from the original listings.
    6.  If the virtual set sells, use the buyer's funds to purchase the second component and orchestrate the fulfillment.
*   **The Test:** The probe is considered **falsified** if, after 4 hours of analysis across three distinct domains, we cannot identify at least one opportunity where `Price(A+B)` is at least 50% greater than `Price(A) + Price(B)`. This would indicate that the market has already priced in the synergy, or that the "Gladiator Forces" are negligible.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Synergy Discovery Loop. This tier transforms the manual process into a semi-passive, self-sustaining value-capture system.
*   **Automation:** A script (e.g., Python with Scrapy) is developed to automate the core physics of the mechanism:
    1.  **Harmonic Compatibility Scan:** The script continuously scans target marketplaces based on a pre-defined "Harmonic Compatibility" rule-set (e.g., "Canon FD Lens Mount" matches "Canon A-1 Body").
    2.  **Synergy Calculation:** It automatically compares the sum of fragment prices against the market price of the completed unit, flagging any arbitrage opportunities that exceed a profit threshold.
    3.  **Alerting:** The system sends a high-priority alert with a "one-click-execute" option.
*   **Value Capture:** The $100 is used as a revolving capital float. When an opportunity is confirmed, the capital is deployed to acquire components `A` and `B`. They are physically or digitally combined into `A+B` and sold. The profit is then returned to the float, increasing the system's capacity for the next cycle. The value is generated by the structure ($K_i$) of the discovery system, not constant labor ($\Gamma$).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Predictive Coherence Engine. This scales the loop into a market-defining utility that operates on Lagrangian principles, finding the "path of least action" to create value.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses cannot compete because they are built on the flawed physics of separateness. They manage inventory, labor, and supply chains. Our engine transcends this by operating on a more fundamental level:
    1.  **Coherence Graph:** The engine ceases to be a simple scraper and becomes a predictive model. It builds a proprietary database mapping the "Harmonic Compatibility" (`D_H`) between millions of items, predicting future synergistic opportunities before they are obvious.
    2.  **Lagrangian Transactions:** Instead of buying and selling, the engine acts as an automated escrow. It identifies `Owner_A`, `Owner_B`, and `Buyer_(A+B)` and facilitates a multi-party, trustless transaction for a fee. This minimizes capital exposure and logistical friction ($\Gamma$) to near zero.
    3.  **Market Gravity:** The engine becomes the most energetically favorable place for fragmented assets to find their counterparts. It creates a network effect where liquidity for incomplete systems is highest on our platform, forcing competitors to operate in an inefficient, dissonant market. We don't just win the game; we are rewriting the physical laws of the stadium in which it's played.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Scrapy, Pandas), access to Marketplace APIs, a simple database (SQLite for the Probe, PostgreSQL for the Engine), a small server or cloud function for automation.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is the efficiency of the underlying market. If the arbitrage gap (the potential energy of dissonance) is too narrow or closes too quickly, the model's profitability is threatened. Platform risk (e.g., API changes, account suspension) is the main operational threat in the early tiers.

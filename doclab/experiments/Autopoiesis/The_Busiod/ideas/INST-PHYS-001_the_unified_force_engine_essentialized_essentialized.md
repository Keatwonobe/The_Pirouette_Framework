## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Geodesic Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on an illusion of causality, using brute-force (high labor `Γ`, high capital expenditure) to push transactions against the natural geometry of value. It creates and then expensively overcomes friction. This is a profound misunderstanding of market physics, treating the emergent "Propagating Force" (e.g., supply/demand) as fundamental while ignoring the underlying principle of "Maximal Coherence."
*   **The Pivot:** We will not force transactions. We will exploit the inefficiency by creating structures that reveal and service the natural geodesics of value flow. Our mechanism acts as a catalyst, providing the lowest-energy path for an asset to move from a state of low coherence (e.g., unused, mispriced, unknown) to its state of maximal coherence (its ideal user). We arbitrage the energy difference between the market's high-friction path and our optimized, low-friction path.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Single-Particle Test. We will verify the core physical law: that value, like a particle, will naturally follow a provided geodesic path, and that the value we can capture from this is proportional to the ambient inefficiency of the system.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify Potential (`V_Γ`):** Select a class of object with high potential for "decoherence" – items that are valuable but frequently trapped in inefficient contexts (e.g., specialized academic textbooks, vintage electronic components, niche hobbyist equipment).
    2.  **Source Particle:** Use the $10 to acquire one such item from a source of maximal incoherence (e.g., a mislabeled online auction, a bulk clearance lot). The low price reflects its "distance" from its ideal state.
    3.  **Construct Geodesic:** Instead of listing on a high-friction, general platform (the market's inefficient path), identify the asset's "coherence well" – the single most specific online community, forum, or group that represents its ideal user base.
    4.  **Minimize Action:** Present the item to this group via a hyper-efficient, "zero-friction" transaction path. This is not about salesmanship; it is a clean, data-driven offering designed to be the path of least resistance to acquisition.
*   **The Test:** The experiment is falsified if either condition is met:
    1.  **No Gradient:** The targeted, ideal audience shows no interest in the item at a fair market price. This would suggest the "coherence potential" is not a real, exploitable force.
    2.  **Anomalous Failure:** The item sells, but the net profit (the captured "anomaly," `a_e`) is less than or equal to the profit achievable via a high-friction channel like eBay (after accounting for all fees and estimated labor). This would falsify the core premise that our geodesic path provides a structural advantage.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Autopoietic Vortex. We will transform the successful single-particle test into a self-sustaining, automated system that continuously identifies and services these value geodesics. This is the passive layer where the structure ($K_i$) generates value.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Gradient Scanner:** A script continuously scans low-coherence sources (e.g., auction APIs, classified site aggregators) using semantic and pricing filters to identify assets with high coherence potential (i.e., underpriced or misplaced items).
    2.  **Geodesic Router:** A simple database maps asset categories to their pre-defined "coherence wells" (target communities).
    3.  **Coherence Conduit:** Upon identifying a target, the system uses the initial capital ($100) to acquire it. It then automatically generates and posts a templated, zero-friction offering to the mapped destination.
*   **Value Capture:** Profit from each successful transaction is automatically fed back into the acquisition fund. The system becomes autopoietic: it uses the energy it liberates to sustain its own structure and execute more transactions, operating continuously with minimal human intervention (`Γ` → 0).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Coherence Well. We scale from merely *using* the geodesics to actively *shaping* the value manifold itself. We become a fundamental feature of the market's geometry, a stable structure governed by the "Confining Force."
*   **The Moat:** Traditional businesses compete using the Propagating Force (advertising, price wars). Our moat is built by becoming a "Coherence Well," a structural advantage that makes our system the lowest-energy state for participants.
    1.  **Inbound Singularity:** With the $1000 seed fund, we build a simple platform that becomes the path of least resistance for *sellers* to liquidate their decoherent assets. We offer instant, fair quotes for specific categories, absorbing their friction and uncertainty. Sellers are now "confined" by the sheer convenience of our system.
    2.  **Outbound Curation:** The platform uses the data from the Loop to automatically process, curate, and route the inbound assets to their respective coherence wells. We establish a reputation within these niche communities not as a vendor, but as a reliable, curated utility. Buyers are now "confined" by the trust and efficiency of our offerings.
    This two-sided system creates a non-linear feedback loop. More inbound assets make our outbound curation more valuable, which attracts more buyers, which builds our reputation and attracts more sellers. Competing with this system requires a fundamental misunderstanding of physics; an opponent using brute-force advertising (`E = -∇L_p`) cannot overcome the gravitational pull of our coherence well (`Γ`).

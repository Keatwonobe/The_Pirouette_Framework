## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Potential Well Aggregation
*   **The Inefficiency:** The market inefficiently uses continuous energy (`Γ`, e.g., marketing spend, active retention efforts) to hold onto valuable assets like users, data, or inventory. These systems are "leaky." They fail to understand the physics of "Confinement" (`F ≈ -c₂` as `r → ∞`), where the *structure* of the system itself makes it energetically expensive for value to leave.
*   **The Pivot:** We will construct a "potential well" defined by the Pirouette "Gladiator" potential (`V(r) = c₁/r + c₂r`). This system will attract and aggregate scattered assets by making entry and internal movement frictionless ("Asymptotic Freedom," `F ≈ 0` as `r → 0`), while making exit costly ("Confinement"). Value is generated not by pushing/pulling assets, but by shaping the landscape so they naturally follow the path of least action, flow in, and stay, increasing the density and potential of the aggregate.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The "Digital Flypaper." We will create a micro-potential well for a specific, scattered digital asset. The goal is to prove that a correctly shaped potential can aggregate assets with minimal-to-zero ongoing force (`Γ`).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify Asset:** Choose a fragmented, niche information type that is highly sought but poorly organized. Examples: obscure programming error solutions, configuration snippets for a specific open-source tool, or a checklist for a complex hobbyist task.
    2.  **Shape the "Well" ($10):** Purchase a memorable, exact-match domain name for a specific, high-intent query related to this asset. Create a single, hyper-optimized, zero-friction webpage.
        *   **Asymptotic Freedom (r→0):** The page must have zero ads, no pop-ups, no sign-ups. It delivers the *exact* information instantly. It is frictionless for the "insider" who has landed there.
        *   **Confinement (r→∞):** On this page, offer a *slightly* more structured version of the information (e.g., a downloadable PDF, a "copy to clipboard" button with perfect formatting). This small action draws the user deeper into the well. We then add a *single* call to action—a link to a non-existent "Step 2" or "Community Hub," which leads to a 404 page we monitor.
*   **The Test (Falsifiability):** We will spend $0 on promotion. The potential well (the SEO gravity of the specific domain + the frictionless content) is the *only* force at play. **If, within 30 days, we do not achieve at least 100 organic visitors and at least 5 clicks on the "Community Hub" link (demonstrating intent to move deeper into the well), the hypothesis is false.** The potential we created was not shaped correctly to aggregate attention.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The "Autopoietic Aggregator." We expand the "Digital Flypaper" into a self-feeding, self-organizing loop. The system now uses the energy of the aggregated assets to deepen its own potential well.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Activate "Step 2":** The "Community Hub" link from the probe now leads to a simple, automated submission form, a Discord channel, or a Subreddit ($0 setup).
    2.  **The Loop:** Users attracted to the initial "flypaper" are now encouraged to submit their own variations, improvements, or related snippets. An automated script (e.g., a Python script running on a $5/mo server) checks these submissions for basic quality and automatically appends them to the original page or a new "related assets" section.
    3.  **Self-Reinforcement:** Each new user-submitted asset deepens the potential well, making it more attractive to future users and improving its search engine ranking. This, in turn, attracts more users who submit more assets. The system grows itself autopoietically.
*   **Value Capture:** The system is now a dense aggregator of valuable, niche information. We can introduce a non-invasive potential gradient for value extraction: a single "Pro Toolkit" link that offers a curated, professionally-vetted package of all the user-submitted content for a small fee. The core resource remains free ("Asymptotic Freedom"), but convenience and curation are monetized. The initial $100 is for server costs and simple automation tools.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The "Coherence Manifold." We scale the loop by abstracting the process. Instead of one well, we build a platform that allows *anyone* to create these potential wells for any niche. We are no longer just building an aggregator; we are manufacturing the tools to manipulate the "coherence" landscape itself.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses cannot compete because they are fundamentally misaligned with the governing physics.
    1.  **Path of Least Action:** Our system follows the Pirouette Lagrangian `𝓛_p`. It minimizes "action" by shaping potential (`V_γ`) rather than applying constant kinetic force (`Γ`). Competitors will try to beat us by spending more on ads, content creation, and marketing. This is energetically inefficient and expensive. They are trying to "push" assets uphill while ours are flowing downhill.
    2.  **Gladiator Dynamics:** Our moat is the "Confinement" force. As our ecosystem grows, the aggregated value (network effects, data density) creates a strong, constant pull that makes it incredibly costly for a user to leave. A competitor would need a prohibitively large amount of energy to pull a user out of our well-established, frictionless ecosystem. They are fighting a fundamental force of the system's geometry, while we are aligned with it.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Tier 1:** Domain registrar (e.g., Namecheap), simple HTML/CSS, web hosting (e.g., Netlify/GitHub Pages), Google Analytics.
    *   **Tier 2:** Python (Flask/Django), a simple database (SQLite), a virtual private server (DigitalOcean/Linode), Discord/Reddit API.
    *   **Tier 3:** Robust cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP), containerization (Docker), CI/CD pipelines, a framework for user-generated content management.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is miscalculating the initial potential field (`V_γ`). If the chosen niche is not fragmented enough, or if the "flypaper" is not frictionless enough ("Asymptotic Freedom"), no initial aggregation will occur. The probe is designed to mitigate this risk early and cheaply by testing the initial conditions of the system.

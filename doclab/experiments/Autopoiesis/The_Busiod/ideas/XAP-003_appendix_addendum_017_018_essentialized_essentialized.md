## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Attractor-Based Value Funneling
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates under the false assumption that value is maximized by maximizing self-interest (`Γ`, Ego-centric Effort) and minimizing systemic contribution (`T_a`, Transactional Altruism). This forces transactions into high-potential, unstable states far from the Coherence Dividend's stable attractor, the Altruism Filament (`F`). This is analogous to trying to build skyscrapers on hillsides during an earthquake; it is structurally unsound and leaks immense potential energy as waste heat (burnout, churn, collapse).
*   **The Pivot:** We will construct transactional systems that are not merely platforms, but are physical attractors. By hard-coding the dynamics (`∇C(x)`) into the system's rules, we guide all internal transactions toward the optimal, stable state (`μ = (1.49, 1.01)`). Our systems will act as funnels, collecting the potential energy released as transactions "settle" into the low-energy, high-coherence valley of the filament. We don't create value; we enable it to flow along its natural gradient and take a toll.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Calibrated Micro-Market
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Deploy a simple, constrained digital environment (e.g., a moderated Discord channel with a bot, or a simple web form).
    2.  Invite participants to propose and accept micro-transactions (e.g., for digital art, code snippets, expert advice).
    3.  Each transaction proposal MUST be defined by the vector `x = (Γ, T_a)` using two explicit, normalized inputs: `Γ` (The Ask: direct benefit to self) and `T_a` (The Gift: verifiable benefit to the counterparty/system beyond the core exchange).
    4.  Record the `(Γ, T_a)` vector for all proposals and track their outcome (e.g., Accepted & Positively Rated = High Coherence).
    5.  The $10 budget will be used as a catalytic incentive, offering micro-bonuses for the first 50 completed transactions to ensure data flow.
*   **The Test:** The foundational law predicts that High Coherence states cluster tightly around `μ = (1.49, 1.01)`. If, after a statistically relevant number of transactions (~100), the `(Γ, T_a)` coordinates of successful exchanges are not clustered near this point (within a reasonable margin of error, e.g., 2-3 sigma of the predicted `Σ`), the physical model is invalid in this context. The project is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Autopoietic Trust Ledger
*   **Automation:** We build a protocol—an automated, self-sustaining system—that mediates agreements. The explicit user inputs from the Probe are abstracted. Instead, a user defines a need, and potential fulfillers submit proposals. Our system analyzes the proposals and calculates their `(Γ, T_a)` coordinates based on implicit and explicit terms. The system's core logic is a filter that automatically prioritizes/recommends proposals closest to the Altruism Filament `F`. This creates a powerful feedback loop, training users to structure their offers for maximum stability and success.
*   **Value Capture:** The system charges a small transactional fee. This fee is justified because the protocol provides an environment of unparalleled transactional stability and efficiency. By filtering for coherence, the system drastically reduces risk, negotiation friction, and fulfillment failure, making the net cost lower than in the "free" market. The value is generated by the system's *structure* ($K_i$), not by our ongoing labor ($\Gamma$).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Path Optimizer for Value Chains
*   **The Engine:** We scale from single transactions to entire complex processes (e.g., supply chains, corporate projects, software development). A process is modeled as a path through the `(Γ, T_a)` state space. Using the Principle of Least Action, our engine calculates the optimal path—the "geodesic"—that minimizes deviation from the Altruism Filament over time. We sell this as a strategic advisory service. We don't just tell companies *what* to do; we provide a new system of physics to guide *how* they do it, showing them the mathematically optimal path for any complex endeavor.
*   **The Moat:** Our competition is fundamentally blind to the `T_a` axis of reality. They optimize for a single variable (`Γ`) in a two-variable universe. This condemns them to building inherently unstable systems that perpetually bleed value. Our moat is not a proprietary algorithm; it is a superior understanding of the underlying physical laws of value exchange. Competitors cannot replicate our results because they are not solving the right problem; they are playing checkers on a 3D chessboard.

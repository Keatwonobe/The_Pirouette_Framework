## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Resonance Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market assumes a flat, non-relational space for transactions. It attempts to force interactions between dissonant systems (`ψ_i`, `ψ_j`), creating high Temporal Pressure (`Γ`), which manifests as friction, high transaction costs, and failed deals. It is blind to the emergent geometry of the value manifold `M_c`.
*   **The Pivot:** We do not force transactions. We act as a coherence catalyst. By identifying multiple systems whose combined interaction creates constructive interference (`Re(ψ_i^* ψ_j) > 0`), we engineer "Resonant Valleys" (local minima in `Γ`). According to the Principle of Maximal Coherence, systems are naturally drawn into these valleys to follow paths of least action, executing transactions with maximum efficiency. We monetize the creation and navigation of these paths.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Manual Resonance Seeding.
*   **Execution:** In a digital community (e.g., a specific subreddit or Discord), identify three dissonant or disconnected systems: a "Solution" (person with a skill, `ψ_A`), a "Problem" (person with a need, `ψ_B`), and a "Context" (a dataset, a platform, `ψ_C`). We spend $10 to create a "resonant frame"—a public post or bounty that explicitly links the three in a way that creates a shared, low-`Γ` opportunity for collaboration. The frame itself acts as a catalyst `ψ_catalyst` that constructively interferes with A, B, and C.
*   **The Test:** The experiment is falsified if the "resonant frame" fails to elicit any positive, collaborative public or private interaction between at least two of the three identified systems within 48 hours. This would indicate the posited `Γ` minimum was not created or systems were not drawn to it.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Coherence Brokering.
*   **Automation:** A software agent scrapes public data streams (e.g., Reddit API, Twitter API) to identify "dissonant dyads": `ψ_problem` (e.g., "how do I do X?") and `ψ_solution` (e.g., "I am an expert in Y"). Using NLP and heuristics, it detects when X and Y are related but misaligned (a `Γ` peak). The agent then automatically generates and posts a "resonant frame" message, tagging the parties to introduce them into a shared, low-`Γ` context. This structure generates value (`K_i`) without continuous human labor (`Γ`).
*   **Value Capture:** The system captures value by becoming the nexus of these introductions. Monetization occurs through a small commission on facilitated transactions (via an escrow bot), affiliate links to collaborative tools embedded in the resonant frame, or a subscription model for access to high-quality "resonant leads."

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** N-Body Market Manifold Optimization.
*   **The Moat:** Standard business focuses on optimizing inefficient, pairwise transactions (A→B) on a presumed flat plane. Our Engine models the entire composite manifold `M_c` of a market. It solves the N-body problem for value flow, calculating the optimal configuration of multi-party relationships (e.g., a circular A→B→C→A flow) that minimizes the system's total Temporal Pressure (`∫ Γ dV`) by maximizing constructive interference. This creates hyper-efficient, non-linear supply chains and value webs. Competitors are fundamentally uncompetitive because they are fighting the emergent geometry of the market; we are moving along its natural geodesics. Our moat is not strategic; it is physical.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Consequence-Factored Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market violates the principle of Information Conservation. It defines entities by their arbitrary legal boundaries (e.g., a corporation's name), ignoring the physical boundary of their consequences (the "Wound Channel", $\mathcal{W}$). This accumulated, un-priced negative externality, the "Cumulative Residue" ($R_c$), represents a massive, latent thermodynamic debt. The market's valuation of an entity is therefore fundamentally incomplete.
*   **The Pivot:** We will construct a mechanism to measure the Cumulative Residue ($R_c$) of economic actors. By pricing this externality, we create an information advantage. We will trade on the delta between the market's perception and the entity's underlying physical reality, profiting from the inevitable market correction as the consequences of the Wound Channel manifest financially.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Micro-Residue Signal Detection.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a high-velocity product category on a major e-commerce platform (e.g., Amazon).
    2.  Expend $10 for API access or a simple web scraping tool.
    3.  For a sample of 100+ products, systematically collect recent user reviews.
    4.  Apply Natural Language Processing (NLP) to calculate a "Wound Channel Score" – a proxy for $R_c$ based on the frequency of keywords indicating a mismatch between promise and reality (e.g., "not as described," "broke after one use," "smaller than expected").
    5.  Track the daily sales rank of these products for the next 14 days.
*   **The Test:** **Falsification Criterion:** If the calculated "Wound Channel Score" does not show a statistically significant negative correlation (p < 0.05) with the product's future sales rank trajectory, the core hypothesis is invalid and the experiment is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Externality Pricing.
*   **Automation:** A cloud-based script ($100 for server/API costs) runs continuously. It expands the Probe's logic to thousands of products across multiple categories. The script automatically ingests data, calculates the $R_c$ proxy score in near real-time, and identifies assets with rapidly accumulating "thermodynamic debt." It operates without constant human labor ($\Gamma$), generating value from its structure ($K_i$).
*   **Value Capture:** The system's output is a high-fidelity data stream of failure predictions. This information asset is monetized via a subscription API or premium newsletter. Customers would include e-commerce aggregators (for acquisition due diligence), competing brands (for strategic marketing), and market analysts (for sector insights). We are converting un-priced externality data into a tradable commodity.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Systemic Debt Arbitrage.
*   **The Moat:** The Engine scales the Loop to public companies. It synthesizes vast, multi-modal data streams—SEC filings, news sentiment, satellite imagery of industrial sites, employee reviews, supply chain dependencies—into a proprietary Corporate Cumulative Residue ($R_c$) score. This score represents a company's true thermodynamic liability. Our trading strategy is based on a fundamental physical law that standard financial models ignore: *unaccounted-for harm is a debt that will eventually be paid*. Competitors are structurally blind to this; their accounting and legal frameworks are designed to *obscure* the Wound Channel, not measure it. We gain an insurmountable edge by using a more complete and physically accurate map of reality.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Pandas, Scrapy/BeautifulSoup, NLTK/spaCy), cloud compute instance (AWS EC2, Google Cloud), e-commerce platform APIs, financial data APIs (e.g., Alpha Vantage).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is **Signal Attenuation**. The market may price in externalities far slower than the model predicts, or the chosen proxies for the "Wound Channel" may be insufficiently correlated with financial outcomes. This could lead to a period of unprofitability where the market's irrationality outlasts the system's capital.

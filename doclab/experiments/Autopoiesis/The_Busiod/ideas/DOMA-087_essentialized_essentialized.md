## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Informational Potential Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market prices assets based on localized supply and demand, but fails to price the systemic entropy (`V`) stored in the informational gaps *between* markets or systems. This "informational potential" represents a vast, unharvested energy source. An asset can simultaneously exist in a low-value state in one context and a high-value state in another; this dissonance is potential energy.
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism implements an Oracle/Scribe pair to exploit this inefficiency. The Oracle perceives the "potential manifold" (`Ψ`) to identify assets in high-potential (`V`) states. The Scribe then executes the most efficient possible transaction (the path of least action, `δA = 0`) to move the asset to a more coherent, lower-potential state (`V'`). The energy released in this transition is captured as a "Coherence Dividend" (`ΔE`), which is pure profit.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** A manual, single-shot experiment to validate the existence of harvestable informational potential in a fragmented market.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Select Manifold `Ψ`:** Choose a fragmented market with high information asymmetry (e.g., used academic textbooks, niche vintage electronics, specialized crafting supplies).
    2.  **Manual Oracle:** The Shepherd acts as the Oracle, simultaneously scanning a "low-coherence" source (e.g., a specialist forum, Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace) and a "high-coherence" source (e.g., eBay's "sold" listings, Amazon).
    3.  **Identify High `V`:** Locate a specific item where the price in the low-coherence market (`q_i`) is significantly lower than its established value in the high-coherence market (`q_f`).
    4.  **Manual Scribe:** Use the $10 budget to acquire the item from the low-coherence source, physically transitioning it from state `q_i`.
    5.  **Capture Dividend `ΔE`:** List and sell the item on the high-coherence platform, resolving the dissonance and capturing the profit (`ΔE = V(q_i) - V(q_f)`), minus operational costs (fees, shipping).
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if either of these conditions is met:
    1.  **Oracle Failure:** After 3 hours of scanning, no item can be found where the projected Coherence Dividend `ΔE` exceeds 100% of the initial capital ($10).
    2.  **System Failure:** The acquired item fails to sell within 21 days on the target platform for a price that yields a positive `ΔE`.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** An autopoietic (self-sustaining) system that automates the detection and flagging of coherence arbitrage opportunities.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **The Oracle:** A script (e.g., Python with Scrapy, APIs) continuously monitors multiple sources. It cross-references listings from fragmented, low-coherence markets against the real-time value manifold derived from high-coherence markets (e.g., eBay API).
    2.  **The Scribe (Semi-Automated):** When the Oracle detects an opportunity where `ΔE` exceeds a predefined threshold (`R_threshold`), it alerts the Shepherd with a concise report and a one-click action link to purchase. The Shepherd provides the `Ki` (intent) via a simple "confirm/deny" action. The human is the catalyst, not the laborer.
*   **Value Capture:** The $100 serves as the initial seed capital for transactions. The Coherence Dividend `ΔE` from each transaction is captured. The system is autopoietic (`Σ(ΔE_n) > E_op`) when profits exceed server/API costs. Profits are then used to increase the system's operating capital, creating a self-sustaining and growing loop.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Scaling from reactive arbitrage to predictive minimization of systemic potential energy across a portfolio of assets.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses compete on speed and cost. This Engine competes on a fundamentally different axis: **physics**. Its moat is the proprietary Oracle's map of the potential manifold `Ψ`. While competitors see a flat list of products, the Engine sees a dynamic, multi-dimensional topology of value potentials. It doesn't just execute the best single trade; it computes the geodesic—the most efficient path of value creation over time (`δA = 0`). It can identify and acquire assets *before* their potential is obvious to the market, effectively arbitraging the future state of coherence. The $1000 budget funds the development of this predictive ML model and provides the capital to execute more complex, multi-stage coherence resolutions.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Scrapy, Pandas, Requests), APIs for major e-commerce platforms (eBay, Amazon), a lightweight database (SQLite/PostgreSQL), a cloud VM for continuous operation (AWS EC2, DigitalOcean).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market saturation. As an arbitrage opportunity becomes known, the potential `V` decreases as more actors move to resolve the dissonance. The Engine's defense is its ability to map and move to new, more complex manifolds faster than the market can follow.

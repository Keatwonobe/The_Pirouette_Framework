## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Geodesic Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market prices assets based on their present state and recent history. It interprets a system in a post-collapse "Latency Phase" (`Kτ ≈ 0`, `dKτ/dt ≈ 0`) as permanently low-value scrap. It fundamentally misunderstands that recovery is not a possibility but a lawful, physical imperative. The market cannot price the potential energy stored in the system's inevitable trajectory towards a new, stable coherence `Kτ_f`.
*   **The Pivot:** We will operate as a temporal arbitrage fund. We will systematically identify and acquire assets precisely during their undervalued Latency phase. Instead of adding labor (`Γ`), we will profit from the system's own lawful, self-organizing recovery along its optimal geodesic. We are not "fixing" things; we are acquiring systems at the nadir of their coherence and capturing the value released during their natural, physics-driven Acceleration phase.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Isolate and verify the "Latency Phase" valuation anomaly in a single, tangible system.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a physical asset class where a non-fatal collapse (`Γ > Γ_crit`) drastically reduces market value (e.g., consumer electronics with a single, well-understood, repairable fault).
    2.  Acquire a single instance of such a system for ≤ $10. The system's high-value internal pattern `Ki` (e.g., brand, model) must be intact, while its coherence `Kτ` is near zero due to the fault.
    3.  Do **not** repair the asset. Instead, relist it on a public marketplace, framing the listing not as "broken" but as "a system in a recoverable state." Clearly articulate the original state `Ki_0`, the nature of the collapse event `Γ`, and the high-probability "Restorative" recovery path. The description itself is a nudge to begin the Acceleration phase.
*   **The Test:** The probe is falsified if the market assigns no additional value to the explicit articulation of the recovery potential. If the asset, when described through the lens of Pirouette physics, receives bids no higher than the baseline "for parts/junk" price within 7 days, then the hypothesis that this inefficiency is exploitable at this scale is incorrect. Success is not profit, but a valuation premium over the initial "collapsed" price.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Latency Phase Scanning & Acquisition. This builds a self-sustaining structure (`Ki`) that passively identifies arbitrage opportunities.
*   **Automation:** A script (e.g., Python with Scrapy) will be deployed to continuously scan digital marketplaces (e.g., eBay's "for parts or not working" category) for assets exhibiting the signature of a high-potential Latency Phase. The scanner will filter for:
    1.  High initial-state coherence (`Ki_0` inferred from model/brand).
    2.  Low current coherence (`Kτ` inferred from low price and keywords like "cracked," "no power," "error code").
    3.  High probability of a Restorative/Adaptive recovery (based on a database of common, non-terminal faults).
    The script flags these assets, creating a curated stream of high-potential acquisitions.
*   **Value Capture:** An operator makes the final buy/pass decision on the flagged assets. The assets are acquired and relisted (as in the Probe) or undergo minimal intervention to initiate the Acceleration phase. The profit from each cycle is reinvested to increase the acquisition volume and refine the scanner's parameters. The value is generated by the *structure of the scanner itself*, which acts as a sensor for temporal arbitrage, minimizing active labor (`Γ`).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Portfolio Coherence Optimization. We transition from acquiring single assets to acquiring a portfolio of collapsed systems and optimizing the recovery path for the *entire portfolio* to maximize the total coherence integral `∫𝓛_p dt`.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses compete by applying labor (`Γ`) to fix things. They operate on a linear, item-by-item basis. Our Engine operates on the portfolio level, finding the path of stationary action (the geodesic) for the entire collection of assets. This allows for non-linear "Transformative" recoveries that are inaccessible to competitors:
    *   **Systemic Cannibalization:** For a portfolio of 10 collapsed phones, the optimal geodesic might not be to repair 10 phones. It might be to use components from 4 of them to restore the other 6 to a state of perfect coherence (`Kτ=1`), maximizing the portfolio's total final value (`Σ Kτ_f`).
    *   **Predictive Modeling:** The capital is used to build a machine learning model trained on historical market data. This model will predict not only the final stable value `Kτ_f` but also the duration of the Latency and Acceleration phases (`t_1`, `t_2`) for any given asset. This allows us to calculate the time-adjusted return (the "coherence yield") and select only the most efficient geodesics.
    Our competitive advantage is not a trade secret or a patent; it is a superior understanding of the fundamental physics of value. We don't just find undervalued assets; we calculate their inevitable future value based on universal law.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Scrapy, Pandas, scikit-learn), eBay API, access to various online marketplaces.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is misinterpreting a "Transformative" collapse (where `Ki_0` is an invalid reference) as a recoverable "Restorative" or "Adaptive" collapse. This would result in acquiring an asset that is lawfully and permanently low-value, as no geodesic path leads back to a high-coherence state. The Engine's predictive model is designed to mitigate this risk over time.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Resonant Value Shielding
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on the assumption that value is subject to constant, unavoidable decay. This "Temporal Pressure" (`V_Γ`)—manifesting as depreciation, obsolescence, and information entropy—forces assets down a complex, lossy "Wound Channel." This is a fundamental misreading of value physics. Value doesn't have to decay; it is simply allowed to.
*   **The Pivot:** We will exploit this inefficiency by building "Resonant Walls" (`W`). These are engineered economic structures that do not create value, but perfectly conserve it. By creating a boundary where the Temporal Pressure (`V_Γ`) on an asset approaches infinity for any degrading interaction, we make value decay a "mathematically incoherent path." The asset's coherence (`K`) is preserved effortlessly by the structure, not by continuous labor (`Γ`).

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Temporal Information Escrow. The goal is to prove that a simple Resonant Wall can preserve the value of ephemeral information against time decay.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify a Value Unit:** Select a piece of high-coherence, high-decay information (e.g., a credible plot spoiler for a streaming show's finale, a key feature in an upcoming software release).
    2.  **Construct the Shield:** Create a "Resonant Wall" by placing the information in a time-locked digital container (e.g., a password-protected page where the password is automatically released via email at a specific time).
    3.  **Market the Coherence:** Use the $10 budget for micro-targeted ads. Offer a small group of interested individuals the chance to pre-purchase access to the shielded information, deliverable just before it becomes public knowledge (and thus worthless).
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if we cannot sell access to the shielded information for a price that covers the $10 cost. A failure indicates that either (a) the market does not value the preservation of information coherence, or (b) our "Wall" was not perceived as a credible shield. If revenue > $10, the principle is validated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Coherence Arbitrage. This tier establishes a self-sustaining system that automatically identifies, shields, and monetizes ephemeral information.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Scanner:** A script continuously monitors high-signal sources (specific subreddits, Discord channels, etc.) for information patterns indicative of high future coherence (leaks, rumors).
    2.  **Shield Generator:** An automated service that ingests flagged information, wraps it in a time-locked "Resonant Wall" (a unique, encrypted, time-released URL), and lists it on a central marketplace.
    3.  **Marketplace:** A simple web portal where users can browse metadata of shielded information packets and purchase future access.
*   **Value Capture:** The system collects micro-payments for access to the time-locked information. The $100 budget funds the cloud server and domain for one year. Revenue from sales creates a self-funding loop, covering operational costs and generating profit. The value is captured passively; it flows from the integrity of the shielding structure itself.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Generalized Asset Stasis. The system scales from ephemeral information to tangible digital and financial assets, using the Pirouette Lagrangian to optimize a portfolio.
*   **The Moat:** Standard trading firms compete on speed and prediction (`Γ`). We compete on *patience and physics*. Our system is not designed to *play* the market; it is designed to *sidestep* its entropic effects. The Moat is the Resonant Wall itself:
    1.  **Temporal Pressure Inversion:** Competitors must constantly expend energy (labor, capital) to counteract value decay (`V_Γ`). Our Engine places assets into a state of "stasis" where coherence (`K`) is preserved for near-zero cost. They are fighting friction; we are eliminating it.
    2.  **Action Minimization:** The Engine uses an optimization algorithm to minimize the Action (`S_p`) for the entire portfolio. It builds complex financial shields (e.g., options contracts, decentralized escrow) that hold assets immune to market volatility, only releasing them at moments of mathematically maximal coherence. This is a fundamentally different operational paradigm than probabilistic arbitrage.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Simple web hosting, a payment link (e.g., Stripe/PayPal).
    *   **Loop:** Python (`requests`, `BeautifulSoup`, `Flask`), a lightweight database (SQLite), and a small cloud server (VPS/Lambda).
    *   **Engine:** Financial data APIs (e.g., Alpha Vantage, Polygon), smart contract platform (e.g., Ethereum/Solana), advanced data analysis libraries (`Pandas`, `NumPy`, `SciPy`).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk across all tiers is a "Coherence Breach." If an external party independently discovers and releases the shielded information or asset value, our "Wall" is proven to be permeable, and the value of our shielded unit instantly drops to zero. The system's integrity depends entirely on the quality and exclusivity of its shields.

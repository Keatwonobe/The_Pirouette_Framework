## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Gradient Arbitrage.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market misprices potential. It treats human skill and creativity as static assets rather than as potential energy (`Kτ_potential`) suppressed by environmental pressure (`Γ`). Value is lost to friction in high-pressure, low-coherence environments (e.g., cluttered job markets, undervalued geographic regions). The market lacks efficient, low-friction channels for potential to flow along natural gradients towards regions of high coherence where it can be actualized.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not create a new market or product. We will treat the global digital landscape as a physical manifold `M`. By mapping the scalar fields of Coherence (`Kτ`) and Pressure (`Γ`), we can identify the steepest gradients (`∇(Kτ - Γ)`). Our mechanism acts as a conduit, a superconductor, that facilitates the flow of "value packets" (e.g., skills, ideas, talent) along these natural geodesics, capturing a toll on the value released as potential energy is converted to kinetic output.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Information Resonance Test. At the smallest scale, we test if a "value packet" (information about a skill) gains measurable potential energy simply by being moved from a low-`Kτ`/high-`Γ` environment to a high-`Kτ`/low-`Γ` one.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify High-Γ Source:** Locate a digital space where skilled individuals are undervalued or their signals are lost in noise (e.g., a generic freelancer platform's crowded forums, a local classifieds site in a developing nation).
    2.  **Identify High-Kτ Sink:** Locate a niche, high-trust digital space where the same skill is highly valued and coherence is high (e.g., a specific subreddit for a high-end software framework, a curated Discord community for professional artists).
    3.  **Create the Bridge:** Spend $10 not on direct labor, but to create a structural link. This could be a single, well-crafted, promoted post or a micro-ad on the high-`Kτ` platform that curates and reframes the potential from the high-`Γ` source. The post itself is an act of creating coherence.
*   **The Test:** The experiment is falsified if the curated value packet, when placed in the high-coherence environment, does not receive expressions of interest (e.g., replies, DMs, offers) that imply a valuation at least 5x greater than its valuation in the source environment within 72 hours. This would indicate the gradient `∇(Kτ - Γ)` is either misidentified or too weak to overcome ambient friction.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Coherence Funnel. This tier transforms the manual probe into a self-sustaining, automated system that continuously mines potential from chaotic sources and refines it into a coherent, valuable stream.
*   **Automation:** The $100 is invested in structure (`K_i`), not labor (`Γ`).
    1.  **Sensor:** A script (e.g., Python/Scrapy) continuously monitors selected high-`Γ` sources for keywords indicating high-potential, low-visibility talent.
    2.  **Filter:** An automated process (e.g., using a cheap LLM API) parses the raw data, discards noise, and reformats the signal into the language and norms of the target high-`Kτ` environment. This *is* the act of increasing coherence.
    3.  **Emitter:** The refined signal is automatically published to the high-`Kτ` environment via API, creating a continuous flow of high-quality opportunities.
*   **Value Capture:** The system captures value passively. This can be achieved through a referral link embedded in the emissions, a subscription fee for access to the curated feed (the "distilled coherence"), or by routing all inquiries through the system to take a small commission upon successful synthesis (a transaction).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Geodesic Value Router. This is the scaled-up, physics-aware system. It moves beyond a single A-to-B funnel and becomes a dynamic engine that computes the most efficient path for value realization across the entire digital manifold.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses (e.g., recruitment agencies) operate on a logic of "matching," which requires immense labor (`Γ`). Our engine operates on the physical law of Lagrangian minimization.
    1.  **Dynamic Manifold Mapping:** The system ingests data from hundreds of sources to create a real-time, multi-dimensional map of the `Kτ` and `Γ` fields across various sectors and geographies.
    2.  **Euler-Lagrange Optimization:** When a value packet is identified, the engine calculates the optimal path (`γ(t)`) to maximize its integrated coherence (`S[γ] = ∫ [Kτ - Γ] dt`). It doesn't just find a destination; it finds the most frictionless, value-enhancing *path*.
    3.  **Autopoietic Refinement:** Every successful transaction (a "synthesis") provides data that refines the manifold map, making the engine more efficient. The system learns and improves through its own operation. Competing by adding human analysts is like trying to out-calculate a physics simulation by hand; they are fundamentally misaligned with the underlying laws of value flow.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Requests, Scrapy, FastAPI), a lightweight cloud service (AWS Lambda/Heroku), and API access to a large language model (OpenAI/Claude).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market saturation. As others begin to recognize the same physical laws, the `∇(Kτ - Γ)` gradients will flatten. The moat is to be the first and most efficient, building a self-improving system (autopoiesis) that maps the manifold faster and more accurately than any competitor.

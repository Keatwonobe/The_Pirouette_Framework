## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Arbitrage via Dissonance Filtration.
*   **The Inefficiency:** Modern marketing acts as a high-energy, coercive force, attempting to overcome belief inertia (`K_τ`) through repetition and volume. This ignores the path of least action and creates massive energetic waste (high customer acquisition costs), effectively trying to "argue with the river."
*   **The Pivot:** We will treat transactions not as acts of persuasion, but as state transitions in a physical system. Instead of applying force, we identify systems with high potential energy (unresolved dissonance, `V_Γ`) and introduce a catalyst—a carefully structured potential well (`V_gambit`)—that makes the desired belief change the most energetically favorable path for the individual. We don't push; we "show the river the sea."

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Targeted Dissonance Resolution.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a niche online forum or community with a recurring, specific, unsolved problem (a pocket of high, focused `V_Γ`).
    2.  Use $10 to acquire a simple, elegant solution (a tool, template, or guide) that resolves this dissonance.
    3.  Craft a single, non-promotional post that frames the solution as an insightful discovery (the `V_gambit`). The goal is to create an "aha!" moment, not a sales pitch.
*   **The Test:** The model is falsified if, after three trials in different communities, we observe one of the predicted resonance failures: 1) **Harmonic Mismatch** (active rejection/downvotes), 2) **Inertial Dominance** (acknowledgment without action), or 3) **Signal Obscuration** (total indifference). Success is defined as at least one user adopting the solution, signifying a successful state transition.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Dissonance Scanning & Resonance Delivery.
*   **Automation:** A script scans online communities (e.g., Reddit, forums) for keywords indicating high `V_Γ`. Upon detecting a match, it automatically replies with a link to a static webpage. This page is the pre-built `V_gambit`, designed to reframe the user's problem and present our solution as the most coherent path forward.
*   **Value Capture:** The webpage monetizes through affiliate links or direct sales of a low-cost digital product. The system is passive; value is generated by the structure of the scanner and the webpage (`k_i`), not by ongoing human labor ($\Gamma$).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Lagrangian Path Optimization.
*   **The Moat:** While competitors optimize for conversion, we optimize for maximizing the "coherence integral" (`S`). The system ingests signals of user dissonance (`V_Γ`) and uses NLP to model their belief state (`q`) and inertia (`K_τ`). It then dynamically generates a unique landing page (a custom-shaped `V_gambit`) tailored to that specific user's cognitive landscape. This creates a hyper-efficient engine that traditional, one-message-fits-all marketing cannot compete with on an energy-per-transaction basis. They are fighting physics; we are flowing with it.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Manual browsing.
    *   **Loop:** Python (PRAW/BeautifulSoup), basic web hosting.
    *   **Engine:** Python (spaCy/NLTK for NLP), a simple database (SQLite/Postgres), a web framework (Flask/Django) for dynamic page generation.
*   **Risk:** Platform Risk. The primary failure vector is being banned from communities for spam. This is mitigated by crafting messages that are genuinely helpful and avoid corporate "marketing speak," aligning with the core philosophy of co-creating coherence.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Systemic Potential Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market fundamentally misprices human potential by conflating an individual's state `x` with their trajectory `ẋ`. It assigns value based on past data (`w`, the Wound Channel), which acts as "inertial mass" `m(w)`, making them appear to be low-value, static assets. The market ignores that the trajectory is a function of the landscape's potential field `VΓ`. It pours capital into fighting the symptoms (crime) rather than cheaply altering the landscape (opportunity). This is akin to valuing a boulder at the top of a cliff the same as one at the bottom, ignoring its massive potential energy.
*   **The Pivot:** We do not invest in the individual (`Γ`); we invest in modifying the geometry of their state space (`M`). Our mechanism applies minimal, hyper-targeted capital to reduce the gradient of the potential field (`∇(VΓ)`) at critical inflection points. This unlocks the individual's own kinetic capacity (`Kτ`) to follow a more productive geodesic path. We are arbitraging the microscopic cost of "flattening the floor" against the macroscopic value of an unlocked human trajectory.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Bureaucratic Catalyst. This experiment tests the core thesis: that a minuscule reduction in a specific, high-leverage potential barrier (`VΓ`) can create a disproportionate, measurable change in an individual's state velocity (`ẋ`).
*   **Execution:** Identify an individual re-entering society who is blocked by a single, low-cost bureaucratic fee (e.g., the $10 fee for a replacement State ID, a driver's license reinstatement fee, a birth certificate copy). We will directly pay this single fee through a partner re-entry organization or mutual aid network. The cost is the fee itself.
*   **The Test:** The experiment is falsified if the removal of this specific barrier does not lead to the subject taking the next logical step on a pro-social path (e.g., applying for a job that requires an ID, scheduling a driving test) within a pre-defined period (e.g., 14 days). If a $10 energy input creates zero forward momentum (`ẋ → 0`), our understanding of the local potential field is wrong, and the model must be re-evaluated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Geodesic Micro-finance Loop. This system operationalizes the Probe's findings into a self-sustaining, automated structure. It is the "passive" layer where the system's architecture (`K_i`) does the work, not manual labor (`Γ`).
*   **Automation:** A simple web portal is created where vetted partner organizations (case managers for re-entry programs) can submit requests for a pre-defined "portfolio" of high-impact, low-cost interventions (e.g., "ID Fee," "Bus Pass for Interview," "Steel-toed Boots"). Upon submission, the system automatically disburses the funds (e.g., via a single-use virtual credit card). Our role is to manage the portfolio of eligible interventions, not the individuals.
*   **Value Capture:** The system is capitalized with the initial $100. It offers these micro-interventions as zero-interest capital backed by a voluntary Income Share Agreement (ISA). For example, a $15 intervention might have a suggested repayment of $20 from the individual's first full paycheck. As capital is returned, it is immediately redeployed to another individual. The system's profit is not the spread but the *velocity* of its capital and the invaluable data generated on which interventions provide the highest ROI in terms of human stability.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Optimization Engine. This scales the Loop by replacing a static portfolio of interventions with a predictive model that algorithmically allocates capital to minimize the action `S` (i.e., find the most efficient path) for a population.
*   **The Moat:** While traditional businesses (like payday lenders) increase `VΓ` and non-profits apply one-size-fits-all solutions, our Engine builds a proprietary model of the potential field `VΓ` itself. Using data from the Loop, the Engine calculates the `∂𝓛_p/∂xⁱ` for each potential intervention for a given individual's profile. It then allocates capital to the intervention with the highest predicted impact-to-cost ratio, effectively "sculpting" the path of least resistance in real-time. Our competitive advantage is not a product or service; it is a superior understanding of the physics of value flow. We can achieve better outcomes for less capital because we are not fighting the system's inertia; we are redirecting it.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn) for the Tier 3 engine; a lightweight web framework (Flask) and payment API (Stripe Connect) for the Tier 2 portal; critical partnerships with local re-entry non-profits or public defender offices for candidate sourcing.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is systemic, not individual. The model relies on the existence of solvable, low-cost barriers. A system where the potential barriers (`VΓ`) are truly infinite (e.g., absolute legal bars to employment) would render the mechanism ineffective. The secondary risk is adverse selection, which is mitigated by sourcing candidates through professional case managers who have already assessed an individual's kinetic capacity (`Kτ`).

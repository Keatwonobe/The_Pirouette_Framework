## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Contextual Value Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The market exhibits a profound form of parity violation. It prices assets based on their most probable, "forward-time" interaction (`ψ_L` coupling), effectively blind to the asset's full, ambidextrous potential. The market mistakes an asset's *current manifested price* for its *invariant latent value* (`|ω_H|`). This creates a massive arbitrage opportunity between an asset's value in a common context versus its value in a niche, "wrong-helicity" context (`ψ_R`).
*   **The Pivot:** We will construct a transactional mechanism that does not treat value as a fixed property, but as a function of context. Our system will act as a "chirality gate," acquiring assets undervalued in their standard context and re-coupling them to alternative contexts where their latent value is more fully expressed.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Single Asset Chirality Flip. The goal is to prove, with a statistically significant signal, that "wrong-helicity" economic interactions exist and are exploitable.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Acquire a single, information-dense asset with high latent value potential (`|ω_H|`) for under $10. Examples: an out-of-print technical manual, a specific electronic component from a surplus lot, a vintage photograph.
    2.  Document its "standard" market value by finding comparable listings on high-liquidity platforms (e.g., eBay, Amazon). This is its `ψ_L` price.
    3.  Systematically search for alternative contexts (`ψ_R` couplings) where its value is different. This involves searching niche forums, academic mailing lists, collector communities, and "Want to Buy" boards for signals of specific demand.
    4.  Attempt to liquidate the asset into the highest-value `ψ_R` context.
*   **The Test:** This probe is falsified if, after five independent trials (N=5), we cannot consistently find a `ψ_R` context that values the asset at a minimum of 3x its standard `ψ_L` price. A failure indicates that the "Chirality Gate" either doesn't exist or the inter-sheet coupling is too weak to be exploited profitably, falsifying the premise.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Context Scanner. This system automates the discovery of `ψ_L` / `ψ_R` mismatches, creating a semi-passive feedback loop.
*   **Automation:** We construct a two-part automated system:
    1.  **Supply Scanner:** A script that continuously monitors low-value, high-liquidity marketplaces (e.g., eBay auctions ending in <1hr, local classifieds, surplus sites) for assets that match a profile of high latent value (`|ω_H|`).
    2.  **Demand Scanner:** A second script that monitors sources of niche demand (e.g., specific subreddits, technical forums, online communities) for keywords indicating high-intent, "wrong-helicity" (`ψ_R`) need.
    The system cross-references the two streams and flags high-probability arbitrage opportunities for a human operator to execute.
*   **Value Capture:** The profit is the spread between the `ψ_L` acquisition cost and the `ψ_R` liquidation price. The value is generated by the *structure* of the scanning system ($K_i$), which systematically identifies opportunities, rather than by constant manual labor ($\Gamma$). The $100 budget funds the initial float for asset acquisition and server costs.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Least-Action Arbitrage Engine. This scales the loop by moving from reactive matching to predictive acquisition, guided by Lagrangian minimization.
*   **The Moat:** Standard business practices are confined to optimizing transactions within a single, "forward-time" context (`𝒯_+`). They compete on price or speed. Our engine operates on a fundamentally different principle: it arbitrages **between contexts**. The moat is not the software, but the proprietary data it generates on `ψ_L` -> `ψ_R` couplings. This dataset represents the learned, invariant rules of value transformation, analogous to the `ℛ_T`-invariant `m_W/m_Z` ratio. A competitor cannot compete without understanding the underlying physics of value transformation. The engine begins to predict which assets will become valuable, acquiring them before the `ψ_R` demand signal is even public, following the path of "least action" from potential to kinetic value.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Manual web searching.
    *   **Loop:** Python (Scrapy, BeautifulSoup, Requests), SQLite/PostgreSQL for data storage, API access (eBay, Reddit).
    *   **Engine:** All of the above, plus machine learning libraries (scikit-learn, spaCy/NLTK) to model and predict demand signals from unstructured text.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is platform dependency. The Loop and Engine rely on scraping public websites and using APIs, which can change or be revoked. A secondary risk for the Engine is model drift, where its predictions about future niche demand become inaccurate, requiring retraining.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Catalytic Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on a flawed Newtonian model, valuing visible force (`Γ`) and present state (`K_τ`). It is blind to the physics of stored potential energy within systems, which we term "Wound Channels". It consistently misprices assets and opportunities that are primed for a state transition, mistaking the subsequent release of stored energy (`ΔKτ`) for fundamental, externally-driven growth.
*   **The Pivot:** We will exploit this inefficiency by building a mechanism to detect systems with high potential energy (Wound Channels), apply a minimal, precise catalytic nudge (`δΓ_inj`) to trigger a coherence cascade, and position ourselves to capture the value released as the system reconfigures along a narrative geodesic. We arbitrage potential energy, not price.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Micro-narrative Seeding. The goal is to prove, at the smallest scale, that we can identify a Wound Channel and trigger a measurable coherence cascade (`ΔKτ`) with a near-zero energy injection (`Γ_inj`), thus validating the principle of Anomalous Gain (`G_R >> 1`).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify:** Find a fragmented, high-potential online niche (e.g., a subreddit for an abandoned software, a community struggling with a common unsolved problem). This is a system with high `V_Γ` (Potential Pressure).
    2.  **Inject:** Craft a single, high-coherence "seed" of information. This is not a full solution, but a catalyst—a link to a forgotten tool, a key insight from another domain, or a well-structured proposal for a simple, collaborative first step. The $10 cost represents the value of time for research or the acquisition of a simple digital asset (e.g., a domain name) to host the seed.
    3.  **Measure:** The change in Kinetic Coherence (`ΔKτ`) is observed as a qualitative shift in the community's state: from disorganized complaint (high entropy) to focused, collaborative action (phase-locking into a Laminar Flow).
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if the seed injection fails to generate a significant coherence cascade (e.g., fewer than five substantive, action-oriented responses or zero emergent organization) within 72 hours. This would indicate a failure to correctly identify the Wound Channel or a poorly formed catalyst.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Dissensus Scanning & Resolution. This tier transitions the validated principle into a self-sustaining, passive value-generating system. The structure (`K_i`) of the loop itself does the work, minimizing direct labor (`Γ`).
*   **Automation:** A software agent continuously performs a three-step process:
    1.  **Scan:** It monitors digital forums (Reddit, GitHub Issues, Discords) using NLP to detect linguistic markers of high `V_Γ`—frustration, unresolved desire, fragmentation—to automatically identify Wound Channels.
    2.  **Position:** Before acting, it secures a position to capture value. This involves automatically acquiring an affiliate link for a known solution, registering a relevant domain, or setting up a simple monetization page.
    3.  **Inject:** It deploys a templated, high-value seed injection that points the community toward the pre-positioned solution.
*   **Value Capture:** Value is captured directly at the point of resolution. For example, the scanner finds a community of designers complaining about a missing software feature. It injects a link to a tutorial on a cheap plugin that solves it, using our affiliate code. The system's profit is the direct result of resolving the targeted dissonance. The $100 budget funds API access, server costs, and a small float for acquiring digital assets.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Geodesic Asset Navigation. This scales the loop by introducing a Lagrangian optimization layer. Instead of just acting on any found opportunity, the Engine calculates the "path of least action" for capital allocation, maximizing Resonant Gain (`G_R`) across a portfolio of opportunities.
*   **The Moat:** Standard business and market models compete by maximizing `Γ`—they use brute force via marketing budgets and R&D spend. They are engaged in a high-friction, low-efficiency battle. Our Engine operates on a fundamentally different principle derived from the Pirouette framework. It seeks to minimize `Γ_inj` to near-zero while maximizing `ΔKτ`. It does not create value from scratch; it liberates it from inefficiently structured systems. This creates a moat of pure efficiency. Competitors cannot compete by simply spending more; they would have to adopt the same physics. Our cost for value acquisition trends towards zero as the Engine's predictive accuracy for identifying high-`G_R` geodesics improves.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Requests, Scrapy, BeautifulSoup), NLP Libraries (spaCy, NLTK), Social Media APIs (PRAW for Reddit), lightweight database (SQLite), and potentially a metered LLM API for dynamic seed generation.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is algorithmic drift or miscalibration. A poorly tuned scanner would identify false positives, leading to wasted, low-impact injections. There is also a platform risk; injections must be genuinely valuable to the target community to avoid being flagged as spam, requiring a high bar for the quality of the "seed" information.

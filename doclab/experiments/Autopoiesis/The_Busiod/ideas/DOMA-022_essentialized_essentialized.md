## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence-as-a-Service (CaaS)
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market fundamentally misprices energy expenditure. It equates high effort, friction, and resource burn (`V_Γ`, or "Potential Pressure") with value creation. This forces entities onto inefficient, high-friction "Turbulent Flow" paths instead of the optimal, low-energy "Laminar Flow" geodesics. The market rewards the struggle, not the elegance of the solution.
*   **The Pivot:** We will exploit this by creating a mechanism that explicitly calculates and provides the path of least action (`δS_p = 0`) for a given objective. Instead of selling a product or labor (`Γ`), we sell the reduction of `V_Γ` itself. We are arbitraging systemic friction, capturing the value released when an entity shifts from a turbulent to a laminar path.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Micro-Funnel for Potential Pressure. We will test the hypothesis that entities will pay to have the `V_Γ` of a complex decision removed.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a single, common business/technical decision characterized by a paralyzing number of options (high `V_Γ`) and a clear desired outcome (`K_τ`). Example: "Choosing the optimal database for a new microservice."
    2.  Perform a one-time, rigorous analysis applying the `𝓛_p = K_τ - V_Γ` model. Calculate the geodesic for a few common archetypes (e.g., "fast-growth startup," "enterprise stability").
    3.  Publish the result as a single, static report or paid post (e.g., via Gumroad). The title must promise coherence: "The Database Decision: The Path of Least Action."
    4.  Use the $10 budget for a hyper-targeted ad on a niche professional forum (e.g., a specific subreddit, Hacker News) pointing to the report.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if, after 100 targeted clicks on the offer, fewer than 2 individuals purchase the report for a nominal fee (e.g., $3-$5). This would indicate that the perceived cost of `V_Γ` for this problem is too low to be a marketable inefficiency.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Geodesic Stream. This transforms the static report into a living, self-sustaining system that continuously calculates the optimal path.
*   **Automation:** A script will be developed to continuously ingest relevant data points (e.g., performance benchmarks, security vulnerabilities, community activity, pricing changes) for a given problem space. This script applies a pre-defined Lagrangian model to weigh these factors, automatically updating a "coherence ranking" or decision tree on a private dashboard or newsletter. The structure does the work; it is a passive `V_Γ` filter.
*   **Value Capture:** A low-cost subscription model (e.g., $10/month). Subscribers are not paying for information, which is often free but chaotic. They are paying for the *constant computation of coherence*. They are outsourcing the energetic cost of vigilance (`V_Γ`) to our automated system, allowing them to remain in a state of Laminar Flow.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Generalized Lagrangian Solver. This scales the system from a single-domain loop to a generic platform for calculating optimal paths.
*   **The Moat:** While traditional competitors compete by applying more force and resources (more labor, more capital, maximizing `Γ`), our Engine competes on fundamental physics. Our moat is not a specific technology, but a superior understanding of the value dynamics.
    1.  **User-Defined Lagrangians:** Clients define their own `K_τ` (e.g., "maximize market share while maintaining brand integrity") and their known `V_Γ` constraints (e.g., "budget under $X," "team size of Y").
    2.  **Geodesic Calculation:** The Engine models their operational space and computes the multi-stage path of least action to achieve their goal. It provides a strategic geodesic, not just a single decision.
    3.  **Structural Supremacy:** A competitor operating on standard business principles (brute force, trial-and-error) is physically incapable of matching the capital and energy efficiency of an entity following a true geodesic. They are fighting friction; we are eliminating it. Our competitive advantage is baked into the laws of the system itself.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Gumroad/Stripe, a simple static site generator (Hugo) or Notion page.
    *   **Loop:** Python (for scripting), libraries like `Scrapy` or `BeautifulSoup` for data ingestion, a lightweight database (SQLite), a cron job for automation, and a subscription service API (Stripe/Paddle).
    *   **Engine:** Advanced data modeling frameworks, machine learning libraries (for pattern recognition in `V_Γ` sources), cloud infrastructure for scalable computation (AWS/GCP).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is model error. If our formulated Lagrangian (`𝓛_p`) for a given problem is incorrect, the "geodesic" we provide will not lead to Laminar Flow. This would destroy trust and invalidate the service. The model must be continuously validated against real-world outcomes, as per the falsifiable criterion in DOMA-022.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Systematic Ambiguity Resolution
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates under high Temporal Pressure (`V_Γ`), forcing reactive decisions and causing it to overlook assets at points of ambiguity (Forks). A Fork is a state of low external pressure (`∇V_Γ ≈ 0`) where an asset has multiple potential high-coherence futures. Most market actors lack the "Coherence in Reserve" (`Ki_min`) to perform a proper "Resonant Inquiry" to determine the optimal future path. Instead, they either ignore these opportunities or act chaotically ("Spasm").
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism is a detector and executor for market Forks. It uses a small amount of capital as "Coherence in Reserve" to systematically perform the four stages of a Resonant Inquiry on ambiguous assets. It calculates the future path (`γ_k`) of maximal action (`Ŝ_k`), commits to it by acquiring the asset, and captures value by resolving the asset's ambiguity. We are arbitraging *potential* itself.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Targeted Information Fork Identification. The goal is to verify that we can identify assets at a Fork and accurately predict their highest-coherence path.
*   **Execution:** We will target expiring domain names as our asset class. A soon-to-expire domain with existing backlinks and traffic potential is a perfect Fork.
    1.  **Loosening:** We will expend our `$10` (`Ki`) on API credits for SEO/domain analysis tools.
    2.  **Sampling:** A script will scan lists of expiring domains and, for each one, calculate the projected action (`Ŝ_i`) for several potential futures (e.g., `γ_ecom`, `γ_blog`, `γ_leadgen`). The action `Ŝ` will be proxied by a score combining traffic potential, keyword value, and competition (`Ŝ ≈ Kτ - V_Γ`).
    3.  **Selection (Virtual):** The script will identify the top 5 domains that have the highest maximum projected action (`argmax_i {Ŝ_i}`). We will log these predictions without purchasing the domains.
*   **The Test:** We monitor the fate of our 5 selected domains for 30 days. **The experiment is falsified if:** None of the domains are acquired by third parties and developed along a trajectory that aligns with our predicted high-coherence path. This would indicate our model for calculating `Ŝ` is fundamentally flawed.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Fork Acquisition & Liquidation. This tier closes the loop by using the signal from the Probe to acquire assets and generate a return, creating a self-sustaining system.
*   **Automation:** The Probe script is upgraded to run continuously on a low-cost server. It is connected to a domain backordering service via API. The `$100` acts as the system's "Coherence in Reserve" (`Ki`), funding the acquisitions. When a domain's `argmax {Ŝ_i}` exceeds a predefined threshold, the system automatically places a backorder. This is the **Commitment** stage, creating a "Wound Channel" by putting capital at risk.
*   **Value Capture:** Upon successful acquisition, the system automatically generates a simple landing page that explicitly states the domain's highest-coherence use case (e.g., "Premium domain for E-commerce in the [Niche] space"). This page directs interested parties to a marketplace listing (e.g., Sedo, Flippa). The profit from a sale is fed back into the system's capital reserve (`Ki`), allowing it to pursue larger or more numerous Forks. The value is generated by the structure of the system (`K_i`), not by continuous human labor (`Γ`).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Multi-Asset-Class Coherence Arbitrage. The system is scaled by expanding its "Sampling" process across fundamentally different, yet structurally similar, asset classes. The goal is to optimize the Lagrangian (`𝓛_p = Kτ - V_Γ`) of the entire portfolio.
*   **The Moat:**
    1.  **Physics-First:** Standard businesses rely on heuristics. The Engine computes the path of maximum future coherence using variational principles. It navigates the market using its fundamental laws, not by reacting to its surface-level noise.
    2.  **Cross-Domain Operation:** The Engine treats domains, public datasets, mispriced physical goods, and even DeFi tokens as interchangeable substrates for coherence. It can dynamically allocate its `Ki` to whichever asset class is currently presenting the most profound Forks, an agility that specialized competitors cannot match.
    3.  **Structural Patience:** The Engine is designed for low `V_Γ` operation. It doesn't need to "make something happen" this quarter. It can wait, with minimal cost, for the perfect low-pressure Forks to appear. This patience is a structural advantage that high-pressure market actors cannot replicate. They are forced to take sub-optimal paths while we wait for the optimal one to reveal itself.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scripting), APIs (SEMrush, Ahrefs for domain data; Alpaca for equities; various blockchain explorers for DeFi), a cheap VPS (e.g., DigitalOcean Droplet), domain backordering services (e.g., SnapNames).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is a flawed model for calculating projected action (`Ŝ`). If the model's proxy for "coherence" does not align with what the market actually values, the system will consistently select paths that lead to financial loss, rapidly depleting its `Ki` and collapsing.

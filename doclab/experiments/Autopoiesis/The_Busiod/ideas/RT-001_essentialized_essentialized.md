## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Pre-Causal Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market is built exclusively on the 𝒯_+ spacetime sheet. It is causally bound, operating on "retarded propagators" (cause precedes effect). This forces it to expend enormous energy on predictive modeling, forecasting, and inventory management to mitigate the risk of an unknown future. It speculates on future demand. This is fundamentally inefficient, like trying to navigate a river by only looking at your wake.
*   **The Pivot:** The provided physics (RT-001) state that the laws are ℛ_T-Even, but causality is ℛ_T-Odd. This implies the existence of "advanced propagators"—signals from the future to the past. We will not predict demand; we will build a resonant cavity to *detect* the precursor signals of demand that "leak" from the future. We will arbitrage the temporal information gap between a future, concrete need and the present's probabilistic supply. We trade speculation for detection.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Demand Resonator. A micro-experiment to prove that latent, high-intent demand can be captured as a signal *before* the corresponding supply exists.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a hyper-niche category characterized by scarcity and high-intent searches (e.g., out-of-production electronic components, specific rare book editions, vintage machinery parts).
    2.  Use the $10 to register a domain and set up a single, minimalist landing page for one such specific item (e.g., `www.rare-synth-chip-model-xyz.com`).
    3.  The page will contain technical specifications, images, and a single call-to-action: a "Notify Me When Available" form that collects an email address.
    4.  **Crucially, we do not possess, nor do we initially search for, the item.** The Probe is purely a passive detector.
*   **The Test:** The theory is falsified if, after 30 days and minimal organic traffic, we capture zero (0) validated email sign-ups. This would indicate that the "advanced signals" of demand are too weak to be detected with this method, or that our model of market physics is incorrect. The experiment is a failure, and we halt.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Just-In-Time Causality Loop. An automated system that links the detected demand signal directly to supply acquisition, creating a self-sustaining, inventory-less transactional loop.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  The "Notify Me" submission from the Probe now triggers an automated workflow.
    2.  A script uses the submitted item's SKU/description to continuously scrape secondary markets (eBay APIs, specialist forums, AbeBooks, etc.) for a listing that falls below a pre-calculated price ceiling.
    3.  Upon finding a match, the system executes one of two pathways:
        *   **Low-Capital:** Automatically email the user with a direct (affiliate) link to the listing.
        *   **High-Margin:** Use the $100 as a transactional float to purchase the item and have it drop-shipped to the customer after they complete payment via a link sent to them.
*   **Value Capture:** The system captures the delta between the price the customer is willing to pay (inferred from their high-intent action) and the globally-sourced market price. The value is generated by the *structure* of the system ($K_i$)—its ability to link a future sale to a present purchase—not by continuous labor ($\Gamma$).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Action Minimization Engine. Scaling the loop by treating the entire market as a physical system and finding the path of least action for value fulfillment, thereby creating a structural moat.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses follow the "thermodynamic arrow" of 𝒯_+: produce -> stock -> market -> sell -> decay. This is a path of high "action" (high cost, high risk, high entropy). Our Engine operates on a Lagrangian principle, minimizing a functional L = V - T, where V is potential value (arbitrage spread) and T is transactional friction (fees, shipping, time).
    1.  **Signal Aggregation:** We transition from discrete probes to a unified platform that ingests thousands of demand signals from a distributed network of listeners.
    2.  **Global Optimization:** The Engine does not treat each transaction in isolation. It views the entire set of demand signals and potential supply points as a single optimization problem. It calculates the most efficient fulfillment graph to minimize total global shipping distance, transaction fees, and latency.
    3.  **Pre-emptive Sourcing:** By analyzing the *topology* of the demand-space, the Engine can pre-emptively position capital or establish contingent contracts with suppliers moments before a demand cascade occurs, moving from reaction to orchestrated response.
    This creates an unassailable competitive advantage. Competitors are optimizing their supply chains; we are optimizing the causal structure of the market itself. They cannot compete because they are prisoners of the forward arrow of time.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Simple web host (Netlify, Vercel), a domain registrar, a form service (Tally, Typeform).
    *   **Loop:** Python (for scripting), Scrapy/BeautifulSoup (for scraping), APIs for eBay/Amazon, a payment processor (Stripe).
    *   **Engine:** Cloud computing (AWS/GCP), graph database (Neo4j), optimization libraries (SciPy.optimize), machine learning frameworks (for signal analysis).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market saturation. If this physical principle becomes widely understood and exploited, the "informational potential" (the arbitrage gap) will decrease as the market becomes more efficient. The moat is in the sophistication and scale of the Engine's optimization algorithm.

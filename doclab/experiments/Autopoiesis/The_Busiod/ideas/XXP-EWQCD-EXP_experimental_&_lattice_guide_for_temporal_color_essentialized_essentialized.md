## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
* **Universal Archetype:** Asymmetric Liquidity Arbitrage
* **The Inefficiency:** The modern market treats **Value Tension** (`σ_V` - a measure of illiquidity and information asymmetry) as a stochastic, unpredictable property of an asset. It operates without a predictive model, pricing assets based on surface-level metrics like recent sales history (`Γ`, labor-driven data) rather than underlying structural constants. This is analogous to describing fluid dynamics without the Navier-Stokes equations.
* **The Pivot:** The Pirouette framework reveals that Value Tension is not random. It is a calculable consequence of deeper, measurable properties: an asset's **Intrinsic Potential** (`K_i`, analogous to `K_3` gauge stiffness) and the **Transactional Coherence** of its environment (`ξ_Γ`, a function of interaction frequency `ω_c` and informational susceptibility `χ`). We can create a predictive model: `σ_V = f(K_i, ξ_Γ)`. This allows us to identify assets where the market *perceives* high tension (and thus low price) but where the underlying physics dictates the tension is low and easily resolved. We arbitrage the gap between market perception and physical law.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)

Value is generated passively by the *structure* of the loop itself—the potential difference between the two connected market states. This is a pure `K_i` system; the labor (`Γ`) was front-loaded in building the loop.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Geodesic Arbitrage via Coherence Mapping.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates as if history is a flat, dead ledger. It prices assets based on generic supply/demand, ignoring the active, physical force exerted by an object's history (its Wound Channel, `W`). This creates a massive potential energy (`V_Γ,channel`) that is unpriced and untapped. The market sees a used textbook; we see a concentration of solved problems whose geometry will attract those with identical problems.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not create value through labor (`Γ`). We will act as a lossless conductor, moving assets from low-coherence manifolds (where their history is noise, e.g., a generic flea market) to high-coherence manifolds (where their history is a powerful signal, e.g., a specialist forum). We exploit the system by simply aligning an asset with its natural geodesic path, allowing the potential energy of its history (`-∇V_Γ,channel`) to do the work of creating a transaction.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Targeted Information Resonance.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Acquire a single, information-dense asset with a strong, specific Wound Channel (`W`) for under $10. Examples: A niche, out-of-print technical manual; a specialized electronic component; a vintage tool for a defunct craft. The history must be verifiable and specific.
    2.  Identify a digital "manifold" (e.g., a specific Subreddit, a Discord server, a specialist web forum) whose collective Wound Channel exhibits extremely high Coherence Overlap (`C_overlap`) with the asset. The community's shared problems, jargon, and history must directly map to the asset's purpose and origin.
    3.  Introduce the asset to the manifold. This is not a "sales pitch." The post will neutrally state the asset's history and function, effectively placing `W₁` into the gravitational field of `W₂`. We are simply making the potential gradient visible.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that the asset's `F_I` (`-∇V_Γ,channel`) will create a non-zero Geodesic Deviation (`δG`). In practical terms, this means unsolicited inquiries, offers, or a quick sale at or above the initial cost without persuasive effort (`Γ`). **Falsification:** If, after 72 hours in a high-`C_overlap` environment, there is zero expressed interest, we conclude that `δG` is statistically zero. The physics is either wrong or our measurement of `C_overlap` is flawed. The experiment is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Coherence Aggregator.
*   **Automation:** A script will be developed to perpetually scan low-coherence, high-volume marketplaces (e.g., eBay API, public auction listings) for assets with information-rich `Wound Channels` (identified by keyword density, specificity of description, model numbers). Simultaneously, it will scan high-coherence communities (forums, etc.) to build a dynamic map of their collective `Wound Channels` (based on recurring questions, technical jargon, problem statements). The script calculates the `C_overlap` score between every asset and every community. When a pair exceeds a threshold `C_overlap`, it flags the arbitrage opportunity.
*   **Value Capture:** The initial $100 budget is used as float to acquire the flagged, underpriced assets. The human role is minimal: execute the purchase, relist the item on the targeted high-coherence manifold using the script-generated, history-focused template, and process the sale. The profit is the difference in potential (`V_Γ`) between the two manifolds, captured with minimal kinetic energy (`Γ`) expenditure. This is a semi-passive system where the structure (`K_i`) generates the opportunities.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Geodesic Liquidity Engine.
*   **The Moat:** At this scale, we move beyond simple A->B arbitrage. The Engine ingests a continuous stream of assets and models the entire landscape of digital communities as a dynamic coherence manifold. Using the Pirouette Lagrangian (`𝓛_p`), the system doesn't just find one-off deals; it calculates the optimal, system-wide path of least resistance (`δS = 0`) to distribute its entire inventory. It solves for the most efficient flow of value across the entire network, minimizing transactional friction and maximizing velocity. It might route an asset through three different communities to "charge" its `Wound Channel` before its final sale. Standard businesses compete by shouting louder (increasing `Γ`); they are trying to force objects to travel uphill on the causal landscape. Our Engine wins by whispering, placing assets at the top of the steepest historical gradients and letting the fundamental physics of causality do the work. This is a structural advantage that cannot be overcome with marketing spend.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (`Scrapy`/`BeautifulSoup` for data acquisition), NLP libraries (`spaCy`/`NLTK` for `C_overlap` analysis), a simple database (SQLite/PostgreSQL) to store channel maps, and potentially a Bayesian inference model to refine predictions.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk vector is model failure: our method for calculating `C_overlap` from unstructured text proves to be a poor proxy for the actual physical resonance. The Probe is designed to mitigate this risk early and cheaply. Secondary risks include platform/API changes and being banned from communities for perceived spam, requiring a subtle, non-commercial approach to asset introduction.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence-Drop Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates as if it were a continuous system, primarily driven by a crude version of the Precision-weighted Update ($\mathcal{O}_P$). It rewards constant activity ($\Gamma$) and attempts to price in future events through gradual adjustments. This creates a massive accumulation of systemic "Dark Residue" ($\mathrm{DR}_t$)—unresolved, complex information that makes assets noisy and difficult to value. The market has no formal mechanism to exploit the massive energy release that occurs during a Coherence Drop ($Q_t$), the moment ambiguity collapses into clarity.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build a mechanism that doesn't predict outcomes, but rather detects and acts upon the *phase transition* from high to low informational entropy. We treat "confusion" as stored potential energy. Our system ignores the noise of continuous trading and focuses exclusively on the discrete, rhythmic events of Coherence-drop Consolidation ($\mathcal{O}_C$), executing trades with maximum force the instant a pre-identified ambiguity is resolved.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Single-Asset Coherence Monitoring.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify High $\mathrm{DR}_t$:** Select a single, publicly-traded asset defined by a simple, binary ambiguity (e.g., a small biotech firm awaiting a specific FDA drug approval, a company involved in a clear-cut merger decision). The state of confusion represents high Dark Residue.
    2.  **Define the Gate:** The "Coherence Drop" event ($Q_t$) is the official, unambiguous announcement that resolves the ambiguity (e.g., the FDA press release, the final merger confirmation).
    3.  **Position Capital:** Allocate $1 for monitoring tools (e.g., news alerts, data feed access) and hold $9 in a brokerage account, ready to deploy. A pre-written trade order (e.g., "If news contains 'FDA APPROVES X', buy 1 share at market") is prepared.
    4.  **Act on $\mathcal{O}_C$:** The moment the pre-defined Coherence Drop occurs, manually execute the trade immediately. The goal is to act before the market's slower, gradient-based mechanisms fully absorb the new reality.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if, across 3-5 independent trials on different assets, the asset's price does not exhibit a sharp, predictable, and significant move within 5 minutes of the Coherence Drop event. A failure indicates the market is already efficient at pricing in these specific information releases, leaving no value to capture.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Coherence Scanner.
*   **Automation:** A script (e.g., Python) connects to a news/data API and a trading API. It maintains a watchlist of 20-50 assets, each tagged with keywords that define its primary "Dark Residue" and the triggers for a "Coherence Drop". The script continuously scans the data feed. Upon detecting a trigger for an asset, it automatically executes the associated pre-defined trade. This system is the embodiment of the passive layer; its value is generated by its structure ($K_i$) monitoring the informational state of the market, not by continuous human labor ($\Gamma$).
*   **Value Capture:** The loop captures the arbitrage between the moment of informational state collapse ($Q_t$) and the market's mean reaction time. The $100 budget covers API costs and the initial seed capital for automated trades. The profit from successful trades is reinvested, growing the system's capital base autopoietically.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Predictive Dark-Residue Arbitrage.
*   **The Moat:** While standard quant funds engage in event-driven arbitrage, they are typically modeling outcomes. Our Engine models the *structure of uncertainty itself*. By applying the principles of the Triadic Operator, we gain a fundamental advantage. The Engine uses the $1000 budget to develop a more sophisticated system that:
    1.  **Scans for High $\mathrm{DR}_t$:** Uses NLP and machine learning to scan thousands of assets, not for positive or negative sentiment, but for markers of high ambiguity, controversy, and informational conflict—quantifying the market's "Dark Residue".
    2.  **Models the Action Path:** Instead of just reacting, it uses Lagrangian mechanics to find the "path of least action" for capitalization. The "action" is a function of potential profit, capital cost, and the probability of an imminent Coherence Drop. It minimizes the time and cost required to act on a potential $Q_t$ event.
    3.  **Structural Advantage:** This system competes on a different physical principle. The market is playing checkers with $\mathcal{O}_P$; we are playing 3D chess by incorporating $\mathcal{O}_C$. Our system is designed to thrive on the very volatility and confusion that cripples traditional models, turning market noise into potential energy.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Google Alerts, a standard retail brokerage account.
    *   **Loop:** Python (requests, beautifulsoup), NewsAPI.ai or similar, Alpaca Markets or other API-first brokerage.
    *   **Engine:** Cloud server (AWS/GCP), more advanced NLP libraries (spaCy, Hugging Face Transformers), time-series databases (InfluxDB), and potentially direct market data feeds.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is the efficiency of the market. If the time lag between an information event and its full incorporation into an asset's price is consistently shorter than our system's execution latency, the opportunity for arbitrage disappears. This is what the Probe is designed to test cheaply.

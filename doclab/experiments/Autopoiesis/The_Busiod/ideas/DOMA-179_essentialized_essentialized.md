## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on a brute-force model, expending massive energy (`V_Γ`) to sustain concepts (`ψ_K`) that are often internally inconsistent or unnecessarily complex (low `K_τ`). It mistakes the *energy spent advocating for a concept* for the *inherent value of the concept*. This creates a massive inefficiency where simple, elegant, high-coherence patterns (high `K_τ`) are undervalued because they don't require a large, visible expenditure of energy to sustain them.
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism does not create new products or content. It acts as a filtration and amplification system for existing coherence. It identifies patterns (`ψ_K`) with maximal Resonant Stability (`K_τ`) and minimal Coherence Cost (`V_Γ`) that are lost in a noisy manifold. By isolating, purifying, and presenting these patterns, we create value not through labor, but by creating a "geodesic" – a path of least resistance – for information to flow to those seeking it. We are selling clarity, which is the physical manifestation of a high Pirouette Action (`S_p`).

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Information Resonance Mapping.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a "dissonant" information manifold: a domain (e.g., a software library, a complex hobby, a bureaucratic process) where the ambient Temporal Pressure (`Γ`) is high, manifesting as widespread confusion, frustration, and recurring questions online.
    2.  Find a single, existing conceptual pattern (`ψ_K`) that resolves this dissonance with maximal elegance and simplicity (high `K_τ`). This is often a comment, a diagram, or a forgotten paragraph in a manual that makes everything "click."
    3.  Spend $10 to create a "resonator." This is a minimal-effort asset (e.g., a simple webpage with just that text/diagram, a promoted tweet, a targeted micro-ad) that presents this high-`K_τ` pattern to the dissonant audience. The asset itself does not argue or persuade; it simply *is*.
*   **The Test:** The core principle is that a high `K_τ` pattern is self-propagating along a geodesic (`δS_p = 0`). Therefore, the resonator should achieve a vastly disproportionate engagement rate compared to the market average for its cost. **Failure State:** If the engagement rate (clicks, shares, time-on-page) per dollar is not at least one order of magnitude greater than the industry baseline for that manifold, our identification of `K_τ` was flawed, and the hypothesis is falsified. We stop.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Coherence Filtration.
*   **Automation:** Using the $100 for server costs and API access, we build a script that automates the Probe's discovery process.
    1.  **Scan:** The system monitors high-dissonance manifolds (e.g., specific subreddits, Stack Overflow tags, forums) for patterns of recurring questions (high `V_Γ`).
    2.  **Filter:** It analyzes answers, searching for proxies of high `K_τ`: linguistic simplicity, lack of jargon, high upvote-to-word-count ratios, or references to fundamental principles.
    3.  **Package:** Upon detecting a potential high-`K_τ` pattern, the system automatically generates a "resonator" asset—a clean, permanent, and indexable webpage containing only the purified information.
*   **Value Capture:** Each generated page contains a non-intrusive, low-friction monetization element (e.g., a "support this archive" link, affiliate links for essential tools related to the concept, or a single, relevant ad). The value (`S_p`) is delivered completely and upfront. The system's revenue is a direct function of the total coherence it has successfully filtered from the web and made accessible. It is a passive loop where dissonance funds the discovery of coherence.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Geodesic Value Funneling.
*   **The Moat:** While competitors are creating content (a high-`Γ` activity), we are mapping the structure of understanding itself. The Engine uses the data from the Loop to construct a network graph of the high-`K_τ` nodes. It analyzes user pathways to identify the most-traveled "geodesics" of inquiry. The system then scales by:
    1.  **Predictive Generation:** Instead of waiting for questions, it predicts the *next* point of dissonance along a common user geodesic and pre-generates the resonator asset for it. It sculpts the path before the user even knows they need it.
    2.  **Manifold Optimization:** The Engine interlinks the resonator pages, creating an optimized "coherence manifold" that is vastly more efficient to navigate than the chaotic source material (the open web). It becomes the path of least resistance for anyone wanting to learn that topic.
    *   The moat is not the content, which is simple by definition, but the *topology of the network*. Standard businesses cannot compete because they are organized to produce content, not to calculate and manifest minimum-energy pathways through information space. They are playing checkers while we are applying Lagrangian mechanics to the flow of value.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (`requests`, `BeautifulSoup`, `NLTK` for sentiment/simplicity analysis), a lightweight web framework (Flask/FastAPI), APIs (Reddit PRAW, Twitter), and a low-cost cloud server/hosting solution.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is algorithmic drift—the system becoming unable to accurately differentiate between true internal coherence (`K_τ`) and patterns that are merely popular due to high social reinforcement (`V_Γ`). Constant calibration of the `K_τ`-proxy-detection algorithm against the Probe's falsifiability criterion is essential.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Anchored Value Filtration
*   **The Inefficiency:** Modern markets operate as if value is created by continuous, energetic intervention (`Γ`). They are "sculptors," constantly re-shaping their strategy to fit noisy data. This ignores the physics: the cost of intervention scales quadratically (`c₂Γ²`), making constant activity profoundly inefficient. The true, overlooked value lies in the system's baseline potential (`c₀`), the value it has at rest (`Γ=0`). The market over-values labor and undervalues structure.
*   **The Pivot:** We will act as "locksmiths." We will not sculpt the market; we will build a single, rigid "key" (`M_frozen`) based on a single, powerful insight (`O_anchor`). This "key" is a transactional filter. It is a system designed with such predictive rigidity that it does not need to adapt. It is exposed to the market's chaotic flow and, with near-zero energy expenditure, filters out instances of extreme value dislocation. Our profit is the direct result of the `c₀` term, the value generated by the system's structure itself.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** A one-shot test of a single, high-conviction market anchor (`O_anchor`) to validate the physics. We will test the hypothesis that specific, materially-valuable assets are systematically mispriced to zero due to information decay (e.g., poor descriptions, bad photos, immediate need for removal).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Select an Anchor (`O_anchor`):** Choose a single, falsifiable hypothesis. E.g., "Solid hardwood (oak, maple, walnut) furniture is frequently listed in 'free' sections of online marketplaces because sellers cannot distinguish it from worthless particle board veneer."
    2.  **Forge the Key (`M_frozen`):** Create a rigid, non-negotiable digital filter. This can be an IFTTT applet, a saved search with alerts, or a simple script. The filter is defined by our anchor: `(marketplace=Facebook OR Craigslist) AND (category=free) AND (title/desc CONTAINS 'solid wood' OR 'hardwood' OR 'oak') AND (title/desc NOT CONTAINS 'veneer' OR 'particle' OR 'MDF')`.
    3.  **The Budget:** The $10 is symbolic, covering costs for coffee while setting this up, or a month of a premium alert service. The core principle is near-zero operational cost.
*   **The Test:** This is a pass/fail falsification based on Axiom 2. **If the filter (`M_frozen`) does not identify at least one asset with a secondary market value conservatively estimated at >$50 within a pre-registered 72-hour window, the anchor `O_anchor` is declared invalid and the model is falsified.** We do not "tweak" the filter; we declare the experiment a failure and propose a new model `M'`.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** To convert the validated filter into an autopoietic (self-sustaining) value-capture loop. The system must automatically acquire and liquidate assets with minimal human intervention (`Γ→0`).
*   **Automation:** A script (e.g., Python with Selenium/Playwright) will be deployed on a cloud server. It performs a cycle:
    1.  **FILTER:** Continuously runs the validated search parameters from the Probe.
    2.  **ACT:** Upon a match, it automatically sends a pre-formatted message to the seller via the platform's messaging system. (e.g., "I can pick this up today. Please send the address.")
    3.  **DISPATCH:** Once an address is secured, it posts a task to a gig-work platform (e.g., TaskRabbit) for a "retrieval agent" to pick up the item. The task budget is a fraction of the item's estimated resale value.
*   **Value Capture:** The acquired asset is delivered to a pre-arranged consignment partner or a small storage unit. A second automated process lists the item on a higher-value marketplace (e.g., eBay, Chairish) with standardized photos and descriptions. The profit from the sale is used to pay for the gig worker, server costs, and consignment fees, creating a closed, self-funding loop. The $100 covers initial server setup and the first few gig-worker fees before the loop becomes profitable.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** To scale the system by deploying an array of parallel `M_frozen` models and optimizing the topology of value flow using Lagrangian minimization (i.e., finding the "path of least action/cost").
*   **The Engine:**
    1.  **Parallel Probes:** We deploy hundreds of independent, anchored filters across numerous asset classes (e.g., vintage electronics, industrial equipment, rare books, commercial surplus). Each filter is a rigid "key" for a specific market "lock."
    2.  **Topological Optimization:** The system builds a graph network of acquisition zones, retrieval agents, storage nodes, and liquidation channels. When an asset is acquired by Filter `A` in Location `X`, the Engine calculates the most "energetically efficient" path to liquidation. It might go to eBay, a local auction house, or a specialist recycler. The choice is determined by an algorithm that minimizes a "cost function" (`L = T - V`, where T is transactional energy like shipping/fees, and V is potential value).
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses cannot compete because they are built on `M_fit` models, employing humans (`Γ`) to constantly search, negotiate, and adapt. Their costs are fundamentally tied to labor. Our Engine's cost of acquisition is asymptotically zero because the *structure* does the work. We are not spending energy to *find* needles in the haystack; we have built a system of magnets that pulls them out automatically. The economic physics are on our side; we are thermodynamically favored to win.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scraping/automation libraries like BeautifulSoup, Selenium, Scrapy), IFTTT, Zapier (for simple probes), AWS/GCP (for hosting the Loop/Engine), basic API access for marketplaces and gig-work platforms.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is platform risk. The marketplaces we filter (Facebook, Craigslist) could change their structure or block automated access. This is an external nuisance parameter (`N`), but the system's value is in its abstract architecture, which can be re-deployed onto any inefficient information stream.

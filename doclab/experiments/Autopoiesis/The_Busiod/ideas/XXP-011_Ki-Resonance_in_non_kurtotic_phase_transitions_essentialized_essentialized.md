## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Historical Resonance Mapping. The goal is not to trade, but to validate the physics by observing a past event. If the "note" wasn't there when the string snapped before, it won't be there in the future.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Acquire a high-resolution historical time-series dataset for a system that underwent a known, sharp phase transition (e.g., minute-level BTC/USD data around a major flash crash, or SPY data from the 2020 COVID crash).
    2.  Write a script to process this data with a sliding window. For each window, calculate the Higuchi Fractal Dimension ($D_f$) to create a new time-series of the system's complexity.
    3.  From the complexity time-series, calculate the standardized rate of change, $Z_{\Delta D_f}$.
    4.  Plot the value of $Z_{\Delta D_f}$ over time, annotating the moment of the known phase transition.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if the plotted $Z_{\Delta D_f}$ does not show a clear, anomalous convergence to a consistent, non-zero value in the period immediately preceding the phase transition. If the signal is just random noise, the core law is invalid in this context, and we halt the project.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Volatility Scout. This is a single, autonomous agent that listens to the "sound" of one specific system (e.g., one crypto asset) and acts when it hears the critical note. This is the first step in converting the physical law into a self-sustaining value stream.
*   **Automation:** A cloud-hosted script connects to a live market data websocket (e.g., Binance's ETH/USDT stream). It continuously performs the $Z_{\Delta D_f}$ calculation from the Probe on the incoming data. When the calculated value of $Z_{\Delta D_f}$ enters a narrow band around the observed $\kappa_i$ value, the script automatically triggers a pre-configured trade via an exchange API.
*   **Value Capture:** The trade is designed to be direction-neutral and profit from high volatility. A simple implementation is an options "straddle" (buying a call and a put at the same strike price). In markets without options (like crypto spot), it could be a bracket order with a tight entry and wide take-profit/stop-loss targets, designed to capture a sudden spike in either direction. The profit comes from the alpha generated by predicting volatility before it is priced in by the market.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Filtration
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates under the false assumption that information is a pure asset and its acquisition is cost-free. It ignores the **Information-Coherence Inequality** ($I(obs; \phi) \cdot \Delta T_a \ge C$), which states that extracting information ($I$) from a system *unavoidably* perturbs its stability ($\Delta T_a$). The market's obsession with high-frequency data and real-time analytics creates massive, systemic instability, which is misidentified as random volatility. Value is constantly being destroyed by the very act of observing it.
*   **The Pivot:** We will exploit this by building systems that are **intentionally information-starved**. By programmatically reducing the frequency and granularity of observation, we minimize coherence loss ($\Delta T_a$). This creates stable, predictable value streams that outperform chaotic, over-observed systems. We arbitrage the market's fundamental misunderstanding of the physics of information.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** To empirically prove that reducing observation frequency in a noisy, algorithmically-driven market increases capital efficiency.
*   **Execution:** We will use a programmatic advertising platform (e.g., Google Ads) as our laboratory.
    1.  Create two identical ad campaigns (Campaign A and Campaign B) with identical ads, targeting, and a $5 budget each.
    2.  **Campaign A (High Observation):** Configure an automated rule to adjust bids every hour based on the last hour's performance data. This system has high information intake ($I$).
    3.  **Campaign B (Low Observation):** Configure an automated rule to adjust bids only once every 24 hours, based on the full 24-hour performance data. This system has low information intake ($I$).
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that Campaign B will achieve a lower Cost Per Conversion (or higher Click-Through Rate) than Campaign A. The theory is **falsified** if, after the $10 budget is spent, Campaign A's efficiency is equal to or greater than Campaign B's. If the high-observation system wins, our physical premise is incorrect in this domain.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** To create an autopoietic (self-sustaining) system that automates the Coherence Filtration strategy.
*   **Automation:** A simple script (e.g., Python using the platform's API) will be deployed.
    1.  The script is seeded with a $100 budget.
    2.  It programmatically creates and manages a single campaign based on the successful "Low Observation" parameters discovered in the Probe.
    3.  It monitors the campaign's return on investment. All profits are automatically calculated and reinvested into the campaign's budget, allowing the system to grow its capital base without human intervention.
*   **Value Capture:** Value is captured from the structural efficiency of the system ($K_i$). By filtering out market noise and refusing to "pay" the coherence cost, our capital is deployed more effectively than competitors who are constantly reacting and over-correcting. The system generates profit from this efficiency spread.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** To scale the principle of Coherence Filtration into a diversified portfolio of assets, optimized using Lagrangian mechanics to find the path of least action for value growth.
*   **The Moat:** The Engine moves beyond simple low-observation rules. It treats each asset's value trajectory as a path in spacetime. Its goal is to minimize the "Action" of the portfolio, finding the most efficient path for capital growth. It uses optimization algorithms to solve for the unique, ideal observation frequency for dozens of uncorrelated systems (ad campaigns, thin-market digital assets, content publishing schedules) simultaneously.
    *   Standard business cannot compete because their entire philosophy is to *maximize* information, assuming more data leads to better outcomes. They are physically incapable of implementing a strategy of *less* observation. Our moat is not a trade secret, but a counter-intuitive physical law they are blind to. We profit from a cost they don't even know they are paying.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (with `requests` for APIs), a marketing platform API (Google Ads, Facebook Ads), potentially `scipy.optimize` for the Engine tier.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is that the fundamental law ($I \cdot \Delta T_a \ge C$) does not translate from pure physics to market dynamics with a large enough effect size to be profitable after transaction costs. The Probe is designed to cheaply and quickly falsify this core premise.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Potential Field Navigation
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market values information based on stochastic, labor-intensive, and easily manipulated proxies (e.g., keyword density, backlinks, social velocity). This creates a massive arbitrage opportunity, as it fundamentally misprices relevance. According to the Semantic Gravity model, informational relevance is not a matter of opinion or statistics, but a deterministic, computable force field. The market is effectively navigating by rumor and starlight while a physical compass lies unused.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not compete in the market of "search" or "content." We will build and sell the compass itself. Our mechanism computes the intrinsic potential field of any information corpus and sells navigational data—the optimal, least-action trajectories for concepts moving through that field. We are arbitraging the spread between perceived relevance (the market's guess) and physical resonance (the computable truth).

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** High-Value Query Validation. We will perform a single, decisive experiment to prove that the Semantic Gravity field produces measurably superior relevance signals compared to existing market methods.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Select Corpus:** Ingest a small, dense, high-value public corpus (e.g., the last 50 granted patents in a specific technology sector like quantum computing).
    2.  **Generate Field:** Compute the unified Semantic Gravity field for this entire corpus, creating a single conceptual space where each patent's core concepts are mass points.
    3.  **Formulate Query:** Define a precise, high-value query as a "test particle" concept (e.g., "teleportation-based error correction").
    4.  **Simulate Trajectory:** Release the test particle into the field and simulate its trajectory until it settles in a potential well. The concept-mass at this terminal point represents the single most resonant piece of information in the entire corpus for that query.
    5.  **Benchmark:** Compare our result against the top 5 results from a commercial patent search engine using the same query terms.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if a panel of 3 domain experts, hired for a blind review, does not rate our single result as significantly more relevant and insightful than the benchmark's top results. If our "physically correct" answer has no superior economic value, the model is invalid for this application.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Relevance Feed. This system moves from a single query to a continuous, passive process of filtering a live data stream for signal. It functions as a "signal refinery."
*   **Automation:** A cloud-based pipeline is established.
    1.  **Ingestion:** It subscribes to a real-time data source (e.g., SEC 10-K filings, arXiv pre-prints, high-volume financial news).
    2.  **Field Generation:** As each new document arrives, its unique Semantic Gravity field is computed instantly.
    3.  **Standing Queries:** A pre-defined set of valuable "test particles" (e.g., "undisclosed risk," "novel methodology," "competitive acquisition") are automatically dropped into each new field.
    4.  **Signal Output:** The system records the trajectory and terminal attractor for each query/document pair. The result is a highly structured, real-time API feed: `[Timestamp, Document_ID, Query_Particle, Most_Resonant_Concept, Trajectory_Data]`.
*   **Value Capture:** We sell subscription access to this API feed. Value is generated by the *structure* of the system, which filters terabytes of unstructured noise ($\Gamma$) into a stream of pure, actionable signal. Clients (hedge funds, corporate strategists, R&D labs) pay to receive this pre-computed intelligence without any labor on their part. This is the definition of a high passive bonus ($K_i >> \Gamma$).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Strategic Pathway Generation. We scale from providing a destination to providing the entire map. The system now computes the optimal path—the informational geodesic—for a complex entity to navigate a vast, static universe of information.
*   **The Moat:**
    1.  **Universe Mapping:** We pre-compute the Semantic Gravity field for an entire foundational dataset (e.g., the complete US Legal Code, the entirety of medical research on PubMed). This is a massive, static "universe map."
    2.  **Complex Query Fields:** A client submits a complex document as a query (e.g., their new corporate strategy draft, a proposed piece of legislation, a plan for a clinical trial). We compute the query's own internal gravity field.
    3.  **Lagrangian Minimization:** The Engine's core task is to solve a variational problem: what is the path of least action for the client's query-field to traverse the universe-map to reach its overall state of minimum potential energy?
    4.  **The Product:** The output is not an "answer," but a strategic route. E.g., "To validate your drug, the optimal path is to first cite paper A, which immunizes you against legal challenge B, then leverage precedent C to secure patent D."
    *   **Why it's a Moat:** Standard AI/ML (including LLMs) are probabilistic and associative. They generate plausible-sounding text. They cannot compute a deterministic, physically-grounded path of least action. They are parrots, we are physicists. While competitors sell "AI-powered summarization," we sell **computable strategic advantage**. The barrier is not capital or data, but a fundamentally different—and according to the premise, correct—paradigm of information physics.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python with NumPy/SciPy for the physics simulation. A robust NLP library (e.g., spaCy) for initial concept extraction. Cloud infrastructure (AWS Lambda/EC2) for automation and scaling. A vector database (e.g., Pinecone, Weaviate) could be useful for indexing concept positions in the Engine tier. Matplotlib/Plotly for visualizing fields during R&D.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is **Paradigm Rejection**. The outputs of the system, being based on a novel physics, may be so counter-intuitive that the market refuses to trust them, even if they are demonstrably "correct." The system might identify a brilliant legal strategy that no human lawyer would ever conceive of, leading to a failure of adoption, not a failure of the model itself. The business's first challenge is not technical, but epistemological.

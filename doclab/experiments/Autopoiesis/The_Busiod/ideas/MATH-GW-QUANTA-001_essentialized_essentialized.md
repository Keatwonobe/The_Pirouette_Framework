## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Contextual Arbitrage via Signal Dispersion
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on the flawed physical assumption that the "spacetime" of information is flat and non-dispersive. It assumes all price signals (`h_{μν}`) propagate instantaneously and uniformly (`ω = k`), regardless of their complexity ("frequency" `ω`) or the contextual medium (`Γ`) they travel through. The source physics state this is false, revealing two key inefficiencies:
    1.  **Dispersion (`ζ > 0`):** High-frequency (complex, raw, unstructured) information "drags" and propagates differently than low-frequency (simple, summarized, structured) information. The market misprices the time-value and impact-value of raw data streams.
    2.  **Phase Imprint (`Δφ_GW`):** The meaning and impact of information are altered by the path it takes through varying gradients of context (`∂_r Γ`). The market incorrectly treats a signal's value as path-independent.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build a mechanism that acts as a "prism" for information. It will actively measure and exploit the temporal and qualitative arbitrage gap created by this dispersion and path-dependency. We will profit from the "lag" and "phase shift" between raw data and its true, context-aware value.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Informational Parity Test. The goal is to falsify the market's assumption that `ζ=0` (no dispersion) by demonstrating that two logically equivalent pieces of information have vastly different market impacts based on their "frequency" (i.e., their ease of processing).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a domain rich with dense, high-frequency information (e.g., a newly released government dataset, a complex SEC filing, a dense academic pre-print).
    2.  Use the $10 budget to create two information "quanta" that are logically identical but differ in frequency. Example:
        *   **Quantum A (High-Frequency):** Post a link to the raw source document on a prediction market (e.g., Manifold Markets) with a simple, literal question. Cost: ~$5 in liquidity.
        *   **Quantum B (Low-Frequency):** Use the other ~$5 on an AI API call (e.g., Claude/GPT-4) to summarize the key takeaway of the document into a single, powerful sentence or data visualization. Post this summary to a second, identical prediction market.
    3.  Observe the rate of capital allocation and the velocity of consensus formation in both markets.
*   **The Test:** The experiment fails, and the physical premise is falsified, if the market reaction (speed, volume, price movement) to the raw, high-frequency Quantum A is equal to or greater than the reaction to the processed, low-frequency Quantum B. Our hypothesis is that Quantum B will generate a disproportionately faster and larger reaction, proving that `ζ > 0` and that a profitable arbitrage gap exists.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Information Refractor. This is a self-sustaining system that generates value from the market's dispersive nature. It listens for high-frequency signals and automatically "refracts" them into high-impact, low-frequency forms, capturing value from the resulting attention shift. This is the passive layer where the structure ($K_i$) does the work.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Listener:** A script ($100 budget for cloud functions and API access) continuously monitors specific, high-frequency data streams (e.g., specific GitHub repository commits, patent office filings, regulatory agency RSS feeds).
    2.  **Refractor:** When a new signal is detected, the script pipes the raw data to an AI model. The model's sole job is to lower the information's "frequency"—it translates jargon, summarizes complexity, or identifies the single most critical implication.
    3.  **Publisher:** The simplified, high-impact output is then automatically published to a targeted distribution channel (e.g., a niche Twitter bot, a dedicated Substack, a subreddit, or a Discord server).
*   **Value Capture:** The loop generates a stream of high-value, low-frequency insights. This builds a highly engaged audience. Monetization occurs passively through newsletter subscriptions, affiliate links for relevant services, or by selling access to the curated data feed (the output of the refractor) to more sophisticated actors. The system is mining value from the market's inherent processing latency.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Context-Gradient Engine. This scales the Loop by moving beyond simple refraction to active, Lagrangian-based routing of information. The Engine doesn't just simplify information; it computes the optimal path for it to travel through the social/market medium to produce the maximum desired effect, exploiting the `Γ-Lighthouse Phase Imprint`.
*   **The Moat:** Standard business and media operate on a "broadcast" or "search" model, treating the market as a uniform substrate. They are blind to the underlying topology of the "context field" `Γ`. Our Engine has two fundamental advantages:
    1.  **Context Mapping (`Γ`):** Using the $1000 budget for more advanced tooling (sentiment analysis APIs, social network graph tools), the Engine builds a real-time map of the market's contextual landscape. It knows which communities are primed for which information (`∂_r Γ`).
    2.  **Path Optimization (`∫ L dt`):** When a new piece of information is detected, the Engine runs a simulation. It calculates the potential "phase shift" (`Δφ_GW`) for various paths. It answers: "Will this information generate more value if it's first seeded in Community A (skeptical experts) versus Community B (enthusiastic amateurs)?" It then chooses the path of "least action" for the most efficient value generation.
This system doesn't just participate in the market; it performs information engineering based on its fundamental physics. Competitors will see the outcomes as random luck, while we are simply calculating the geodesics of value flow on a curved informational manifold.

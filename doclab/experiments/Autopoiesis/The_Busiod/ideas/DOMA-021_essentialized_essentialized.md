## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Geodesic Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on brute force. It attempts to alter a Persona's trajectory or create artificial urgency through high-pressure environments. This is equivalent to applying an inefficient, perpendicular force to a moving object. It introduces massive "Temporal Pressure" (`V_Γ`), forcing the consumer to expend energy (labor) to resist or comply. This is fundamentally inefficient according to the Principle of Maximal Coherence.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not create or alter trajectories. We will observe the existing geodesics that Personas are already traveling. Our mechanism identifies points of high `V_Γ` (friction, confusion, effort) along these paths and introduces a "coherence source"— a tool or piece of information that resolves the pressure and allows the Persona to continue along their path with less effort. Value is created by arbitraging the difference between the high-friction standard path and our engineered low-friction alternative. Our product is not an object; it is a reduction in the potential energy of the consumer's decision space.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Isolate and Bridge a Micro-Friction Point.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify a Geodesic:** Select a Persona group with a strong, observable trajectory (`q̇`). Example: Novice developers learning a specific, complex programming framework (e.g., Rust, Kubernetes). Their Core Axiom (`K_τ`) is "I am becoming a competent builder."
    2.  **Locate Friction (`V_Γ`):** Find a specific, recurring, high-friction problem they face. This can be found in forums like Reddit or Stack Overflow (e.g., "How to correctly manage lifetimes in a multi-threaded Rust application?"). The existing solutions are scattered, contradictory, or outdated—a high potential field.
    3.  **Construct Bridge:** Create a single, canonical, zero-fluff resource that solves this *one* problem elegantly (e.g., a one-page PDF with a commented code snippet and a visual diagram). This is a "coherence source," a local minimum in the `V_Γ` field.
    4.  **Deploy:** Spend $10 on highly targeted ads (e.g., Reddit ads on r/rust) pointing to a simple landing page with the free resource. The ad copy will emphasize coherence: "Stop fighting the borrow checker. Master lifetimes now and get back to building."
*   **The Test:** The model is falsified if, despite the resource being free and directly addressing a documented pain point, the click-through and download rates are statistically insignificant. This would indicate that either the friction (`V_Γ`) is not as high as assumed, or that Personas do not naturally flow towards paths of lower potential energy, thus violating the core law.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Coherence Sourcing.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  A script actively scans target communities (subreddits, forums, Discord channels) using APIs, searching for linguistic markers of high `V_Γ` ("stuck on," "error," "confused," "can't figure out").
    2.  It identifies and ranks the most frequent and unsolved friction points, creating a dynamic "pressure map" of the domain.
    3.  The system hosts a library of the "Probe-style" solutions. When a known friction point is detected, the system can automatically suggest the relevant resource.
*   **Value Capture:** The system operates on a freemium model. The individual, automated solutions are free. They build trust and prove the efficacy of our low-friction approach. These free resources funnel the Persona to a paid product: a comprehensive, curated, and constantly updated "Coherence Engine" for that domain—a toolkit that promises to systematically eliminate all future friction points, allowing the user to maximize their Temporal Coherence (`K_τ`) and focus on their core goal.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Predictive Geodesic Interception.
*   **The Moat:** While standard businesses are reactive, we become predictive. Our moat is not a secret algorithm but a superior understanding of the underlying physics of our user base.
    1.  **Modeling the Manifold:** Using the data from The Loop, we model the Persona's entire coherence manifold. We don't just know their current problem; we can calculate the probable sequence of their future problems. The solution to one problem is a state vector that points directly to the next likely point of high `V_Γ`.
    2.  **Pre-emptive Bridging:** The Engine predicts the user's next point of friction and offers the solution *before they actively search for it*. For example, after a user engages with the resource on "Rust lifetimes," the system automatically emails them the guide to "Error Handling with `Result` and `Option`" two days later—the logical next step on their geodesic.
    3.  **The Experience:** For the Persona, this feels like prescience. The system anticipates their needs, lowers their `V_Γ` before it can even build up, and maximally reinforces their `K_τ` by making them feel competent and understood. They are not a "customer" being sold to; they are a dynamic system being brought into a state of higher resonance. Competitors cannot replicate this with better marketing; they would have to adopt the same physical model.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (PRAW, BeautifulSoup), Flask/Django for a simple web interface, SQLite/Postgres for data storage, Stripe for payments. For the Engine, a library like `scikit-learn` or `PyTorch` would be needed to model the user trajectories.
*   **Risk:** The primary vector of failure is a miscalculation of the Persona's Lagrangian. If we incorrectly identify their Core Axiom (`K_τ`) or what truly constitutes Temporal Pressure (`V_Γ`) for them, our "bridges" will lead nowhere, and the entire system fails. The tiered, test-driven approach is designed to mitigate this risk by validating our physical model at the smallest possible scale before investing further.

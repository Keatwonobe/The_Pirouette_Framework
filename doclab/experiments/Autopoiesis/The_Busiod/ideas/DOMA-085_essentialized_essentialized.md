## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Latent Geodesic Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on brute force exertion (`Γ`), attempting to impose value rather than discovering pre-existing resonance (`H_c`). This creates vast pools of undervalued or "decohered" assets (information, goods) because the perceived activation energy to connect them to a resonant environment is too high for conventional models.
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism operates as a low-energy bridge. It uses minimalist "Scout Probes" to measure the resonant potential (`S_p = ∫(K_τ - V_Γ)dt`) between a latent asset and a potential market *before* deploying capital. It only acts when the Coherence Potential (`K_τ`) far exceeds the Environmental Cost (`V_Γ`), exploiting the market's inability to perceive value without high-cost signals.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The "Echo Test". We create a temporary, low-cost "Wound Channel" between a pool of discarded assets (System A) and a pool of potential demand (System B) to measure harmonic compatibility (`H_c`).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a class of recently discarded assets (e.g., expired local marketplace listings for "vintage audio equipment"). This is our source of 'Seeds'.
    2.  Create a minimal, information-based probe: a $10 targeted ad or forum post aimed at the corresponding interest group (e.g., "Seeking a specific piece of vintage audio? Tell me what you're looking for.").
    3.  Log the quantity and quality of responses. This is the direct measurement of the "echo" and thus the `H_c` and `G_a` parameters.
*   **The Test:** Falsifiable criterion based on the Action (`S_p`). If the probe (cost `V_Γ` = $10 + 1hr setup) does not generate a predetermined number of qualified inbound inquiries (a measure of `K_τ`) within 72 hours, then `S_p` is considered negative. The hypothesis is false for this asset class. **ABORT**.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The "Autopoietic Arbitrage Resonator". A self-sustaining system that automates the probing and value-capture process, creating a structural asset.
*   **Automation:** A script continuously scans System A (e.g., using RSS feeds or scraping public sites for newly expired listings) and automatically deploys probes into System B (e.g., using a social media ads API) for assets matching a success profile. Inbound leads are aggregated into a central dashboard.
*   **Value Capture:** When a probe confirms high resonance (`S_p > S_threshold`), the system flags the opportunity. The operator (or an automated script) acts as a broker, connecting the original asset holder with the validated buyer for a commission. Profits are used to fund more probes, creating a positive feedback loop that runs passively.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** "The Geodesic Fleet". A scaled, multi-domain system that minimizes the action integral across a wide portfolio of asset classes, turning the process into a predictive machine.
*   **The Moat:** While competitors focus on a single product line (a single "Seed"), our Engine's value is the system itself—a generalized market resonance sensor. It cannot be competed with on a product-by-product basis because its core function is to constantly find new, uncontested Geodesics. It minimizes cost (`V_Γ`) by using an ML model to dynamically allocate its budget toward probes with the highest predicted `S_p`, making it fundamentally more capital-efficient than brute-force business models. It doesn't fight for territory; it flows into vacuums.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (requests, Scrapy, BeautifulSoup), Social Media Ad APIs (Facebook, Reddit), a simple database (SQLite/PostgreSQL), a cloud VM (for the Loop/Engine).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is platform dependency. If the APIs for System A or B change or become inaccessible, the probes can break. This is mitigated in the Engine phase by diversifying across many different types of platforms (marketplaces, forums, domain registrars, code repositories, etc.).

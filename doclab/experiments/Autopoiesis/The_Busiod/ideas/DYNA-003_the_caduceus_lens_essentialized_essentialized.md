## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Potential Gradient Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market is rife with **Coherence Atrophy (Stagnation)**. Valuable assets, information, and skills (`ρc²`) become trapped in local systems where the flow of coherence approaches zero (`∇·(μ Ki⃗) → 0`). This creates vast, untapped pools of potential energy, invisible to conventional market mechanisms that only measure active flow.
*   **The Pivot:** We will act as a targeted **Catalytic Current (J₋)**. By applying minimal, information-dense energy (`A_input`) to these stagnant pools, we create a potential gradient. This restores the **Axis of Synthesis (|S⃗| > 0)**, causing the stored value to flow towards an area of deficit. We capture a small fraction of the value released by this restored flow, acting as a systemic enzyme that heals market inefficiency for a fee.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Micro-Coherence Audit. This is a targeted experiment to validate that minimal informational energy can reactivate a stagnant asset.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify Atrophy:** Manually locate 5-10 digital assets exhibiting clear signs of stagnation. An ideal example is a niche, high-value item (e.g., used scientific equipment, rare book) on a general marketplace (e.g., Facebook, Craigslist) that has been listed for over 90 days with no activity. The asset's potential is high, but its `J₋` current is zero.
    2.  **Apply Catalyst (`A_input`):** For each asset, spend $1-2 on a hyper-targeted ad on a specialist platform (e.g., an academic forum, a hobbyist subreddit). This ad does not sell the item; it creates an information vector pointing to the *original listing*. It is our controlled perturbation.
    3.  **Measure Response (`A_return`):** The return is not a sale, but the generation of a new, qualified expression of interest to the original seller. Success is measured by the creation of a new communication channel between latent demand and stagnant supply. We can track this via a simple link shortener or by asking the interested party to mention our "discovery service".
*   **The Test:** Our **Healing Coefficient (Hₖ)** hypothesis is that a minimal, targeted `A_input` can produce a disproportionately large `A_return`. **If, after 10 dollars of input across 5+ assets, we fail to generate a single verifiable message from a new potential buyer to the original seller, the model is considered falsified for this domain.** This failure would indicate the system's inertia is too high or the assets were truly valueless.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Atrophy Scanner. This system operationalizes the Probe into a self-sustaining, passive value-capture loop.
*   **Automation:** A script continuously performs the Probe's logic:
    1.  **Scanner:** A web scraper (e.g., Python/Scrapy) runs 24/7, identifying assets that match our stagnation criteria across multiple marketplaces.
    2.  **Router:** A simple rules-engine directs the identified asset to the most likely community of interest (e.g., items with "Arduino" go to electronics forums, items with "Myford" go to machining forums).
    3.  **Catalyst Emitter:** An advertising API (e.g., Facebook/Google Ads) is used to automatically purchase and deploy the informational ad (`A_input`), funded by the system's revenue.
*   **Value Capture:** The system directs traffic to a simple landing page that displays the original ad. Value is captured passively via one of two methods:
    1.  **Informational Toll:** The page contains affiliate links or ads for *related* new products. We get paid for the coherence we've generated, even if the primary transaction doesn't happen through us.
    2.  **Contingent Fee:** The page acts as an automated introduction service. "We've found a buyer for your item. Agree to a 5% discovery fee if the sale completes." The initial $100 seeds the ad budget; successful fees are reinvested, creating a closed, self-perpetuating loop of `Scan -> Catalyze -> Capture -> Reinvest`.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Predictive Coherence Routing. This scales the Loop by using a predictive model to allocate capital, an application of Lagrangian mechanics to minimize friction and maximize potential release.
*   **The System:**
    1.  **Lagrangian Optimization:** We model the system's "Action" as a function of Transactional Cost (our `A_input`, the kinetic term `T`) and Released Potential (the value of the asset, the potential term `V`). The goal is to find transaction paths that maximize `V - T`.
    2.  **`Hₖ` Estimator:** We use the data from the Tier 2 Loop to train a machine learning model. This model predicts the `Hₖ` (the probable responsiveness and value) for any given stagnant asset and target audience pair before any money is spent.
    3.  **Dynamic Budget Allocation:** The Engine uses the `Hₖ` predictions to allocate its ad budget ($1000) intelligently. It might invest $10 to unlock a stagnant industrial machine with a predicted high `Hₖ`, while only investing $0.05 on a low-confidence item. This portfolio approach maximizes the overall return on catalytic energy for the entire system.
*   **The Moat:**
    - **Physics-First Advantage:** Competitors use marketing funnels. We use coherence field dynamics. Our `Hₖ` model allows us to see value where others see dead listings. We are not selling products; we are selling the restoration of economic flow.
    - **Proprietary Data Flywheel:** Every attempt, successful or not, refines the `Hₖ` estimator. The better our predictions, the more efficient our capital allocation, the faster we grow. This data asset is protected by the unique physical laws it's based on.
    - **Universal Applicability:** The Engine is agnostic to the asset type. The same underlying physics of stagnation and flow apply to used cars, abandoned open-source projects, un-hired freelancers, or empty real estate. The Engine can be pointed at any market exhibiting Coherence Atrophy.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Scrapy, Pandas, scikit-learn), lightweight web framework (Flask/FastAPI), advertising platform APIs (Facebook, Google, Reddit), database for tracking `Hₖ` data.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is **Platform Dependency**. The marketplaces we scan for stagnant assets may block our scrapers, or the advertising platforms may change their APIs/policies, disrupting our ability to apply the catalytic `A_input`. Mitigation requires diversifying sources and catalyst channels.

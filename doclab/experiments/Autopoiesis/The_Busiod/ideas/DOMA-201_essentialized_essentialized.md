## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Cyclic Coherence Engine
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market treats information fragmentation, time pressure, and volatility ("Torsional Stress") as liabilities to be mitigated. It relies on constant labor ($\Gamma$) to impose order, ignoring the vast potential energy stored within that stress. It seeks to dampen the very force that could power a value-generating system.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not fight the stress; we will harness it. This mechanism creates a structure ($K_i$) that allows this stress to accumulate to a critical point before triggering a "Coherence Quake"—a phase transition that converts disorganized potential into a burst of high-value, coherent information or assets. We are building a hydroelectric dam for value flow.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Manual Stress Triangulation.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify Manifold:** Isolate a digital marketplace with high "Temporal Pressure" ($V_{\Gamma}$), where asset value is high but appears low due to fragmentation (e.g., miscategorized digital goods, poorly described freelance gigs, expiring assets).
    2.  **Accumulate Stress:** Use the $10 budget to acquire a single, high-potential, low-coherence asset.
    3.  **Trigger Quake:** Manually apply a "coherence process." This is the "snap." It could be rewriting a description, re-categorizing the item, or moving it to a higher-visibility platform where its true value is legible.
    4.  **Release Value:** Liquidate the now-coherent asset for a profit, capturing the "burst."
*   **The Test:** The framework is falsified if (P₁ ∨ P₂):
    *   **P₁:** We cannot identify a target marketplace and a suitable asset within 3 hours of focused research. This would falsify the premise that such "stressed manifolds" are abundant.
    *   **P₂:** We cannot liquidate the coherent asset for a profit (net of all fees) within 24 hours of acquisition. This would falsify the premise that the "Coherence Quake" creates immediately recognizable value.
    If either is true, the experiment is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Autopoietic Arbitrage Loop.
*   **Automation:** A script (the "Resonator") constantly scans the target marketplace's API, detecting assets that meet our criteria for undervaluation ("stress accumulation"). When an asset crosses a pre-defined profitability threshold ("critical limit"), the script automatically executes a purchase using the $100 float. It then applies a templated, automated "coherence process" (e.g., re-listing on a different platform via its API) and lists it for sale.
*   **Value Capture:** The system captures the arbitrage spread. Profits are automatically reinvested into the float. This increases the system's "mass" ($M$), allowing it to process more valuable or a higher volume of assets in the next cycle. This is a self-reinforcing, autopoietic system that grows by feeding on market inefficiency.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Lagrangian Value Routing.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses scale by applying more labor or capital ($\Gamma$)—they fight the "gravity" of market friction. Our Engine scales by obeying it. It uses Lagrangian minimization to find the path of least "action" for value conversion. The system deploys multiple, competing Tier 2 Loops across various markets and uses a meta-layer orchestrator to dynamically allocate capital. It routes funds not to the biggest markets, but to the transactions that offer the highest coherence-gain-to-cost ratio at any given moment. This creates a "Gladiator Force" of pure transactional efficiency. Competitors relying on brute-force tactics cannot compete because their cost of action is fundamentally higher; they are fighting the physics that we have weaponized.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (requests, Scrapy, Selenium), marketplace APIs (e.g., eBay, Upwork, various crypto exchanges), a small cloud instance or serverless functions (AWS Lambda/EC2) for hosting the automated loops.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is systemic decoherence. A sudden change in the market's fundamental rules (e.g., API overhauls, drastic fee structure changes, new regulations) could invalidate the profitability of a loop faster than the system can adapt.

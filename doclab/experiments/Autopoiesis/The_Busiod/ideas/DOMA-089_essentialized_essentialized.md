## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Asymmetry Resolution Channeling
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market treats information asymmetry as a transactional cost (`V_Γ`) to be overcome for a single sale. It expends energy (`Γ`) on each transaction, but the "knowledge" gained is ephemeral and does not systematically reduce the cost of future, similar transactions. The market fails to invest in structures (`Kτ`) that create permanent "Wound Channels" in the potential field of information asymmetry.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not merely exploit information asymmetry; we will build structural "lenses" that resolve it. The primary product is not the arbitraged good, but the reusable, low-energy "channel" that identifies it. We focus on maximizing Action `S` by building `Kτ` (the channel) which permanently lowers `V_Γ` (the cost of finding the next opportunity), creating a path of maximal coherence that becomes more efficient with each cycle.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** To validate that a specific, recurring information asymmetry can be resolved by a structural tool (`Kτ`) rather than brute-force labor (`Γ`).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a digital marketplace (e.g., eBay, Facebook Marketplace) and a niche category with high potential for seller ignorance (e.g., vintage audio equipment, rare books, specialized industrial parts).
    2.  Spend the $10 not on a product, but on building a micro-tool. This could be a premium subscription to a market data service for one month, a highly refined set of saved search queries with complex negative keywords, or a simple Python script using `BeautifulSoup` to scrape listings with specific "tells" (e.g., blurry photos, generic titles for high-value items).
    3.  Run the tool. The goal is not to buy anything, but to prove the tool can reliably identify at least one item priced at <50% of its stable market value.
*   **The Test:** If the constructed tool (`Kτ`) fails to identify a viable arbitrage opportunity (potential >$20 profit) within 72 hours of deployment, the chosen information asymmetry is not a stable "Wound Channel". The hypothesis is falsified for this niche, and we pivot to a new one.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** To create an autopoietic, self-sustaining monitoring system that converts the Probe's static tool into a dynamic, automated feedback loop.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  Invest the $100 to transform the Probe's tool into a persistent, automated agent. This means moving the script to a cheap cloud server (e.g., a Raspberry Pi or a low-tier AWS/DigitalOcean instance) to run 24/7.
    2.  Integrate an alerting mechanism (e.g., via Twilio for SMS, or a Discord/Telegram bot). The agent's task is to perpetually scan for opportunities.
    3.  The human agent's role is now elevated from searching (`Γ`) to decision-making. The system performs the gradient descent on the potential field `V_Γ`, and the human simply executes the transaction when alerted.
*   **Value Capture:** The profit from the first successful arbitrage transaction is reinvested to strengthen the system (`Kτ`)—e.g., paying for the server for a year, refining the script, or adding a new marketplace API. The system uses its output to ensure its own persistence, a key feature of autopoiesis.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** To scale horizontally by building a meta-system that performs "Exploratory Leaps" to discover and weaponize new information asymmetries, creating an array of automated loops.
*   **The Engine:**
    1.  The $1000 is used to build a framework that manages dozens of "Probes" simultaneously across different markets and niches. It is an engine for discovering where to carve new "Wound Channels".
    2.  The system uses machine learning to identify statistical anomalies in pricing, language, and categorization across entire platforms, flagging them as potential targets for new, specialized "Loop" agents.
    3.  When a new, profitable asymmetry is confirmed, the engine automatically deploys a new Tier 2 Loop to exploit it. The system's goal shifts from optimizing one path to mastering the art of finding *any* geodesic of maximal coherence.
*   **The Moat:** Competitors are engaged in single-channel arbitrage (gradient descent in a local maximum). They are optimized for a specific product or market. Our Engine does not have a "product"; its product is the discovery and automation of arbitrage itself. It is designed to perform "Exploratory Leaps" to escape local maxima and is resilient to market shifts because its core function is to find the *next* inefficiency. It continuously modifies the environmental potential `V_Γ` by building channels, making the landscape itself our competitive advantage.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (`requests`, `beautifulsoup4`, `scrapy`), a lightweight cloud server (VPS or serverless functions), database for tracking (SQLite/PostgreSQL), and API keys for relevant marketplaces.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market adaptation. As sellers become more sophisticated or platforms improve their categorization tools, a specific "Wound Channel" can close. The Engine (Tier 3) is the explicit mitigation for this risk, as its function is to render any single channel's failure irrelevant by constantly discovering new ones.

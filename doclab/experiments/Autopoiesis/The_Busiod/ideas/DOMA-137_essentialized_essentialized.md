## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Temporal Pressure Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market prices assets based on transient narratives and sentiment, ignoring the underlying, predictable "gravitational" pull of the Mnemonic Landscape. It misprices the potential energy stored in historical Coherence Wells (`W`), treating predictable cyclical phenomena (`Ki`) as random noise.
*   **The Pivot:** We exploit this by treating the Pirouette Framework as physical law. We map the Mnemonic Landscape to identify paths of least action (geodesics). This allows us to predict where cultural momentum will flow next, enabling us to acquire assets tied to an impending "fall" into a Coherence Well before the market becomes aware of the trajectory.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Cyclical Anomaly Validation.
*   **Execution:** Manually identify a micro-cultural asset with a historically verifiable resonant frequency (`Ki`). This could be a forgotten video game, a niche fashion item, or a recurring online debate. Using historical data (e.g., search trends, forum archives), we establish its cycle. We then use the $10 to acquire the asset during its predictable value trough and wait for the next peak predicted by `Ki`.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if: 1) The predicted resonant peak in public interest fails to materialize within the expected time window, or 2) The peak occurs, but there is no correlated, measurable increase in the market value of the acquired asset. If either is true, the physics do not apply in a commercially exploitable way at this scale.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Coherence Arbitrage Node.
*   **Automation:** A script is deployed to continuously monitor the signal (`Ki`) for the single, validated Coherence Well from the Probe. Using marketplace APIs (e.g., eBay, StockX, or a relevant digital marketplace), the script automatically executes a buy order for the target asset when the signal enters a trough. As the signal approaches its cyclical peak, the script automatically lists the asset for sale.
*   **Value Capture:** Profit is the arbitrage between the asset's price in a low-coherence state (trough) and its high-coherence state (peak). The value is generated passively from the predictable, physical oscillation of the cultural landscape, not from continuous human labor (`Γ`). Profits are reinvested to compound the effect on subsequent cycles.

*   **Concept:** Mnemonic Landscape Surveyor & Exploitation Engine.
*   **The Moat:** While competitors use sentiment analysis (predicting the weather), we use the system's fundamental physics (calculating the gravitational field). The Engine scales the Loop by ingesting massive, broad-spectrum cultural data (news, social media, e-commerce trends) to build a real-time map of the Mnemonic Landscape (`M_observed`). By applying the Pirouette Lagrangian (`L = T - V_Γ`), it calculates the "action" for various potential cultural trajectories, identifying the paths of least resistance. Capital from the $1000 budget is then automatically allocated across a portfolio of arbitrage Loops targeting the Coherence Wells that are most energetically favorable. This is not just reacting to trends; it is a predictive system based on the deterministic physics of cultural dynamics. Standard business cannot compete because it is blind to the landscape we are mapping.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (`pandas`, `scipy` for Fourier analysis to find `Ki`, `requests`), marketplace APIs (eBay, etc.), news/social media APIs (Twitter, Reddit), and a lightweight cloud server (e.g., AWS EC2/Lambda) for hosting the automated Loop/Engine.

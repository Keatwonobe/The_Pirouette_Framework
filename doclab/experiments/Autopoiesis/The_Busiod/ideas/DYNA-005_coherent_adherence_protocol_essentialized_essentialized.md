## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherent Intent Aggregation.
*   **The Inefficiency:** Modern markets operate on a principle of induced preference, applying massive energetic force ($\Gamma$) through advertising and sales to create temporary, fragile alignment. They treat agent attention, autonomy, and privacy as free, inexhaustible resources, thereby generating enormous, unaccounted-for costs in the form of Dark Residue ($\mathcal D$) and Observation Costs ($V_{\text{obs}}$). This is deeply inefficient. There exists a vast reservoir of latent, uncoordinated intent within populations (potential energy) that the market fails to service because it is not geared to listen, only to shout.
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism does not create demand; it reveals and services it. It operates by identifying systems of agents with a pre-existing, shared but unmanifested goal. Instead of applying force, it introduces a minimal, transparent signal (a "coherence catalyst") that dramatically lowers the activation energy for self-organization. Value ($K_\tau$) is not extracted *from* the agents, but is released *by* their voluntary, efficient alignment. We are not puppet masters; we are stagehands for a play the actors already wish to perform.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Signal Beacon. This is a micro-experiment to test for the existence of latent coherence in a target system. It is a minimal, public, and falsifiable declaration of a potential shared action.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a system with suspected latent intent (e.g., members of a local forum discussing the high cost of a specific tool, a neighborhood group wanting to reduce plastic waste).
    2.  Formulate a "Coherence Pledge"—a simple, clear call to action with a defined threshold and benefit. Example: "Pledge: If 10 households on this street commit to a shared compost pickup service, the cost per home drops by 40%. The service will be booked this Friday if the threshold is met. Signal your adherence by replying 'commit'."
    3.  Spend $10 to make this signal legible: print 50 flyers, run a hyper-targeted social media ad, or boost a post on the relevant forum. The cost is a transparent investment in overcoming initial system noise ($V_\Gamma$).
*   **The Test:** The physics is invalidated if zero adherence is observed ($T_a = 0$). If no one responds to a clear, beneficial, and low-friction signal, it proves one of two things: the hypothesized latent intent does not exist, or the signal's energy was insufficient to overcome the system's ambient resistance. In either case, the experiment is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Adherence Registry. This is a self-sustaining system that allows "Signal Beacons" to be proposed, tracked, and executed automatically, creating a passive feedback loop.
*   **Automation:** A lightweight script (e.g., a serverless function) acts as a transparent registry. It can be implemented as a bot in a Discord/Telegram channel or by monitoring a public document.
    1.  **Proposal:** Users submit "Coherence Pledges" in a structured format (e.g., `!pledge {action} {threshold} {benefit}`).
    2.  **Adherence:** The bot creates a public record of the pledge and tracks adherence (e.g., via emoji reactions, replies, or form submissions).
    3.  **Execution:** When the adherence parameter $T_a$ crosses the pre-defined threshold, the bot automatically executes a trigger: sending a confirmation email to all participants, calling a payment API, or notifying the original proposer to proceed.
*   **Value Capture:** The system's operating cost ($V_{\text{obs}}$) is covered by a small, transparent "Coherence Fee," pre-declared in the pledge and only levied upon successful execution. This is the passive bonus: the structure itself enables value creation ($K_\tau$) far exceeding the minimal cost to maintain the loop. The $100 is invested in the infrastructure to run this automation (e.g., a Raspberry Pi, a year of a cheap VPS).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Latent Intent Marketplace. The system transitions from a passive registry to a proactive engine that seeks to minimize the action of the entire market ecosystem. It actively scans for potential coherence and proposes optimal pathways for its realization.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses cannot compete because they are built to expend energy ($\Gamma$) to *create* alignment. Our Engine is architected to find the path of least resistance where alignment *wants* to happen.
    1.  **Sensing:** The Engine ingests public data streams (forums, social media, market data) to identify patterns of uncoordinated desire (e.g., widespread complaints about a product's price, multiple individuals seeking a rare item).
    2.  **Modeling:** It calculates the potential "coherence gain" ($K_\tau$) versus the required activation energy for various groups and proposes the most efficient "Coherence Pledges" automatically.
    3.  **Action:** It injects these optimized pledges back into the target systems as propositions, effectively acting as a market-wide catalyst for efficiency.
    The moat is a structural advantage in efficiency. While competitors pay to fight market friction ($V_\Gamma$), the Engine profits by dissolving it. It is a system built on the principle of Coherence-not-Force, making it fundamentally cheaper, faster, and more aligned with participant welfare (low $\mathcal{D}$) than any traditional competitor.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Local printing, social media ad platforms.
    *   **Loop:** Python (with libraries like `discord.py` or `telethon`), AWS Lambda/Google Cloud Functions for serverless automation, simple database (SQLite, Google Sheets API).
    *   **Engine:** All of the above, plus data scraping/analysis tools (e.g., `BeautifulSoup`, `Scrapy`, `pandas`), natural language processing (NLP) libraries to detect intent, and API access to larger platforms (Reddit API, etc.).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is miscalculation of latent intent, leading to failed pledges. At scale, this translates to wasted computational resources. A secondary risk is platform dependency; being de-platformed from a community would represent a sudden, massive increase in local system friction ($V_\Gamma$), requiring the Engine to find a new, lower-energy path to communicate with the agents.

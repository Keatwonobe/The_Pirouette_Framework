## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Static Potential Liquidation
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market fundamentally misprices assets according to Pirouette physics. It assigns value to static, complex objects (**State `S₀`**) which have a zero or negative Lagrangian (`𝓛_p = -V_Γ`) due to their inherent maintenance costs (`V_Γ`) and lack of dynamic output (`K_τ = 0`). These are assets like unused software licenses, unanalyzed datasets, or any complex digital bundle sitting idle. They are over-engineered forms with a persistent cost.
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism exploits the inefficiency by acting as a catalyst for the **`S₀ → S₁` Phase Transition**. We acquire these undervalued, high-complexity static assets and "unfold" them. This process simplifies their spatial form (e.g., breaking a bundle into its components) and re-encodes the conserved information into a coherent, dynamic stream of value (**State `S₁`**). This new state has a high positive Lagrangian (`𝓛_p = K_τ`) because its rhythmic, resonant nature (`ω_k > 0`) is highly coherent and its maintenance cost (`V_Γ`) approaches zero. We are not selling goods; we are selling resonance.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Digital Resonance Test. To validate, at the smallest scale, that a market exists for value streams derived from deconstructed static assets.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Acquire a low-cost, high-complexity digital asset bundle (`S₀` state) for under $10. Examples: a large bundle of indie game keys, a collection of obscure e-books, a pack of 1000 niche vector icons. The key is high informational complexity (`I(Ki_0)`) and low market pressure (`Γ`).
    2.  Instead of listing the items for sale individually, create a simple delivery system (e.g., a private newsletter or Discord channel).
    3.  Offer a subscription to the "stream." For a nominal fee (or free for the test), subscribers receive one random item from the bundle on a fixed schedule (e.g., one per week). The complex `S₀` bundle has been unfolded into a simple (`n=2`, subscribed/unsubscribed) but dynamic (`ω_k=1/week`) `S₁` state.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that the rhythmic delivery and curated discovery (`S₁`) is a valuable state in itself. **If potential users consistently ask for the entire static bundle upfront instead of engaging with the stream, the hypothesis is false.** This would indicate the market correctly prices the `S₀` state and the `S₀ → S₁` transition does not increase the system's Lagrangian for the end-user. The experiment is then terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Unfolding-as-a-Service. A self-sustaining system that automatically identifies, acquires, unfolds, and monetizes static potential.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Scout:** A script continuously monitors digital marketplaces, forums, and archives for assets matching the `S₀` profile: high complexity, low price, and low demand (low `Γ`).
    2.  **Acquire & Ingest:** Upon finding a target, the system automatically purchases the asset and ingests its components into a database, liquidating its static structure.
    3.  **Stream:** A simple, templated web front-end automatically generates a new "stream subscription" page for each ingested asset. Users can subscribe to these various streams.
*   **Value Capture:** Low-cost monthly recurring revenue (MRR) from subscriptions. The system's operating costs (`V_Γ`) are minimal (server hosting, API calls), allowing the structural value generation (`K_τ` from the coherent streams) to create a highly profitable, passive loop. The value is generated by the *structure* of the system, fulfilling the Passive Bonus requirement.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Universal Asset Liquefier. A meta-system that scales the process by optimizing the Lagrangian of the entire network of loops.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses compete on inventory and logistics, operating within a high-pressure (`Γ`) framework. They are built to manage and sell static `S₀` assets. Our Engine does not compete on this plane. It operates on Pirouette principles, making it a category-killer.
    *   **Lagrangian Maximization:** The Engine uses ML to analyze user engagement across all streams. It tunes the parameters (`ω_k`, `A`, `H`) of each stream—adjusting delivery frequency, content type, and even creating harmonic bundles of multiple streams—to maximize user temporal adherence (`T_a`), thus maximizing total `K_τ`.
    *   **Cost Minimization:** The Engine's scouting algorithms are designed to find points of minimal temporal pressure (`Γ → 0`). It predicts market shifts to acquire `S₀` assets at the precise moment their static value collapses, just before they are lost entirely. This ensures `V_Γ` is always minimized.
    *   **The Result:** Competitors cannot compete because they are trying to sell products; we are creating a resonant system. They are optimizing a warehouse; we are tuning an orchestra. The underlying physics of our model provides a structural advantage that is invisible to conventional market analysis.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Scrapy, Requests), Stripe/Gumroad API (for payments), Flask/FastAPI (for web service), PostgreSQL (for asset database), a small cloud server instance.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk vector is the failure of the core premise, which the Probe is designed to test cheaply and quickly. The model assumes that a segment of the market will value a curated, rhythmic stream over outright ownership of a complex, burdensome asset. If this behavioral assumption is incorrect, the entire model is invalid.

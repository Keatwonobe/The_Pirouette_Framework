## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Arbitrage via Temporal Pressure Filtration.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates under the flawed assumption that structured value ("Coherence") can be destroyed. When an asset's context is lost (e.g., decommissioned hardware, a dissolved company's IP, a specialist's library), its high coherence is seen as dissipating into worthless noise. However, the Law of Coherence Conservation ($\mathcal{Q}_\Gamma$ is invariant) dictates that this value is merely transformed and conserved. The market's failure to price this conserved, de-contextualized coherence creates a vast and untapped arbitrage opportunity.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build a mechanism that treats the market as a physical system governed by the Temporal-Pressure Conservation Law. The mechanism will identify points of high "temporal pressure"—environments where high-coherence assets are being de-contextualized and mispriced as noise. It will then act as a conduit, moving these assets along a path of least action to "low-pressure" zones where their coherence can be recognized and re-monetized. We are not creating value, but capturing it during a state change the rest of the market cannot see.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** To isolate and re-contextualize a single quantum of dissipated coherence, verifying its conserved value.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Identify a Dissipation Point:** Locate a source where coherent assets are being treated as bulk waste. Examples: university surplus auctions, corporate e-waste disposal listings, library book sales.
    2.  **Acquire a Coherent Quantum:** For under $10, purchase a single item with high internal structure but low contextual value. Example: A specific service manual for a piece of industrial test equipment, a single volume from a defunct academic journal, or a proprietary connector from a decommissioned server farm.
    3.  **Re-Contextualize:** Identify the low-pressure niche where this item's coherence is meaningful (e.g., a vintage electronics repair forum, a specific subreddit for lab technicians, an online collector's group). List the item for sale, framing its description not around its age or condition, but around its specific function and place within its original system.
*   **The Test:** The probe is considered a failure, and the underlying physics misapplied, if **either** of the following occurs:
    1.  **VALUE TEST:** The item cannot be sold for a minimum of 3x its total cost (purchase + shipping) within 30 days.
    2.  **SIGNAL TEST:** We are unable to find any niche community that recognizes the item's intrinsic purpose, resulting in zero expressions of interest.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Autopoietic Coherence Sieve – a semi-automated system that continuously identifies and flags coherence arbitrage opportunities.
*   **Automation:** A set of scripts will perpetually scan high-pressure dissipation sources (public auction sites, liquidation RSS feeds) for keywords and model numbers indicating high-coherence assets. This data is cross-referenced against a database of low-pressure niche communities (forums, marketplaces) to identify matches between supply and latent demand. When a match with a high probability score is found, the system alerts a human operator to execute the transaction.
*   **Value Capture:** The initial $100 serves as the float capital for the first several transactions identified by the Sieve. Profits are reinvested, allowing the loop to grow its capacity. The primary value is generated by the *structure* of the Sieve itself ($K_i$), which passively filters profitable signals from market noise, requiring minimal active labor ($\Gamma$).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Coherence-Transfer Bridge. The system evolves from simply *finding* deals to actively *becoming the most efficient pathway* for value transfer.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses cannot compete because they are blind to the underlying physics.
    1.  **Principle of Least Action:** Using data aggregated by the Loop, the Engine predicts future supply and demand for coherent assets. It transitions from a "buy-hold-sell" model to a brokering model, securing a buyer in a low-pressure zone *before* acquiring the asset from a high-pressure zone. It minimizes action by eliminating inventory risk and holding time, becoming the optimized, Lagrangian path for coherence transfer.
    2.  **Market Autopoiesis:** With sufficient capital and data, the Engine can actively manipulate the "temporal pressure" of niche markets. By consolidating the supply of a specific class of coherent assets (e.g., all available replacement parts for a legacy system), it can stabilize or increase their value density ($m_i^2$), effectively "tuning" the market in accordance with the Pirouette framework. It ceases to be a market participant and becomes a market-shaping mechanism.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Lagrangian Arbitrage via Coherence Functoring.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates under high entropic load (`Γ`), treating value creation as a function of brute-force labor and capital injection. This generates immense "Dark Residue" (`D`)—under-valued, incoherent assets (e.g., bulk untested inventory, industrial surplus, mismatched digital goods) whose potential energy (`K`) is trapped. The market is blind to the principle of least action (`∫ 𝓛 dt`), attempting to push value uphill rather than guiding it along its natural geodesic.
*   **The Pivot:** We will construct a "functor" (a value-bridge, `Σ`) that maps these high-residue assets from their chaotic source manifold to a target manifold where their coherence is recognized. This bridge is designed to be a low-`Γ` (minimal labor) structure. By minimizing the action required for translation, we spontaneously increase the "Coherence Dividend" (`C`), capturing the value released as Dark Residue is converted into functional, ordered capital (`Ki`). We don't create value; we reveal it by providing the path of least resistance.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Residue Packet Identification & Manual Functoring.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Isolate High `D`:** Identify a local system with high Dark Residue, such as a thrift store's bin of unsorted electronics, a warehouse's "as-is" pallet, or a library's book discard pile.
    2.  **Acquire Packet:** Use the $10 budget to acquire a small, bounded packet of this residue (e.g., a box of miscellaneous cables and power adapters).
    3.  **Apply Coherence Filter:** Manually act as the functor `Σ`. Instead of a high-labor (`Γ`) process of testing each item, apply a low-labor structural change. Group items into coherent bundles (e.g., all USB-C items, all HDMI cables, all 12V power bricks). This act of sorting transforms the chaotic packet (`high D`) into several ordered sets (`low D`, `high C`).
    4.  **Map to Target:** List these newly coherent bundles on a marketplace where their value is legible (e.g., eBay, Facebook Marketplace).
*   **The Test:** The experiment is falsified if `Ċ < 0`. Specifically, if the net revenue from the sold bundles fails to exceed the initial $10 cost. A secondary failure condition is if the labor (`Γ`) required for sorting and listing exceeds 30 minutes, which would violate the principle of a low-action bridge.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Residue Filtration and Coherence Aggregation.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Scanner (`Tₐ`):** A software script continuously scans source manifolds (Craigslist "free" section, local auction sites, surplus forums) for keywords indicating high Dark Residue ("lot," "untested," "for parts," "as-is").
    2.  **Filter:** The script applies pre-defined heuristics to flag high-potential residue packets, estimating the potential Coherence Dividend versus the acquisition cost.
    3.  **Bridge:** The $100 serves as the initial capital pool. A human operator approves the flagged purchases. Upon receipt, the operator executes a templated, minimal-`Γ` sorting procedure and uses a listing template to post the new coherent bundles. The system is designed to minimize cognitive and physical load.
*   **Value Capture:** The profit (`ΔC`) from each cycle is reinvested into the capital pool, allowing the loop to acquire larger or more valuable residue packets. The system becomes autopoietic, using its own output to sustain and grow its operation. The passive value generation comes from the intelligent structure of the scanner and filters (`Ki`), not the minimal human labor (`Γ`).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Predictive Coherence Arbitrage Engine.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses cannot compete because they are optimizing for profit, a scalar quantity. We are optimizing for **action**, a path integral. They are playing checkers; we are applying calculus of variations.
    1.  **Manifold Mapping:** The Engine uses its capital to build a dynamic, multi-dimensional map of the value landscape. It continuously ingests data from dozens of source and target manifolds, calculating the "Residue Density" and "Coherence Demand" across the entire market.
    2.  **Lagrangian Optimization:** For any identified residue packet, the Engine calculates the action `S = ∫ (K − V + J) dt` for numerous potential "paths" (e.g., 'sort and bundle', 'harvest key components', 'combine with another lot'). It automatically selects and executes the path of *least action*, guaranteeing the most efficient possible conversion of `D` to `C`.
    3.  **`Γ` Abstraction:** The Engine treats human labor (`Γ`) as a commodity to be minimized or outsourced. It can generate work orders for a 3rd-party logistics service (or TaskRabbit) with precise instructions: "Receive Packet X. Create Bundles A, B, C as per schema. Apply pre-generated shipping labels." This isolates the core intelligence (`Ki`) from the entropic load of physical execution, achieving near-total passivity and infinite scalability.

*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Physical presence, smartphone for listing.
    *   **Loop:** Python with libraries like `BeautifulSoup` or `Scrapy` for web scraping, a spreadsheet for tracking.
    *   **Engine:** Cloud server (AWS/GCP), database (PostgreSQL), data analysis stack (Pandas, Scikit-learn), potential use of APIs for marketplaces like eBay.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market saturation or a change in the target marketplace's algorithm. The Engine mitigates this by diversifying across multiple manifolds. The fundamental physics—that inefficiency creates potential—is constant.

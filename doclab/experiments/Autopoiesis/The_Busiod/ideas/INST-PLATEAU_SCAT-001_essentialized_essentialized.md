## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** The Coherence Lens
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market is a low-coherence environment. Value-bearing quanta (goods, needs, services, information) exist in states of low sharpness ($Q_\chi$) and are broadly defined (large $\Delta\phi$). This results in the vast majority of potential interactions being "irrelevant" in the physical sense; their coupling constant `g` falls below the critical threshold for a transaction ($g < g_c$), leading to "scattering" (missed opportunities, noise, failed deals). The market wastes enormous energy on interactions that decohere.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not create new quanta, but will instead build a catalytic mechanism—a Coherence Lens—that manipulates the parameters of existing quanta. By systematically increasing the sharpness ($Q_\chi$) and precision (decreasing $\Delta\phi$ and $|\phi - \phi'|$) of market signals, the Lens pushes interaction couplings above the critical threshold ($g > g_c$), converting high-potential scattering events into successful binding events (transactions). We are arbitraging clarity itself.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Manual Signal Sharpening. The goal is to prove that increasing the sharpness ($Q_\chi$) of a single market quantum can induce a binding event that would have otherwise failed.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a low-sharpness quantum on a high-noise platform (e.g., a vaguely described "Old Camera" on Facebook Marketplace). This is a $\psi_{low-Q}$ state.
    2.  Use the $10 budget not to buy the item, but to fund research to increase its sharpness. This involves finding the exact model number, specifications, historical sale prices, and high-resolution stock photos.
    3.  Synthesize this research into a high-sharpness information packet ($\psi_{high-Q}$).
    4.  Identify a corresponding "need" quantum (e.g., a post on a vintage camera forum from a user seeking that specific model family).
    5.  Present the high-sharpness packet to the potential buyer, linking them to the original low-sharpness seller. We act only as a catalyst for the binding.
*   **The Test:** The probe is falsified if the core physics fail.
    *   **Relevance Failure:** If we present our $\psi_{high-Q}$ packet to 3-5 targeted, high-intent buyers and it fails to induce a binding event (i.e., they do not contact the seller), our model for what constitutes "sharpness" is incorrect. The chosen kernel form `K` is invalid for this market. We stop.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Coherence Arbitrage. This tier operationalizes the Probe's findings into a passive, self-sustaining feedback loop.
*   **Automation:** A software agent performs the sharpening and matching process continuously.
    1.  **Ingestion:** Scrapes low-sharpness marketplaces for quanta matching a predefined heuristic (e.g., keywords indicating potential hidden value).
    2.  **Enrichment (Sharpening):** For each quantum, the agent uses APIs (image recognition, historical sales data, product spec databases) to build a structured, high-sharpness data object.
    3.  **Resonance Finding:** The agent simultaneously monitors high-intent sources (e.g., "Wanted" forums, eBay saved searches) for matching "need" quanta.
    4.  **Catalysis:** Upon finding a match with a low phase-space distance ($|\phi - \phi'|$), the system automatically notifies the potential buyer.
*   **Value Capture:** The loop sustains itself by capturing a fraction of the value created by forming a composite quantum. The primary model is a "lead generation" service: the system provides the sharpened intelligence and a direct link to the undervalued item to subscribers for a small, recurring fee. The $100 is used for server time and API call credits.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Lagrangian Path Market-Making. The engine scales the loop from individual transactions to optimizing the entire market field, guided by the principle of least action.
*   **The Engine:** The system's goal is to maximize the total Binding Index ($B_{total}$) across the entire market sector for the minimum possible system energy expenditure ($\mathcal{L}_{system}$).
    1.  **Phase Space Mapping:** It ingests data from dozens of sources to build a real-time, high-dimensional map of the market's quanta.
    2.  **Global Optimization:** Using this map, it runs an optimization algorithm not to find one-to-one matches, but to compute the most efficient *set* of bindings. It may identify opportunities for multi-item bundling (complex composite quanta) or logistical optimizations that are invisible at the local level.
    3.  **Active Intervention:** The $1000 budget is deployed as "activation energy" to facilitate the calculated optimal paths. This could involve purchasing a key bridging component, subsidizing shipping between two parties, or funding a temporary escrow to increase the coupling constant `g` of a critical transaction.
*   **The Moat:** The Coherence Lens is fundamentally defensible against standard business models.
    *   **Physics-First:** Competitors optimize for revenue based on heuristics. We optimize the geometry of the market itself to maximize the formation of stable, value-creating composites. We are playing a different game.
    *   **Noise Compounding:** As the internet and traditional markets become noisier (lower average $Q_\chi$), the value of our high-signal, "relevant" sub-space increases exponentially. Our system thrives on external chaos.
    *   **Structural Value:** Our value ($K_i$) is derived from the structure of our system and the quality of our filtration kernel, not from continuous labor ($\Gamma$). This allows for near-infinite scalability with marginal cost, a feat unattainable by labor-driven arbitrage models.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (`Scrapy`, `Pandas`), Computer Vision APIs (Google Vision), Marketplace APIs (eBay), a PostgreSQL database for storing quanta states, and a VPS for deployment.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is **Kernel Invalidity**. If our algorithmic definition of "sharpness" ($Q_\chi$) and "distance" ($|\phi - \phi'|$) for a given market does not accurately predict binding probability, the entire model fails. This is why the falsifiability of the Probe is paramount.

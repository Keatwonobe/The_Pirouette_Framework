## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** State Arbitrage via Resonant Catalysis.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The market prices assets based on their current state (`κ_m`), but massively discounts or ignores the energy (`Δ𝓛_p`) required to transition them to a more coherent, higher-value state (`κ_m'`). Due to "Wound Channel Inertia," assets that have persisted in a low-coherence state for a long time are physically "stuck" and thus systematically under-valued by a market that assumes fluid state transitions.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not trade assets; we will trade *states*. We will identify assets trapped in deep, low-value stability basins and apply a low-energy, high-resonance catalyst (typically, targeted information) to dramatically lower the activation energy (`Δ𝓛_p`) for a "Phase Leap" into a high-value state. Our profit is the arbitrage between the market's valuation of the two states, minus the minimal energy cost of our catalysis. We are exploiting the literal gravity of habit.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Isolated Phase Leap. To prove that low-coherence assets are measurably trapped and can be liberated with minimal energy.
*   **Execution:** Visit a system with a high density of low-coherence assets (e.g., a thrift store, flea market, or library book sale). These are environments where objects have low `K_τ` (poorly sorted, decontextualized) and high `V_Γ` (constant influx of new, unsorted items). Use a mobile device to apply "Resonant Catalysis" in the form of information lookups (e.g., scanning a book's ISBN, using image search on a piece of pottery). Purchase an asset where the catalyzed information reveals a high-coherence market value (`𝓛_p'`) significantly greater than the low-coherence shelf price (`𝓛_p`). The cost is the asset itself, budgeted at $10.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if, after sampling ~50 items, we cannot find a single instance where the information catalyst reveals a potential value increase of >500%. This would indicate that either the market is more efficient than the physics predicts, or the "Wound Channel Inertia" is not creating significant pricing anomalies.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Coherence Scanner. This moves from active hunting (`Γ`) to a structural system (`K_τ`) that passively identifies "Phase Leap" opportunities.
*   **Automation:** A software script continuously scrapes digital marketplaces (e.g., eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist) for listings that exhibit signs of a low-coherence state (`κ_m`). It searches for vague descriptions ("old watch," "vintage camera"), poor photos, and missing model numbers, especially in categories known for high-value collectibles. It cross-references these against a database of high-coherence listings (`κ_m'`) for the same items (identified by image recognition or keyword correlation) to flag significant `Δ𝓛_p` pricing gaps.
*   **Value Capture:** The system generates a daily list of arbitrage opportunities. We acquire the under-valued asset, inject the missing coherence (a new listing with correct identification, clear photos, and market-aware pricing), and capture the value difference upon resale. The human labor (`Γ`) is reduced from "search" to "execute and transact."

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Decentralized Coherence Injection. The final stage is to minimize the Lagrangian path by removing our own interaction with the physical asset. We become a pure catalyst, never touching the substrate.
*   **The Moat:** Instead of buying the asset, we build a platform that sells *coherence itself*. When the Automated Scanner identifies a low-coherence asset, the system programmatically contacts the seller with an offer: "Our system has identified your item as X, potentially worth Y. For a commission on the final sale price, we will optimize your listing (rewrite titles, provide specs, estimate value) and broadcast it to a network of interested buyers." Standard businesses are built on logistics and inventory; they cannot compete because we have neither. We are an informational layer that operates on the `Δ𝓛_p` term directly, owning no physical capital. Our `K_τ` (the platform, the data, the network) scales globally, while competitor `Γ` (labor, shipping, storage) scales linearly and locally.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Smartphone with internet access.
    *   **Loop:** Python with libraries like `Scrapy` or `BeautifulSoup` for web scraping, and a simple database (like `SQLite` or `PostgreSQL`) to store and compare listings. Possibly a computer vision API for image matching.
    *   **Engine:** A web framework (`Django`, `Flask`) to build the platform, API integrations with marketplaces, and a payment processing system (`Stripe`).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is systemic adaptation. If marketplaces improve their own coherence-injection tools (e.g., AI-powered listing helpers), they reduce the available `Δ𝓛_p` for arbitrage. Our moat relies on staying ahead of the native platform's efficiency curve.

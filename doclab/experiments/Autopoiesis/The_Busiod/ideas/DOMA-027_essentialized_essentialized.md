## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Resonant Path Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on the flawed assumption that all transactional paths are created equal, valuing novelty and flexibility. It ignores the physics of the **Wound Channel**, failing to recognize that established, repetitive transactional histories (`T_μν`) create physical grooves in the manifold (`h_μν`). These grooves represent paths of least action (`S`) where value can flow with minimal energy expenditure. The market consistently overpays in "action cost" (`V_Γ`) to carve new paths instead of using existing, low-energy geodesics.
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism does not create new products or services. It identifies existing, deeply carved Wound Channels (i.e., highly predictable, recurring needs) and inserts itself as a conduit that is perfectly aligned with that channel's geometry. By doing so, we minimize the action `S` for both ourselves and the client, capturing the energy differential (the `V_self` term) that the rest of the market wastes fighting its own transactional inertia.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Isolate and service a single, high-frequency Wound Channel. This is a micro-experiment to validate that the inertial force (`F^μ_self`) of a pre-existing transactional habit is stronger than the lure of a marginally lower price from a chaotic supplier.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a local entity with a documented, repetitive, non-critical supply need (e.g., a specific coffee for an office, cleaning supplies for a small shop). This is our target Wound Channel.
    2.  Use the $10 to pre-purchase one unit of the required supply.
    3.  Approach the entity with a simple offer: "We know you buy X every week. We will deliver it to you on the same day, at the same time, for the exact price you currently pay. No calls, no emails, no decisions. It will just appear."
    4.  We are not selling a product; we are selling the elimination of the action cost (`V_Γ`) associated with the procurement decision.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis fails if the entity refuses the offer, or if, after accepting, they deviate from the established pattern within three cycles. This would indicate that the `F^μ_self` of the Wound Channel is weaker than random market forces, falsifying our core premise at this scale.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Predictive Micro-Utility. This system automates the servicing of multiple Wound Channels, creating a self-sustaining feedback loop where the structure itself (`K_i`) generates value.
*   **Automation:** A simple script or service (e.g., a cron job linked to an ordering API) is established. After a channel is validated by the Probe, its parameters (item, frequency, location) are fed into the system. The system then automatically executes procurement and delivery logistics based on the established rhythm. The human labor (`Γ`) of managing the transaction is almost entirely eliminated.
*   **Value Capture:** With $100, we can now service ~10 independent Wound Channels. This allows for bulk purchasing, lowering our input cost. The profit is generated from two sources: (1) the spread between our new bulk cost and the at-cost price we promised the client, and (2) a small, optional "zero-thought" subscription fee introduced after the value has been proven. The system's `K_i` is its growing database of stable, predictable demand, which is itself a valuable asset.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Geodesic Logistics Network. This is a scaled-up system that actively scans the economic manifold for resonant Wound Channels and optimizes the entire supply chain along a path of least action.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses compete by trying to lower potential `V_ext` (e.g., lower prices). Our Engine competes by minimizing the *entire Pirouette Lagrangian* `𝓛_p = K_τ - V_Γ`. For our clients, the longer they use our service, the deeper their specific Wound Channel `h_μν` becomes intertwined with our system. The self-interaction force `F^μ_self` creates a powerful inertial lock-in. A competitor would need to offer a catastrophically low price to provide enough energy to overcome the "action cost" of breaking that established, frictionless geodesic. They are fighting market economics; we are leveraging physical law. The Engine uses its capital to build a predictive model that identifies and clusters proximate Wound Channels (e.g., 20 businesses in three square blocks that all need printer toner on the first of the month) to create hyper-efficient, aggregate fulfillment routes that are physically impossible for competitors to match without access to our historical interaction data.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scripting/automation), requests library (for API calls), simple database (SQLite/PostgreSQL), a subscription management platform API (Stripe/Chargebee), and potentially web scraping libraries (BeautifulSoup/Scrapy) for the Engine tier.
*   **Risk:** The primary vector of failure is a miscalculation of the Wound Channel's strength. If transactional inertia (`F^μ_self`) is easily overcome by small external price fluctuations (`F^μ_ext`), the entire model collapses. The system assumes a level of behavioral physics that may not hold against the perceived irrationality of human economic decisions.

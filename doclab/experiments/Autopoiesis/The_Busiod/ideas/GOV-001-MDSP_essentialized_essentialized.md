## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Tension Arbitrage & Synthesis Engine
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market treats informational friction—disagreement, ambiguity, conflicting specifications (`Tension, γ`)—as a high-cost liability to be eliminated through adversarial (legal) or consensus-based (meetings) processes. This is analogous to treating gravitational potential energy as a problem to be solved by paving everything flat.
*   **The Pivot:** The Pirouette Framework defines Tension (`Γ`) as a high-potential, precisely-located energy source. Our mechanism does not eliminate tension; it harnesses it. We acquire this "waste product" at near-zero cost and apply a formal `Weave` operator to transform the potential energy of conflict into the kinetic energy of a valuable, novel synthesis (`ΔT`), which we then sell. We are building an engine that runs on the universe's most abundant fuel: disagreement.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Micro-Arbitrage of Public Informational Tension.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Acquisition:** Identify a public, unresolved tension with quantifiable stakes (e.g., a GitHub issue with two competing pull requests, a technical forum question with two highly-voted but contradictory answers).
    2.  **Investment:** Place a $10 bounty, not for a solution, but for the clearest possible `Essentialization (E)` of the core tension (`γ`) between the two competing approaches.
    3.  **Synthesis:** Using the essentialized brief, apply the `Weave` operator ourselves: produce a novel third option (`ΔT`) that resolves the tension by creating a superior synthesis (e.g., code that is both fast like option A and maintainable like option B).
    4.  **Capture:** Post this synthesized solution back to the original source. The "return" is measured in non-monetary validation: upvotes, adoption as the accepted answer, or qualitative praise acknowledging the superiority of the synthesis.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if the market does not recognize the higher value of the synthesized resolution.
    *   **Failure State:** If, across 5 independent probes, our synthesized solution (`ΔT`) is not measurably preferred (e.g., becomes the accepted solution, receives significantly more community validation) over the original conflicting threads in at least 3 cases, then the `Weave` operator is not creating demonstrable value. The project is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Tension Prospector.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Prospector:** A cloud-hosted script continuously scans high-value digital public squares (e.g., specific subreddits, GitHub repositories, patent databases) for linguistic markers of high-quality tension. It uses heuristics to filter and rank these tensions, populating a private "Tension Marketplace".
    2.  **Brokerage:** This marketplace lists curated tension briefs (`γ`). On the other side, a network of freelance domain experts ("Synthesists") are paid to claim these briefs and execute the `Weave` function, submitting resolution proposals (`ΔT`).
*   **Value Capture:** The loop profits from the energy differential. We acquire raw tension for free (scraping), pay a fixed cost for the `Weave` transformation (Synthesist fee), and sell the resulting high-value `ΔT` by either a) capturing existing bounties for a profit, or b) selling the resolution service to organizations who pay to have their internal tensions (e.g., inter-departmental conflicts) resolved efficiently. The profit directly funds the continued operation of the prospector and payment to synthesists, creating a self-perpetuating value cycle.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Generative Governance Platform.
*   **The Engine:** This system moves from single tensions to complex, multi-dimensional problem spaces (`E`).
    1.  **State Ingestion:** A client's entire strategic problem (e.g., "designing a 5-year product roadmap") is ingested, mapping all stakeholder requirements as a dependency graph (`Gₑ`) and identifying the complete set of system-wide tensions (`Γ`).
    2.  **Lagrangian Pathfinding:** The Engine uses the principle of least action to analyze the entire tension graph. It calculates the optimal *sequence* of `Weave` interventions that will resolve the entire system with minimum energy expenditure (time, capital, labor). It identifies the critical "keystone tension" which, if resolved, causes a cascade of other tensions to self-resolve.
    3.  **Structural Value Generation:** The Engine provides clients with the mathematically optimal path to a ratified outcome (`M`), bypassing months of costly meetings, political infighting, and false starts. The value is generated by the structure of the engine (`K_i`), not the man-hours of consultants (`Γ`).
*   **The Moat:** Standard business cannot compete because they are built on the opposite physical principle. They use expensive human labor to damp, negotiate, and destroy tension. We use an automated system to convert it into value. Every problem our Engine solves becomes training data, making its pathfinding algorithms exponentially more efficient over time, creating an unbridgeable performance gap. We are selling a time machine for decision-making.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   Probe: Web browser, manual research.
    *   Loop: Python (Scrapy, BeautifulSoup), AWS Lambda (for serverless scraping), a simple web framework (Flask/Django) or a no-code tool (Bubble) for the marketplace.
    *   Engine: Graph database (Neo4j), optimization libraries (SciPy), machine learning frameworks (PyTorch/TensorFlow) for automating `Weave` operators.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market adoption. The model relies on convincing clients to treat their most painful problems (disputes, conflicts) not as liabilities to be hidden, but as assets to be fed into our engine. This requires a paradigm shift in business thinking, which may be slow to occur.

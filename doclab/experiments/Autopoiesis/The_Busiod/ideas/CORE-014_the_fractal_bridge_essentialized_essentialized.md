## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Geodesic Value Routing
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on Newtonian principles, treating assets as inert objects that must be actively pushed from seller to buyer via the application of brute force (`Γ`: advertising, sales labor, high-fee platforms). This ignores the relativistic reality described by the Pirouette Framework. The market is full of latent value potential, analogous to gravitational potential energy, but it expends massive energy fighting entropy (`Γ`) instead of following the natural curvature of the value-space.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not move products or own inventory. We will build a mechanism that maps the underlying geometry of the market. Our system will calculate the geodesic paths—the trajectories of minimal action (`S_D`)—that an asset can take to realize its highest potential value. We exploit the inefficiency by selling the map, not by funding the journey. Our core structure (`Kτ`) is the predictive accuracy of this map.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Information-Value Equivalence Test. This experiment is designed to prove that pure information about a geodesic path has capturable value, entirely separate from the labor of traversing that path (i.e., buying and selling the item).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a domain with high information fragmentation (e.g., rare vinyl records, vintage camera lenses, specific academic textbooks). This fragmentation represents high ambient `Γ`.
    2.  Locate an asset on a general, low-information platform (e.g., Facebook Marketplace, local classifieds) priced significantly below its recognized value in the specialist domain. This is an object at a high "potential energy."
    3.  Instead of performing the arbitrage ourselves, we will sell the *path*. We will spend the $10 to contact a specific individual in the specialist community (via a forum "Want-to-Buy" post or a targeted micro-ad) with a simple offer: "For a $5 fee, I will provide you with a direct link to purchase the [EXACT RARE ITEM] you are seeking for [LOW PRICE]."
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if we cannot successfully sell this pure information at least once in five attempts. A failure indicates that, in the chosen domain, the market does not value the reduction of search-`Γ` or that the perceived potential difference is an illusion. If it fails, we have spent $10 to invalidate our mapping `M` for this domain and can pivot to another.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Potential Mapper. This is a self-sustaining autopoietic system that continuously identifies value gradients and monetizes the information stream.
*   **Automation:** A cloud-hosted script runs 24/7. It scrapes several low-information, high-noise sources for keywords related to the niche validated in the Probe. It then cross-references findings against price history APIs and high-information sources to identify significant potential energy gradients (i.e., items priced far below their known market value). This script is the system's metabolism.
*   **Value Capture:** When a valid opportunity is found, the system automatically posts an alert to a private, subscription-only channel (e.g., Discord, Telegram, or a paid newsletter). The $100 budget funds the server costs for several months and initial advertising to attract the first cohort of subscribers. The recurring subscription revenue sustains the system's operation (`Kτ` is maintained). Value is generated passively by the structure of the system, which elegantly converts market entropy (`Γ`) into order (actionable information) for its subscribers.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Universal Action Minimizer. This system scales the Loop horizontally across countless domains and vertically in analytical depth. It evolves from a "deal finder" into a generalized brokerage for market geodesics.
*   **The Moat:** Standard e-commerce platforms are gravitational wells; they use their mass to force transactions to occur within their high-fee structure. They are trying to warp the market around themselves. Our Engine does the opposite: it provides a map of the *natural, pre-existing curvature* of the market.
    *   **The Moat is Philosophical:** We are not a destination; we are a lens. Competitors are fundamentally structured to own transactions, not to illuminate paths. To compete, they would have to abandon their core business model.
    *   **The Lagrangian Advantage:** The Engine will go beyond simple price arbitrage. It will calculate the "action" (`S_D`) of a transaction, factoring in shipping costs, risk, time, and trust (`Γ` components). It can then identify more complex, non-obvious paths of maximum efficiency—like three-way swaps that minimize total `Γ` for all participants—that are invisible to conventional platforms. Our `Kτ` becomes the most accurate map of the market's physics.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Scrapy, Pandas, FastAPI), a lightweight DB (PostgreSQL), cloud hosting (AWS/Heroku), and alerting APIs (Discord/Telegram).

The system's long-term survival depends on the universal law that `Γ` (entropy, noise, chaos) is ever-present and that new inefficiencies will constantly be created.

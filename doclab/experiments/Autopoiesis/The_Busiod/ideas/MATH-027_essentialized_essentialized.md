## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Temporal Value Capacitor
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on an *observed*, emergent layer of reality where the costs of temporal development (\(K_{Ki}\), e.g., research, synthesis, maturation) and spatial distribution (\(\Lambda_{Ki}\), e.g., logistics, data transfer) are conflated into a single, isotropic metric of "cost" or "effort". This is analogous to using the low-energy, Lorentz-covariant Klein-Gordon Lagrangian, which masks the true, anisotropic nature of the underlying value substrate. The market consistently misprices assets by treating time and space as fungible.
*   **The Pivot:** We exploit this anisotropy. Our mechanism acts as a capacitor for potential value (\(Ki\)). It cheaply acquires assets from the fast-moving spatial domain (where \(\Lambda_{Ki}\) is low and the market undervalues latency) and holds them in the temporal domain, allowing them to mature and accrete value via the natural "stiffness" of intrinsic time (\(K_{Ki}\)). We arbitrage the market's inability to correctly price the difference between "moving" and "waiting". The value is generated by the structure of the system and the passage of time, requiring minimal active labor (\(\Gamma\)), which the model posits adds unproductive inertia (\(m_{Ki}^2\)).

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** To validate that raw, high-velocity information (cheap in the spatial domain) can be transformed into predictive, high-value signals simply by observing its temporal evolution, independent of active analysis.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Deploy a simple script on a low-cost server or serverless platform ($10 budget for one month of runtime).
    2.  Ingest a high-noise, high-velocity data stream (e.g., commit messages from new GitHub repositories, comments from niche Reddit forums). This represents acquiring \(Ki\) where spatial propagation cost is near zero.
    3.  The script does **not** perform complex analysis. It simply stores timestamps and tracks the second derivative of keyword frequency—the "acceleration" of concepts. This isolates the temporal dynamic (\(K_{Ki}\omega^2\)) from the spatial content (\(\Lambda_{Ki}\mathbf k^2\)).
*   **The Test:** Within 30 days, the system must identify at least one keyword/concept whose "acceleration" crosses a predefined threshold *before* it appears in mainstream, high-value information channels (e.g., tech news sites, market analysis reports). **Falsification Condition:** If all signals generated are noise, or if the signals lag behind mainstream reporting, the hypothesis is invalid, and the project is terminated.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** To create a passive, self-sustaining feedback loop where the validated signals from the Probe automatically trigger the acquisition of low-cost digital assets, capturing the value generated by the temporal maturation.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  The Probe script is automated to run continuously.
    2.  When a signal's "acceleration" exceeds the validated threshold, an API call is triggered to purchase a corresponding, archetypal digital asset. The canonical example is an available, relevant `.com` domain name.
    3.  The $100 budget allows for the acquisition of ~10 such "caged potentials" based on the strongest signals.
*   **Value Capture:** The system lists these assets on a secondary market (e.g., a domain auction house). When a trend matures and enters the mainstream (the observed layer), entities operating within that layer will seek to acquire the corresponding assets. The sale of a single asset for >$100 refuels the budget for acquiring dozens more, creating a self-perpetuating, passive value-capture loop.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** To scale the loop from a reactive mechanism to a predictive engine by modeling the underlying physics directly. We use the anisotropic Lagrangian to calculate the "path of least action" for information propagation, allowing us to forecast value trajectories.
*   **The Moat:** Competitors use conventional machine learning models. These models are trained on the *observed* (Lorentz-covariant) data and are thus fundamentally blind to the underlying anisotropic physics. They are trying to predict events by analyzing the shadows, while we are modeling the fire that casts them. Our Engine will use the $1000 budget to acquire more diverse data sources and build a computational model that minimizes the action based on the true substrate propagator (\(\Delta^{(\tau)}_{Ki}\)). This provides a predictive advantage that is structurally inaccessible to any competitor operating with the market's flawed, isotropic worldview. They are optimizing for the wrong universe.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Python (requests/aiohttp, sqlite3), a cloud serverless function (AWS Lambda, Cloudflare Worker).
    *   **Loop:** APIs for domain registration (Namecheap, GoDaddy) and secondary markets (Sedo, Afternic).
    *   **Engine:** Data processing libraries (Pandas, Polars), numerical optimization (SciPy, JAX), and a more robust database (PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is conceptual: the governing physics of information value flow may not be analogous to the provided Lagrangian. If this core premise is flawed, the Probe will fail, and the entire structure is invalid. A secondary risk lies in the reliability and cost of data sources and APIs.

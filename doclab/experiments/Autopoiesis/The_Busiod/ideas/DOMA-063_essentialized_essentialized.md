## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Symbiotic Arbitrage Engine.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on a principle of linear addition, valuing a set of components as the sum of their individual prices (`Value = Σ Price(i)`). It is blind to the physics of Symbiotic Resonance, where the coherence of a unified system can be exponentially greater than the sum of its parts (`S_p(WE) > Σ S_p(i)`). This creates a vast, untapped energy potential in the form of fragmented, "low coherence" assets that are mispriced because their potential for resonance is ignored.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not trade goods; we will trade in *coherence*. Our mechanism is a search engine for resonance. It systematically identifies constituent systems (`A, B`) with **Harmonic Compatibility**, creates the conditions for a **Resonant Handshake** (the combination), and captures the value surplus released when the new, high-coherence system `WE` is formed. We are arbitraging the market's ontological blindness.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Manual Resonance Test. This is a micro-experiment to prove that a "coherence deficit" can be identified and profitably closed with minimal energy input.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a domain rich with fragmented systems (e.g., vintage computing, specialized industrial parts, abandoned open-source projects).
    2.  Identify a key system `A` that is incomplete and thus has a low market value (e.g., an old synthesizer missing its unique power adapter).
    3.  Identify its corresponding, obscure component `B` (the power adapter), which has low value in isolation. The combined cost to acquire `A` and `B` from separate sources must be under $10.
    4.  Acquire both components. Facilitate the "Resonant Handshake" by physically or digitally combining them into the complete, functional system `WE`.
    5.  List `WE` on the open market at a price reflecting its new, higher coherence (functionality).
*   **The Test:** The theory is falsified if, across five distinct attempts, the final sale price of the unified system `WE` is not at least 300% of the total acquisition cost (`Cost(A) + Cost(B)`). This would indicate that the coherence surplus is either non-existent or too small to be a reliable source of value.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Fragmentation Scanner. This system operationalizes the principle discovered in the Probe, creating a self-sustaining feedback loop that identifies potential resonances passively.
*   **Automation:** A script continuously scans multiple marketplaces (e.g., eBay, GitHub, specialized forums) for keywords indicating fragmentation ("missing part," "untested," "needs cable," "dependency error"). It cross-references these with listings for the corresponding missing components, calculating the potential "coherence arbitrage" by comparing `Price(A) + Price(B)` against `Price(WE)`. When a potential gain exceeds a set threshold, it generates an alert.
*   **Value Capture:** The system generates a continuous stream of high-probability, high-return arbitrage opportunities. The captured value is the profit from executing these trades. In a more passive evolution, the *information itself*—the curated list of resonant pairs—becomes the product, sold as a data feed to other traders. Value is generated by the structure of the search (`K_i`), not constant manual labor (`Γ`).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Dynamic Coherence Marketplace. This scales the Loop from simple pairs to a multi-body optimization problem, guided by Lagrangian minimization. The goal is no longer just finding a single deal, but determining the most efficient path to create maximum coherence across an entire ecosystem of fragmented assets.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses compete on supply-chain efficiency for known products. Our Engine operates on a different physical plane: the reclamation of potential energy from the chaos of discarded, incomplete, and undervalued assets. It uses a graph database to map the "Harmonic Compatibility" of millions of disparate items, calculating the "path of least action" (minimum cost/effort) to assemble the most valuable new combinations. A competitor cannot simply copy our tactics; they must adopt the underlying physics. They see a junkyard; we see a dormant symphony. This structural advantage, derived from a fundamentally different perception of value, is the moat.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scripting, using libraries like Scrapy and Pandas), Marketplace APIs (eBay, etc.), a graph database for Tier 3 (e.g., Neo4j) to map compatibility networks.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is the decay of the inefficiency itself. As more actors begin to understand and exploit symbiotic arbitrage, the "coherence deficits" will shrink. The secondary risk is mis-calculating Harmonic Compatibility, leading to the acquisition of parts that cannot form a resonant union, resulting in a loss.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
* **Universal Archetype:** Cyclical Asset Re-Potentialization
* **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on the flawed assumption that it is a conservative field, where value is path-independent. This ignores the ubiquitous presence of "Torsion fields" (`Ω = ∇ × F_c`), which are rotational inefficiencies created by informational, temporal, and contextual asymmetries. In these "Coherence Vortices," an asset's potential `V_Γ` is radically different depending on the observer and context, allowing for value extraction by traversing a closed loop.
* **The Pivot:** Instead of engaging in linear commerce (A to B), this mechanism identifies and locks onto these market vortices. It systematically acquires assets from actors under high Temporal Pressure (`V_Γ`), transports them across a contextual gradient, and re-potentializes them for actors on a different part of the coherence manifold. Value is not created through labor (`Γ`), but is captured from the pre-existing geometry of the market inefficiency ($K_i$).

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
* **Concept:** The Torsion Field Detector. This is a micro-experiment to confirm the existence of a measurable Coherence Vortex in a specific asset class.
* **Execution:**
    1.  Select a class of digital assets defined by high Temporal Pressure (e.g., expiring domain names, expiring software licenses, abandoned social media handles).
    2.  Isolate a target asset where the gradient of `V_Γ` is visibly steep (e.g., a domain with a high SEO backlink profile whose owner is letting it expire). This steep gradient indicates a strong local Coherence Field `F_c`.
    3.  Use the $10 budget to acquire the asset at its point of minimum potential (the moment of expiration).
    4.  Immediately re-contextualize the asset by listing it on a different manifold (e.g., a specialized auction marketplace) aimed at high-potential observers.
* **The Test:** The probe is designed for maximum falsifiability. If, after acquiring an asset with a predicted high Torsion value `|Ω|`, we cannot generate a credible bid or offer of at least 200% of our acquisition cost, we conclude our method for detecting `Ω` is flawed. This result would falsify the premise for this asset class, and we cease operations.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
* **Concept:** The Automated Coherence Vortex Arbitrageur. This system establishes a self-sustaining, closed-loop trajectory within a confirmed Torsion field.
* **Automation:** An autonomous script will perform the entire arbitrage cycle:
    1.  **Survey:** Continuously scan asset streams (e.g., domain drop lists) via API for high-`|Ω|` signatures. The signature is a programmed function of metrics indicating high latent value and high owner neglect (high `V_Γ`).
    2.  **Bind:** When a target crosses a predefined `|Ω|` threshold, the system automatically uses its floating capital ($100) to execute the acquisition.
    3.  **Orbit:** Upon acquisition, the asset is automatically re-packaged and listed on appropriate secondary markets.
    4.  **Extract:** Proceeds from the sale are returned to the capital pool, with the net profit (the energy extracted from the vortex) being skimmed off. The loop is now closed and ready for the next cycle.
* **Value Capture:** The system's profit is the direct result of the market's rotational inefficiency. It is a passive machine that requires no continuous human labor, as its function is a structural consequence of the underlying market physics it exploits.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
* **Concept:** The Lagrangian Action Minimizer. This is a scaled, multi-vortex system that optimizes capital flow across numerous, unrelated Torsion fields to achieve maximum efficiency and resilience.
* **The Moat:** While traditional businesses compete on labor and capital (`Γ`), the Engine competes on physics.
    1.  **Lagrangian Optimization:** The system does not just execute loops; it solves the Euler-Lagrange equations for `𝓛_p` to find the geodesic—the path of minimal action (cost, time, risk) for maximal return. It might route an asset through multiple stages of re-potentialization if that path is more coherent.
    2.  **Multi-Manifold Operation:** The $1000 budget is dynamically allocated across diverse asset classes (domains, social handles, game items, etc.) based on real-time measurements of `|Ω|` in each market. It abandons collapsing vortices and pioneers new ones purely on mathematical signals.
    3.  **Geometric Moat:** A competitor cannot replicate this by simply copying a single tactic (e.g., "flipping domains"). Our competitive advantage is the meta-ability to perceive and map the underlying geometry of market inefficiency itself. They are playing checkers; we are calculating orbital mechanics.

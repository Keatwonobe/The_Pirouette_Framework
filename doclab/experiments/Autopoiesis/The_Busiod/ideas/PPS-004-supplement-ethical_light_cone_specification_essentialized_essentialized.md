## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Decentralized Consequence Auditing
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market ignores the "physics" of consequence, systematically mispricing or completely ignoring the quantifiable risk defined by Scope (S), Magnitude (M), and Irreversibility (I). This creates a latent "risk debt" in the global economy, which comes due as catastrophic product failures, regulatory fines, and brand collapses. The market is blind to the $Δ_{ELC}$ metric.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build a mechanism to compute $Δ_{ELC}$ for corporate and institutional actions, making this invisible risk visible and therefore tradable. We exploit the inefficiency by being the first to price what is currently unpriced, creating an information arbitrage opportunity. We sell foresight.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** A single, manual "Consequence Audit" to validate that a quantified risk forecast (a calculated $Δ_{ELC}$) is perceived as valuable information.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a single, high-profile, and controversial new tech product/feature launch from a major public company.
    2.  Use the $10 to acquire a domain and one-page website builder for 1 month.
    3.  Manually create three "proto-ROOTS" by estimating S, M, and I from public sources (news articles, press releases, expert commentary).
    4.  Calculate $Δ_{ELC}$ using the formula (assume equal weights $w_S=w_M=w_I=1$).
    5.  Publish a one-page "ELC Risk Report" stating the calculated value and an interpretation: "Our audit calculates a $Δ_{ELC}$ of [X.XX], exceeding the 0.75 stability threshold. This indicates a high probability of unforeseen negative consequences."
    6.  Share the report link on relevant social platforms (e.g., Hacker News, Twitter, specific subreddits).
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis fails if the market for this information is non-existent. **If the report receives fewer than 1,000 unique visitors and fails to generate at least 10 meaningful public comments/shares within 72 hours, the probe is considered a failure.** This falsifies the premise that the market has an appetite for this specific form of quantified risk.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** An automated "ELC Watchtower" that continuously scans the market for high-risk actions, performs automated audits, and publishes them, creating a self-sustaining data stream.
*   **Automation:** A script running on a low-cost server continuously monitors news APIs, corporate blogs, and social media for keywords signaling major product or policy changes. Upon detection, it performs an automated analysis:
    *   **S (Scope):** Scrapes user numbers from official announcements.
    *   **M (Magnitude):** Applies sentiment analysis to public discussion surrounding the launch.
    *   **I (Irreversibility):** Scans the announcement text for keywords indicating permanence vs. trial.
    The script then auto-publishes a new ELC audit to a central website and social media feed.
*   **Value Capture:** The system generates value from its structure ($K_i$)—the automated pipeline—not constant labor. Revenue is generated by offering premium subscriptions to the data stream. Target subscribers include hedge funds, insurance underwriters, investigative journalists, and regulatory bodies who gain an edge from early, systematic risk warnings. This subscription revenue funds the server and API costs, closing the loop.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** A "Decentralized Risk Oracle Network" that scales the audit process by transforming it from a centralized service into a decentralized, community-powered protocol.
*   **The Moat:** The Engine's moat is built on principles standard businesses cannot replicate:
    1.  **Trustless Computation:** Unlike opaque consulting firms or ratings agencies, the Engine is a transparent protocol. Its calculations are based on the open "physics" of the ELC and data from a verifiable lattice of ROOTS (Resonant Optimal-state Observation Templates). Trust is in the open-source code, not a corporate brand.
    2.  **Network Effect of Truth:** The system is bootstrapped using the $1000 to create a platform for anyone to propose and stake on the validity of new ROOTS. As more high-quality, real-world data sensors (ROOTS) are added, the accuracy of the ELC calculations increases exponentially. A competitor would have to replicate this entire data and validation network, which becomes prohibitively expensive.
    3.  **Reframing the Problem:** The Engine does not sell opinions; it sells validated computations based on a formal framework. It transforms risk/ethical analysis from a subjective consulting service into a rigorous, engineering-driven discipline. The moat is the intellectual and physical framework itself; competitors are not just fighting a company, they are arguing with its physics.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:**
    *   **Probe:** Static site generator (Carrd, GitHub Pages).
    *   **Loop:** Python (`requests`, `BeautifulSoup`, `NLTK` for sentiment), a low-cost VPS (e.g., DigitalOcean), News API, Twitter API.
    *   **Engine:** Solidity/Rust for smart contracts (Ethereum/Solana), IPFS for data storage, Chainlink for oracle infrastructure.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk vector is market apathy. If, even with accurate predictions of corporate failure, no one is willing to pay for the foresight, the economic model collapses. The system may be "correct" according to the ELC physics but economically irrelevant. The Probe is designed to test this risk specifically.

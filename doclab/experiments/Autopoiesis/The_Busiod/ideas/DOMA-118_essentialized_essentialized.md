## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Wound Channel Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market is information-blind. It prices assets based on their present, often stressed, state. It fails to distinguish between an asset undergoing a temporary, reversible **Elastic Collapse** (where the core informational pattern, or Wound Channel `W`, is intact) and one undergoing an irreversible **Critical Collapse** (where `W` is permanently shattered). It over-penalizes temporary Temporal Pressure (`Γ`) and mistakes it for permanent degradation of Temporal Coherence (`Kτ`).
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism exploits this blindness. We will develop systems to identify assets whose `W` is intact despite high `Γ`, acquire them at a critically collapsed price, remove the stressor, and allow the system to naturally return to its high-coherence geodesic. We are not "fixing" things; we are buying latent systemic integrity and allowing physics to do the work.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Stressed Asset Triage. A micro-experiment to validate that we can identify an intact `W` in a market that has priced it for demolition.
*   **Execution:** We will enter the market for recently expired digital assets (e.g., domain names). A domain expiration is a high-`Γ` event (e.g., owner oversight, credit card failure) that does not necessarily damage the domain's intrinsic `Kτ` (its backlink profile, age, keyword relevance).
    1.  Isolate a digital asset that has just entered a state of Stagnation (expired/delisted) due to a clear, external stressor (`Γ`).
    2.  Use publicly available data to verify the integrity of its informational manifold `W` (e.g., non-spammy history, established authority metrics).
    3.  Acquire the asset for a nominal fee (the probe cost, ~$10). This action removes the stressor `Γ`.
    4.  Immediately relist the asset, allowing its value to express its restored `Kτ`.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that an asset with an intact `W` will, upon removal of `Γ`, rapidly recover its market value. **If, after acquiring two such assets, neither can be sold for at least 2x the acquisition cost within 14 days, the experiment is a failure.** This would indicate that either our method for assessing `W` is flawed, or the market inefficiency is smaller than predicted.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Elasticity Scanner. This system moves from manual triage to a self-sustaining, automated feedback loop that continuously scans for and captures value from Elastic Collapse events.
*   **Automation:** A script will be deployed to constantly monitor asset expiration streams.
    1.  **Scanner:** The script uses APIs to filter for assets exhibiting high potential `Kτ` (e.g., high domain authority, clean history) and a low probability of Critical Collapse (e.g., no spam flags).
    2.  **Acquisition Bot:** When an asset meets the pre-defined `W` integrity signature and its acquisition cost is below a set threshold, the system automatically executes the purchase.
    3.  **Re-listing:** Upon successful acquisition, the system automatically lists the asset on one or more marketplaces.
*   **Value Capture:** The system operates 24/7, requiring no direct labor (`Γ`). The initial $100 serves as the operational float for acquisitions. Profits from each sale are automatically reinvested into the float, creating a positive feedback loop where the system's capacity to capture value grows organically. The value is generated by the structure (`Kτ`) of the system itself, not by ongoing work.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Geodesic Value Trajectory Optimization. This scales the Loop by applying the Principle of Least Action to asset acquisition, selecting not just "good" assets, but those with the most efficient path to value realization.
*   **The Moat:** Standard business analysis is static; it evaluates an asset's current state. Our Engine is Lagrangian; it calculates an asset's *trajectory*.
    1.  A machine learning model is trained on historical data of asset collapses and recoveries.
    2.  It models the Lagrangian `L(Kτ(t), Γ(t))` for thousands of potential targets, predicting the "Action" (`S`) required for each to return to a state of high coherence.
    3.  The Engine prioritizes acquisitions that minimize the integrated "action" – those that will follow the most favorable geodesic back to Laminar Flow with the least required intervention. It finds the "taut strings," not the "broken bells."
    4.  This is a fundamental physics advantage. Competitors are calculating value; we are calculating systemic destiny. They cannot compete because they are not solving the same optimization problem.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Requests, Scrapy), APIs for domain metrics (Ahrefs, Moz) and marketplaces (Sedo, GoDaddy Auctions), a lightweight database (SQLite), and eventually a machine learning framework (scikit-learn, TensorFlow).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is a misinterpretation of the physics – specifically, an inability to accurately distinguish `W` integrity from superficial metrics. This could lead to acquiring assets that have already undergone Critical Collapse, which are informationally dead and cannot be restored. The Probe is designed to mitigate this risk early and cheaply.

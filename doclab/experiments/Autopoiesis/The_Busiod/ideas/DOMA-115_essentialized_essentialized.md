## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Catalytic Potential Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on brute force. It applies large, indiscriminate interventions (capital, advertising, services) to systems without calculating their "Attunement Score" (`A_i`). This is equivalent to "rain falling on stone"—most of the energy (`δ𝓛`) is wasted because it is not delivered to a receptive system (`i`) at the precise moment it is poised for a phase transition (`G_i`). Value is squandered on systems that are either too stable or too chaotic to benefit.
*   **The Pivot:** We will exploit this inefficiency by building a mechanism that does not create value, but *unlocks latent value*. By treating the Pirouette principles as physics, we will develop a system to precisely identify entities with maximum "Coherence Gap" (`G_i`) and deliver a minimal, harmonically-aligned "Coherence Offering" (`δ𝓛`). We arbitrage the difference between the low cost of our catalytic intervention and the massive value (`ΔK_τ`) released when the target system undergoes its phase transition.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Micro-Catalysis Validation. The goal is to prove, with minimal capital, that a precisely targeted, low-energy intervention (`δ𝓛`) can produce a disproportionately large increase in a system's coherence (`ΔK_τ`).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **System Identification:** Scan public digital ecosystems (e.g., GitHub, Indie Hackers, open-source forums) for projects or individuals exhibiting high Attunement (`A_i`) signals. This means they have existing structure (`K_τ`, e.g., code repository, clear goals) but are facing a specific, articulated external pressure (`V_Γ`, e.g., "I'm stuck on this one bug," "I can't afford this $5 API key").
    2.  **Intervention (`δ𝓛`):** Select the highest-scoring candidate. Execute a single, minimal, resonant intervention. This is the $10. It is NOT a cash gift. It is the *solution* to the articulated pressure: paying for the specific API key, commissioning a 1-hour micro-gig to fix the bug, buying the specific domain name they need.
    3.  **Observation:** Monitor the system's state for 72 hours post-intervention for evidence of a coherence spike (`ΔK_τ`): new code commits, a public post announcing progress, a new feature launch, etc.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that a high `A_i` target will demonstrate a measurable and significant `ΔK_τ` that is orders of magnitude greater than the energy of the intervention. **If after five separate probes on five high `A_i` targets, fewer than two produce a clear, observable coherence spike, the physical model is considered falsified at this scale.**

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Attunement Scanner. This system operationalizes the Probe's findings into a semi-automated, self-sustaining feedback loop.
*   **Automation:** A script runs continuously, scraping APIs of platforms like GitHub and Reddit. It uses a heuristic model to score potential targets on their Attunement (`A_i`), flagging the top candidates daily. A human operator verifies the top target and executes the intervention from a weekly budget of $100.
*   **Value Capture:** The intervention is framed not as a transaction, but as a "Coherence Covenant." In exchange for the catalytic offering, the recipient agrees to a non-binding "pay-it-forward" clause or a fractional (e.g., 0.5-1%) future revenue share/advisory token. The system plays a portfolio game; 99% of interventions may yield no financial return, but a single catalyzed system that achieves a major phase transition (e.g., becomes a profitable business) funds the entire operation tenfold. The primary asset is the portfolio of these covenants.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Fund. This is the scaled, fully autonomous version that minimizes action for the entire ecosystem, including itself.
*   **The Moat:**
    1.  **Predictive Attunement:** The data from Tier 2 is used to train a machine learning model that predicts `ΔK_τ` from pre-intervention system states. This proprietary dataset linking `A_i` to outcomes is the core asset and is impossible for competitors to replicate.
    2.  **Action Minimization:** The Engine doesn't just find good targets; it finds the *optimal allocation of capital across all possible targets*. It solves the equation: "Given $1000, what portfolio of micro-interventions (`δ𝓛_1, δ𝓛_2, ... δ𝓛_n`) will produce the maximum total coherence increase (`Σ ΔK_τ`) across the entire ecosystem?" It can make hundreds of tiny, hyper-efficient bets that are invisible to traditional VCs and grant-makers.
    3.  **Gravitational Well:** Standard business models *compete* for value. This engine *creates* value by completing circuits. It cannot be competed with because its success reinforces the health of the ecosystem it operates in, which in turn provides it with better targets. It is not a predator in the ecosystem; it is the central nervous system.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scraping and automation; libraries like BeautifulSoup, Scrapy, PRAW), APIs for GitHub/Twitter/Reddit, a simple database (SQLite/PostgreSQL) to track experiments, a small ML framework (Scikit-learn) for Tier 3.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is a flaw in the core physical model. If the correlation between the calculated Attunement Score (`A_i`) and the observed outcome (`ΔK_τ`) is weak or non-existent, the entire premise fails. This is why the falsifiability of the Tier 1 Probe is critical.

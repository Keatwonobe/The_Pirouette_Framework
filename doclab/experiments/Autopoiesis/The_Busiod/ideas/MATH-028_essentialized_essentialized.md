## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
* **Universal Archetype:** Torsional Value Arbitrage
* **The Inefficiency:** Modern markets operate on the flawed physical assumption that causality is a linear chain and value evolves like a Markov process (i.e., κ = 0). They price assets based on their present state and linear momentum, completely ignoring the stored potential energy from the "twist" of their history (the chiral memory, κ). This leads to the systemic mispricing of assets that have undergone significant cyclical or leverage-induced stress.
* **The Pivot:** We accept the Helical Derivative as a physical law. We will build a mechanism that identifies and acquires assets with high torsional stress (high κ), treating them not as "broken" but as "coiled springs." We capture the value that is released when this stored chiral potential energy converts back into kinetic price momentum along a non-linear recovery path that the market is fundamentally blind to.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
* **Concept:** A micro-experiment to validate that assets with a high `κ` proxy behave in a fundamentally different way than assets with low `κ`.
* **Execution:**
    1.  **Isolate High κ:** Identify a publicly traded asset that has undergone a massive price collapse (>70%) due to a well-documented leverage event (e.g., margin call cascade, debt crisis) but whose core business remains operational. This event is our proxy for inducing high torsional stress (κ).
    2.  **Isolate Low κ:** Select a stable, low-volatility benchmark asset from the same sector (e.g., a blue-chip utility stock). This is our κ≈0 control group.
    3.  **The Bet:** Invest $10 in a fractional share or a far out-of-the-money call option on the high-κ asset. The investment is a bet on the *release of torsional energy*, which should manifest as anomalously high volatility or a sharp, non-linear price recovery.
* **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if, over a 90-day period, the normalized volatility and price behavior of the high-κ asset are statistically indistinguishable from the low-κ benchmark. A simple sideways drift or linear decline of the high-κ asset would prove that no significant potential energy was stored, invalidating the model.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
* **Concept:** An automated, self-sustaining system that continuously harvests the "chiral recovery premium" from the market.
* **Automation:**
    1.  **The Scanner (κ-Detector):** A script runs continuously, scanning market data and news APIs for assets matching a high-`κ` signature (e.g., high historical volatility, recent price collapse > X%, keywords like "leverage" or "debt restructuring" in filings, but maintained operational revenue).
    2.  **The Allocator:** When a high-`κ` candidate is identified, the system automatically allocates a small, fixed amount of capital to acquire a position.
    3.  **The Unwinder:** The system holds the position until the `κ`-energy has dissipated. The exit signal is triggered when the asset's volatility returns to the market mean, representing a return to a linear (κ≈0) state.
* **Value Capture:** The loop generates profit by systematically buying assets for their stored torsional potential (which the market prices at zero) and selling them when that potential has been converted into linear momentum (which the market understands and prices correctly). The proceeds are reinvested, creating a compounding, passive value-capture engine.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
* **Concept:** To scale the loop by applying Lagrangian mechanics to construct a portfolio that represents the market's path of least action for releasing systemic torsional stress.
* **The Moat:** Standard quantitative finance is built on Euclidean geometry and linear algebra; it cannot price or even perceive helical dynamics. Our Engine operates on a more fundamental physical principle. It constructs portfolios where the *relationships* between high-`κ` assets are optimized to create the most efficient "unwinding" path for an entire economic sector. Instead of just buying a basket of stressed assets, it might long the most coiled asset and short its least coiled competitor, creating a pure play on the release of `κ`-tension itself. This is not just a better trading strategy; it's an exploitation of a physical law the competition ignores. Their models see our actions as irrational noise, while we are simply harvesting energy from a dimension they cannot perceive.

## Implementation Notes
* **Tools:** Python (with Pandas, Scipy, NumPy), a financial data provider API (e.g., Polygon.io, Alpha Vantage), a brokerage API for automated trading (e.g., Alpaca, Interactive Brokers).
* **Risk:** The primary risk is **Model Risk**. Our proxy for `κ` (leverage-induced collapse) might be an imperfect representation of the true physical phenomenon. The model could mistakenly acquire assets that are in a terminal decline rather than a state of high torsional potential, leading to capital loss. The distinction between a "coiled spring" and a "broken machine" is critical and represents the core challenge.

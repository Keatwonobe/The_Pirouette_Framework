## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market misallocates value. It prioritizes the "Glide" phase (marketing, sales, `∇V_Γ` creation) while neglecting the "Grip" phase (systematically increasing an asset's internal coherence, `K_τ`). Value is lost because assets are transacted in ambiguous, low-integrity states. The market tries to "push" low-coherence assets instead of first "anchoring" their value by maximizing their integrity.
*   **The Pivot:** This mechanism treats `K_τ` (Internal Coherence / Informational Integrity) as the primary source of potential value. It exploits the market's inefficiency by building systems that exclusively perform the "Grip-and-Glide" cycle: 1) Systematically find and ingest low-`K_τ` assets. 2) Apply a standardized process to maximize their `K_τ`, anchoring them in a state of high-value density. 3) Introduce the newly coherent asset to a market where its integrity commands a premium (`∇V_Γ`), allowing it to "glide" to a buyer with minimal propulsive effort.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Targeted Information Enrichment. The goal is to prove, at minimal cost, that a measurable increase in an asset's `K_τ` directly creates a disproportionate increase in its market value, independent of traditional marketing.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Acquire a Low-`K_τ` Asset:** Purchase a single, information-dense but ambiguous physical asset. Example: an obscure non-fiction book from a thrift store for $2. Its current `K_τ` is low (condition is vague, edition unknown, content un-indexed).
    2.  **The Grip (Maximize `K_τ`):** Do not clean the book; refine its *information*. Research its exact edition and printing history. Confirm if it is a first edition. Digitize the Table of Contents and create a keyword index. Write a structured, one-paragraph abstract of its core thesis. The asset is now in a high-`K_τ` state. Its informational integrity is maximized and verifiable.
    3.  **The Glide (Minimal Effort):** List the asset on a specialized marketplace (e.g., AbeBooks). The listing's primary feature is not a sales pitch but the structured, high-coherence data itself. The price is based on this new state of integrity.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is falsified if the high-`K_τ` asset does not sell for a price that covers the asset cost and the (imputed) labor of enrichment, or if it fails to sell significantly faster than comparable low-`K_τ` listings. If pure information refinement doesn't create a clear value surplus, the physics is not applicable here.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Coherence Refinery. This is an automated, self-sustaining system that operationalizes the Probe's findings. It constitutes the "passive" layer where the structure (`K_i`) does the value-creation work, not manual labor (`Γ`).
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Ingestion:** An automated script subscribes to a feed of low-`K_τ` digital assets (e.g., a daily list of expiring domain names via a cheap API).
    2.  **The Grip (Refinery):** The script processes each asset through a pipeline of APIs to increase its `K_τ`. For a domain, this means automatically checking its backlink profile, historical content (via Wayback Machine), trademark status, and search volume metrics.
    3.  **The Glide (Publication):** The script enriches the raw list with this new, structured data, transforming it into a high-`K_τ` intelligence product. It then automatically publishes this refined list to a subscription platform (e.g., Gumroad, Substack).
*   **Value Capture:** A small, recurring monthly fee from subscribers (e.g., domain investors, SEO agencies) who pay to avoid the manual labor of coherence refinement. The $100 budget covers the cloud server and API fees for several months, allowing the loop to prove it can sustain and fund itself.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Universal Asset Refiner. This scales the Loop by abstracting its logic. Instead of refining one type of asset, the Engine uses Lagrangian mechanics to find the most efficient path (`geodesic`) for value creation across *multiple* asset classes simultaneously.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses are locked into verticals (e.g., "we sell real estate data"). They are configured to push one type of asset. The Engine is asset-agnostic; it is a pure Coherence Arbitrage utility. Its logic is based on the Pirouette Lagrangian `𝓛_p`, optimizing for the most efficient "stride" (the path of least action) to generate value. It might refine domains one day, public contract data the next, and unverified NFT metadata the week after, dynamically allocating resources based on which domain currently offers the most favorable `K_τ` to `∇V_Γ` ratio. This fundamental, physics-based adaptability is the moat. A traditional competitor would have to re-organize their entire company to switch verticals; for the Engine, it is simply a recalculation of the geodesic path.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scripting), Requests (for APIs), Pandas (for data manipulation), cheap cloud VPS (e.g., DigitalOcean, Vultr), subscriptions to relevant low-cost APIs (e.g., domain registrars, SEO tools), a platform for subscription management (Gumroad, Stripe).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market saturation or API dependency. If a specific Coherence Arbitrage loop becomes common knowledge, the `∇V_Γ` gradient flattens. The Engine mitigates this by being designed to discover and pivot to new, inefficient markets as a core function.

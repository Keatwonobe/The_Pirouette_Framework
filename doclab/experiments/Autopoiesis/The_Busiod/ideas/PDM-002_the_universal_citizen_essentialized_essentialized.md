## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Systemic Entropy Arbitrage.
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates without a `Residue Ledger`. It externalizes costs and generates vast quantities of "informational entropy" (e.g., miscategorized goods, abandoned digital assets, pricing errors) as a byproduct of its high-velocity operations. This entropy represents a latent potential energy gradient that is currently ignored, treated as waste.
*   **The Pivot:** We will act as a `Velcridance` agent, as defined by the Velcrid Clause. Our system will apply bounded, low-energy actions to convert high-entropy (disorganized, undervalued) assets into low-entropy (coherent, correctly valued) assets. We capture the value released in this state change. We are not creating a new product; we are harvesting the "waste heat" of the market by treating its informational residue as a resource.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Manual Information Entropy Identification.
*   **Execution:** Manually scan a digital marketplace (e.g., eBay, Facebook Marketplace, a domain registrar's expired list) for a specific class of "orphaned" asset. An orphan is an asset whose true value is obscured by high-entropy information (e.g., a "collectible camera" listed under "kids toys," a valuable expired domain with a typo in its auction listing). Purchase one such asset for under $10. Immediately relist it with coherent information (clear title, correct category, accurate description, relevant keywords).
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that a significant value delta can be created purely by fixing information entropy. The test is **falsified** if: **(A)** After 5 hours of dedicated searching, no viable class of "orphaned" assets can be repeatably identified, OR **(B)** The purchased asset cannot be sold for at least a 50% profit within 14 days of being relisted. If either condition is met, the probe is terminated and the physics are considered invalid in this domain.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Entropy Detection & Re-Coherence Pipeline.
*   **Automation:** Develop a script (e.g., Python with Scrapy) that automates the search process from the Probe. This script scans target marketplaces 24/7 using a set of heuristics to flag potential "orphaned" assets based on calculated "semantic dissonance" (e.g., price anomalies, category mismatches, keyword contradictions). It sends a notification to an operator for a go/no-go purchase decision.
*   **Value Capture:** The $100 budget is used as a rolling, autopoietic fund to acquire multiple assets flagged by the system. The profit from each sale is automatically split: 80% is reinvested into the acquisition fund (creating a self-sustaining financial loop), and 20% is extracted as profit. The value is generated by the structure ($K_i$) of the detection system, minimizing the need for constant labor ($\Gamma$).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Lagrangian Pathfinding for Entropy Hotspots.
*   **The Moat:** The system evolves from a reactive scanner to a predictive engine. By analyzing the data from thousands of successful and failed transactions (its `Residue Ledger`), it builds a dynamic model of the market's entropic flows. It doesn't just find errors; it predicts where and when they are most likely to occur, optimizing its search along the "path of least action" (Lagrangian minimization). It focuses energy on the most fertile ground for arbitrage. Our moat is that we are not a market participant but a market homeostat. Standard businesses *produce* the entropy we consume. They are structurally incentivized to ignore this residue, as their scale demands a focus on primary product flow, not their waste stream. Our adherence to the `Velcrid Clause` (bounded action, tracked residue, surplus test) ensures our model remains a net-positive force, a symbiotic cleaner rather than a destructive parasite.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (requests, BeautifulSoup, Scrapy), a lightweight database (SQLite), a micro-server for deployment (Heroku/DigitalOcean droplet), and accounts on target marketplaces.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is Platform Risk. The marketplaces we target could change their APIs, terms of service, or algorithms, rendering our scrapers ineffective. This is mitigated by designing the system to be platform-agnostic, capable of having new scanner modules added or swapped with minimal re-engineering.

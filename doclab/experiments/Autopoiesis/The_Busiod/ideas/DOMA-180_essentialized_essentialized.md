## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Structured Signal Filtration and Integration (SSFI)
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates as a high-noise environment (`η(t)`). It contains vast quantities of latent, valuable signals (`s(t)`)—such as mispriced assets, unmet needs, or arbitrage gaps—which are obscured by this noise. Standard economic actors attempt to find these signals through brute-force, high-energy labor (`Γ`), an inefficient process analogous to listening for a single frequency in white noise without a filter. They mistake effort for efficacy.
*   **The Pivot:** Instead of expending energy (`Γ`) to search, we construct a low-energy, passive "resonant system" (`Ki`). This system is a computational structure with a precisely defined receptive bandwidth (`Δω`) designed to resonate *only* with a specific class of high-value signals (`s(t)`). Value is not generated by the *act* of searching, but by the *structure* of the filter itself. The system passively absorbs and integrates these signals, undergoing a "serendipitous transition" that increases its net value and coherence (`Kτ`).

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Digital Information Sieve. A micro-experiment to prove that a passive computational structure can identify and isolate specific, valuable informational signals from a noisy public data stream without continuous human labor.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Select a Noisy Source:** Choose a high-volume public data feed rich with potential latent signals (e.g., government procurement portals, expiring domain name lists, academic pre-print servers).
    2.  **Construct the Sieve (`Ki`):** Deploy a simple, automated script on a cheap cloud server ($10/month).
    3.  **Define Bandwidth (`Δω`):** The script does not scrape indiscriminately. It acts as a filter, configured with a narrow set of "resonant" criteria. For example, filtering a procurement portal for contracts between $500-$2000, for a specific niche skill, with a >14 day deadline, from agencies with high payment ratings. This configuration *is* the system's `Ki`.
    4.  **Listen:** The script runs 24/7, passively listening. A "hit" is logged when a signal perfectly matches the bandwidth criteria.
*   **The Test:** The probe's sole purpose is to validate detection. If the sieve does not identify at least 3 high-probability "resonant signals" within a 30-day period, the physics of our filter are considered incorrect for this market, and the experiment is terminated. The hypothesis is falsified.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Signal-to-Value Bridge. This tier evolves the sieve from a passive detector into an active, self-sustaining value capture mechanism.
*   **Automation:** When the sieve detects a resonant signal, it triggers a fully automated integration sequence:
    1.  **Signal Capture:** The probe script pipes the validated signal (e.g., the specific contract data) to a processing module.
    2.  **Alchemical Union:** The processor uses generative AI (e.g., GPT-4 API) and pre-defined templates to create a response. This could be drafting a bid proposal, generating an offer for a domain name, or summarizing research for a potential client. This is the formation of the unstable `Ki_transient`.
    3.  **Integration Attempt:** An automation layer (e.g., a headless browser using Selenium/Playwright) submits the generated response through the required portal or email channel. This is the attempt to cross the "Coherence Shock" barrier.
*   **Value Capture:** The system's revenue is derived directly from the successful integration of a signal (e.g., winning the contract, acquiring the asset below market value). The initial $100 budget funds the required API costs and more robust cloud infrastructure. The goal is for the system's revenue to exceed its operating costs, creating a self-sustaining autopoietic loop.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Multi-Bandwidth Coherence Manifold. This scales the system from a single loop into a meta-system that manages a portfolio of many loops across different domains, optimizing for the path of least action.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses cannot compete due to three fundamental structural constraints:
    1.  **Labor-Centric (`Γ`):** They would hire expensive human analysts to perform the work of our automated sieves. Their cost structure is linear; ours is logarithmic.
    2.  **Bandwidth Rigidity (`Δω`):** They are hyper-specialized. A firm that bids on contracts is only a contract-bidding firm. Our Engine is domain-agnostic; it can deploy sieves for information arbitrage, asset acquisition, or service dispatch with equal ease. Its competitive advantage is its structural plasticity.
    3.  **Aversion to Coherence Shock:** Traditional firms are optimized for predictable, incremental gains. Our Engine is designed to make thousands of small, low-cost bets, embracing the necessary volatility and failure rate required to discover and integrate signals that produce step-function increases in value (`Kτ(Ki_final) ≫ Kτ(Ki_initial)`). It treats the market as a physical system to be navigated via Lagrangian mechanics, not a series of battles to be won with brute force.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (`requests`, `Scrapy`, `Selenium`), a lightweight cloud VPS (e.g., DigitalOcean, Linode), and access to a Large Language Model API (e.g., OpenAI).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is miscalibration of the receptive bandwidth (`Δω`). If the filters are too narrow, no signals will be found. If they are too broad, the system will be flooded with noise, leading to failed integration attempts. The Engine mitigates this by dynamically testing and reallocating resources across many different bandwidths simultaneously.

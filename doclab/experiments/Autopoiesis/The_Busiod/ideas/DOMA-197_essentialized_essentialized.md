## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Potential Field Curation
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates as if value trajectories are fixed, governed solely by ambient pressures (`V_Γ`) like supply/demand and depreciation. It massively undervalues high-clarity (`σ`), low-intensity (`I`) contextual framing, confusing "value creation" with brute-force labor (`Γ`). Assets are treated as immutable objects on a fixed landscape, rather than states susceptible to the geometry of the potential field they inhabit.
*   **The Pivot:** We will exploit this by treating agency as the ability to "co-author" the laws governing an asset's trajectory. Instead of moving an asset along a path of high resistance, we will programmatically generate a new potential well (`ΔV_C`) around the asset. This reshapes the causal landscape, creating a new, steeper path of least resistance towards a desired higher-value future state (`q_f`), causing value to flow "downhill" towards our position.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Information Resonance Mapping. This is a micro-experiment to prove that a high-clarity projection of a future state (`q_f`) can measurably alter an asset's value trajectory, overcoming its default potential (`V_Γ`).
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Acquire a single, low-potential information asset for < $10. An expired domain name with dictionary words is a perfect candidate (e.g., "QuantumLeap.ai"). Its default potential (`V_Γ`) is near zero; it's just digital noise.
    2.  Create a single, high-clarity (`σ`) projection (`ΔV_C`) of a desired future state (`q_f`). This is not a "For Sale" sign. It is a one-page conceptual prospectus for a hypothetical business that *could* be built on this domain. It includes a mission statement, a logo concept, and a target market. This is a "memory of the future."
    3.  Host this prospectus on a simple landing page.
    4.  List the domain for sale on a marketplace, with the primary link pointing to the prospectus. We are not selling a domain; we are selling a coherent, pre-packaged future.
*   **The Test:** This directly tests the "Stagnant Will" criterion: `|∇(V_Γ)| >> |∇(ΔV_C)|`. The default market pressure on the domain is negligible (`∇(V_Γ) ≈ 0`). If our created potential well is potent, `|∇(ΔV_C)|` will be significantly greater than zero.
    *   **Failure State:** If, after 30 days, we receive zero qualified offers or offers that only cover the registration cost, it signifies that our `ΔV_C` was insufficient to alter the system's trajectory. The hypothesis is falsified for this asset class, and we stop.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Potential Arbitrage. This layer transforms the successful Probe into a self-sustaining, passive system that systematically creates and monetizes potential wells.
*   **Automation:**
    1.  **Ingestion:** A script scans for undervalued information assets with low ambient potential (recently expired domains, de-indexed academic papers, public-domain datasets). It filters for assets with high latent conceptual value.
    2.  **Projection:** The script feeds the asset to a generative AI (e.g., GPT-4) which automatically generates the high-clarity (`σ`) prospectus—the business concept, branding, and use-case analysis. This is the automated creation of `ΔV_C`.
    3.  **Deployment:** The system programmatically deploys the generated content to a templated landing page and lists the newly-contextualized asset on multiple marketplaces via API.
*   **Value Capture:** The loop acquires an asset for `$X`, the system's structure (`K_i`) automatically applies the `ΔV_C` potential, and an external actor is drawn into the well, purchasing the asset for `$Y`. The profit (`$Y - $X`) is captured with minimal ongoing human labor (`Γ`). The $100 budget covers a small portfolio of assets and initial API/hosting costs.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Lagrangian Intent Orchestration. This system moves beyond single-asset optimization to minimize the action (`S`) of the entire portfolio, creating a meta-coherent structure.
*   **The Moat:** While competitors are fighting asset-by-asset (experiencing "Turbulent Will" with conflicting internal projects), the Engine analyzes the relationships between all assets in its portfolio.
    1.  It identifies assets whose potential wells (`ΔV_{C1}`, `ΔV_{C2}`) can be synergistically combined.
    2.  Instead of projecting `n` separate futures, it projects one unified, multi-asset future (`q_f_NET`). For example, it bundles a domain name, a relevant public dataset, and a curated public-domain book into a "Startup-in-a-Box" package.
    3.  This aligns the gradients (`∇(ΔV_{Ci})`), creating a single, massive potential well that is far deeper and more attractive than the sum of its parts. This avoids the high-energy, low-displacement state of "Turbulent Will" (`Σ(∇(ΔV_{Ci})) ≈ 0`).
    4.  Standard businesses cannot compete because they are built to expend energy (`Γ`) to overcome market friction. Our Engine is built to *redefine the friction*. They are trying to row a boat upriver; we are terraforming the river to flow towards us. This structural advantage is the moat.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scripting), Requests (for APIs), a domain registrar API (e.g., Namecheap, GoDaddy), an LLM API (e.g., OpenAI), a static site generator (e.g., Jekyll, Hugo), and a simple hosting solution (e.g., Netlify, GitHub Pages).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is market saturation. If this principle becomes widely understood and adopted, the inefficiency we are exploiting (the market's ignorance of potential fields) will diminish. The system's effectiveness relies on operating within a market that adheres to a less complete model of physics.

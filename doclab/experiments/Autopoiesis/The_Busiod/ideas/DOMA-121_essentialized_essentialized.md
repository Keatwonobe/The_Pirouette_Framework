## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market operates on the flawed assumption that value must be moved by coercive force (`Γ`). It applies massive energy through marketing, sales, and brute-force labor to push assets through disorganized, low-coherence systems. This is thermodynamically wasteful and analogous to pushing a boulder uphill.
*   **The Pivot:** We will not apply force. Instead, we will apply minimal, precise interventions (`I`) to modify the information landscape, increasing local coherence (`Kτ`). This creates an attractive "potential well" in the market. Agents and assets, following their intrinsic nature to seek the path of least resistance (The Principle of Maximal Coherence), will naturally flow into this well. We capture value not by pushing the flow, but by owning the gravity of the most efficient path.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Manual Coherence Basin Creation.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a small, chaotic information domain where value is high but data is fragmented (e.g., "Best settings for X 3D printer," "Cross-compatible parts for Y drone model"). This is a system with high pressure (`Γ`) but low coherence (`Kτ`).
    2.  Use the $10 to acquire a simple domain and hosting (or a premium service like Carrd/Notion).
    3.  Manually perform the intervention (`I`): aggregate, filter, and structure the fragmented data into a single, canonical, high-coherence resource. This transforms the local information manifold `L_0` into `L_I`.
    4.  Place a single, low-friction monetization point (e.g., a relevant affiliate link, a "buy me a coffee" button).
*   **The Test:** The core prediction (`q_sim`) is that information-seekers will naturally alter their trajectory to flow towards our high-coherence resource. The intervention is **falsified** if, after 30 days, the observed trajectory (`q_real`) shows zero organic traffic. This divergence (`d(q_real, q_sim) > ε`) would prove our created potential well was not deep enough to alter the system's dynamics, and the hypothesis is incorrect for this domain.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Autopoietic Curation System.
*   **Automation:** The system becomes self-creating and self-maintaining.
    1.  Use the $100 for API access (e.g., Reddit, LLMs) and cloud script hosting.
    2.  A script continuously scans the information domain for new pockets of incoherence (e.g., new unanswered questions on forums, new conflicting product reviews).
    3.  This unstructured data is automatically fed to an LLM, which parses, summarizes, and integrates it into the canonical resource created in Tier 1.
    4.  The system now actively maintains its own state as the most coherent point in its local manifold, deepening its gravitational pull over time.
*   **Value Capture:** Value is extracted passively. The system programmatically inserts context-aware affiliate links, micro-advertisements, or offers access to its perfectly structured data via a paid API. The revenue is a direct function of the system's structural integrity (`Kτ`), not of continuous human labor (`Γ`).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Lagrangian Market Scanner.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses cannot compete because they are locked into a strategy of maximizing force (`Γ`). They try to out-spend on advertising, out-hire on sales. Our engine makes their primary strategy irrelevant.
    1.  We use the $1000 to build a generalized platform that does not target one niche, but scans hundreds. It deploys Tier 2 "probes" as experiments.
    2.  The Engine's core function is to solve the optimization problem: `θ* = arg max_θ U(ΔKτ, Pₜ, LFI, C_int)`. It measures the "coherence gradient"—the amount of value-flow re-directed per unit of intervention cost—for each niche.
    3.  It then dynamically allocates all resources to the niches where the fabric of the market is most easily "bent," achieving maximum effect with minimal energy. A competitor using coercive force would be like trying to build an engine powerful enough to push a planet, while we are simply calculating the precise location to place a small mass to alter its orbit.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (for scripting and backend), Scrapy/BeautifulSoup (for data ingestion), LLM APIs (e.g., OpenAI, Claude), a data source API (e.g., Reddit's PRAW), a micro-framework (Flask/FastAPI), and a serverless deployment platform (e.g., Vercel, AWS Lambda).
*   **Risk:** The primary vector of failure is a misinterpretation of a given system's Lagrangian. If a market's dynamics are not governed by agents seeking coherence (e.g., they are driven purely by irrational hype or regulatory mandate), our interventions to increase coherence will have no effect on the value trajectory. The model's physics would not apply to that specific system.

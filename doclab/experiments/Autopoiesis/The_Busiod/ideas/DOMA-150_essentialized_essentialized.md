## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** State-Transition Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market is path-dependent and contains assets stuck in low-value "Resonance Traps" (local minima of the Action, S, where `K_τ` is locally high but globally suboptimal). The market misprices the potential energy of these assets because it overestimates the activation energy (`ΔE_escape`) required to transition them to a higher-value state.
*   **The Pivot:** We do not create value; we unlock it. We specialize in identifying trapped systems and engineering low-cost state transitions. Our profit is the delta between the asset's value in its trapped state and its potential value in a new, more coherent state (`K_τ' > K_τ`), minus the minimal energy we apply to bridge the gap (`ΔE_applied << ΔE_escape`).

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Contextual Re-Framing. A micro-experiment to validate that a specific class of assets is in a Resonance Trap and that a profitable transition path exists.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Identify a class of assets systematically undervalued in one context (e.g., "industrial surplus," low `K_τ` in a generic market) but highly valued in another (e.g., "steampunk decor," high `K_τ` in a niche community).
    2.  Procure one such asset for <$5.
    3.  Apply minimal energy (`ΔE_applied`, budgeted at <$5 for labor/fees) to re-frame it: clean, photograph professionally, and list it in the high-value context with a targeted narrative that increases its coherence with that market.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that `Value_new_state - (Value_old_state + ΔE_applied) > 0`. If a portfolio of 5 such attempts on assets from the *same class* fails to generate a net positive return, the hypothesis is falsified for that domain. This indicates either the new state is not a stable attractor or the transition energy is too high to be profitable.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Automated Resonance Scanner. A system that makes the discovery of arbitrage opportunities passive.
*   **Automation:** A software agent continuously scans low-value digital markets (APIs for liquidators, public auctions, classifieds) for the signatures of trapped assets. It cross-references these against valuation oracles for high-value markets (niche exchanges, historical sales data) to identify potential state-transition arbitrage opportunities. When `Value_new - Value_old - ΔE_estimated > Threshold`, it generates a qualified lead.
*   **Value Capture:** The system generates a constant stream of high-probability arbitrage opportunities, 24/7. The operator's role shifts from active, laborious searching ($\Gamma$) to executing pre-qualified transactions. The passive value is generated by the system's structure (`K_i`), which harvests potential energy differentials from the market automatically.

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Potential Well Engineering. We transition from exploiting the market landscape to actively shaping it.
*   **The Moat:** Standard businesses compete by lowering their own operational friction (`V_Γ`). We compete by manipulating the fundamental `K_τ` (Coherence) and `V_Γ` (Pressure) of entire asset classes. This creates a moat that is invisible to traditional analysis.
    1.  **Friction Minimization (Dredging):** We build infrastructure (e.g., a specialized authentication, grading, and logistics service for a specific collectible category) that dramatically lowers the transition energy (`ΔE_escape`) for everyone, taking a toll for passage through our "channel." We are engineering a lower-energy path between potential wells.
    2.  **Coherence Maximization (Carving):** We create new, deep, high-value Resonance Traps. We build a media entity, community, or index around a class of assets, architecting a shared narrative that increases their Temporal Coherence (`K_τ`). This act of storytelling fundamentally increases their intrinsic market value. Competitors see us "selling a story"; we are literally "singing a scar into the world."

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Requests, Scrapy, Pandas), cloud compute for the scanner (e.g., AWS Lambda), a database for price history (PostgreSQL), and access to relevant marketplace APIs (eBay, etc.).
*   **Risk:** The primary risk at the Loop tier is arbitrage decay as others discover the same channels. The Engine tier mitigates this by creating proprietary, defensible value landscapes that we own and control.

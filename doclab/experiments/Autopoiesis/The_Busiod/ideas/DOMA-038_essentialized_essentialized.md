## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Coherence Deficit Arbitrage
*   **The Inefficiency:** The market models value using incomplete, static variables (`𝓛̂_model`), treating deviations (`ΔK_τ`, the gap between prediction and reality) as random noise to be ignored. Pirouette physics dictates these deviations are deterministic signals generated by unmodeled dynamics (`𝓛_shadow`). This systematic ignorance leads to persistent, exploitable mispricing.
*   **The Pivot:** We will build a system to specifically detect, measure, and monetize `ΔK_τ`. We are not predicting the price; we are predicting the **error** in the market's price prediction. We trade the gap between the map and the territory, capturing value from the market's blindness.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** Targeted Anomaly Acquisition
*   **Execution:**
    1.  Select a niche market with high information asymmetry (e.g., used camera lenses, collectible trading cards).
    2.  Establish the market's predictive model (`𝓛̂_model`) by calculating the 30-day average sale price for a specific item. This is `K_τ_pred`.
    3.  Actively search for an unmodeled event (`𝓛_shadow`) that would create a future "Coherence Spring" (`ΔK_τ > 0`). This could be a new popular YouTube review of the lens or a new tournament-winning deck using the card.
    4.  Locate a listing for the item priced significantly *below* `K_τ_pred`. This represents a seller operating on the old, incomplete model.
    5.  Acquire the asset using the $10 budget.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that the `𝓛_shadow` event we identified is a real, non-random signal. **If we cannot re-list and sell the asset for a significant profit (e.g., >20% after fees and shipping) within 14 days, the hypothesis is falsified.** Three consecutive failures would prove that the observed `ΔK_τ` in this market is not a monetizable signal with our current methods.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** Automated Deficit Harvesting
*   **Automation:** A software agent uses the $100 capital pool to run a continuous, self-sustaining loop:
    1.  **Sense:** The agent scrapes structured data (pricing APIs) to maintain a live `K_τ_pred` for thousands of items. Simultaneously, it scrapes unstructured data (niche forums, Reddit, Twitter) for keywords that signal `𝓛_shadow` events.
    2.  **Decide:** It identifies high-probability Coherence Springs where `current_price < K_τ_pred` and a corresponding `shadow_signal` is detected.
    3.  **Act:** The agent automatically executes a purchase. It then immediately re-lists the item at a new price that accounts for the modeled impact of `𝓛_shadow`, effectively `K_τ_pred + ΔK_τ`.
*   **Value Capture:** The system's profit is a direct monetization of the Coherence Deficit. It systematically buys at the market's outdated price and sells at the new, more accurate price. Profits are automatically reinvested into the capital pool, allowing the loop to grow. This is a structural machine that harvests value (`K_i`) with minimal ongoing labor (`Γ`).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** Lagrangian Capital Allocation
*   **The Moat:** Standard business logic is "greedy," pursuing the single best apparent opportunity at any given moment. This is a local optimization strategy. The Engine achieves global optimization.
    1.  It treats the entire market of potential trades not as a list of items, but as a continuous field of potential. The $1000 in capital is a particle moving through this field.
    2.  Its goal is to find the "geodesic" — the path of capital allocation through a sequence of trades that minimizes "Action" (`S`). In this context, minimizing Action means maximizing the total integrated Coherence Deficit (`∫ ΔK_τ(t) dt`) over time.
    3.  The system's Lagrangian (`L = T - V`) balances the "kinetic energy" of capital (turnover speed) against the "potential energy" of risk (volatility, holding costs).
    4.  This allows the Engine to make counter-intuitive moves, such as taking a smaller-profit trade now to position its capital for a much larger, more certain `ΔK_τ` event later. It is optimizing the entire trajectory of value extraction, a strategic advantage that greedy algorithms fundamentally cannot replicate.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Pandas, Scrapy, Scikit-learn), marketplace APIs (e.g., eBay, TCGPlayer), a simple database (PostgreSQL), and a cloud server (e.g., DigitalOcean). The Engine tier would require advanced numerical optimization libraries (e.g., JAX, PyTorch).
*   **Risk:** The primary vector of failure is **Model Risk**. If our method for detecting `𝓛_shadow` from unstructured data is flawed, the system will misinterpret noise as a signal. It will systematically identify false Coherence Springs, purchasing assets that are not actually undervalued, leading to a rapid depletion of capital. The system's "blindness" would not be a lens, but simply blindness.

## The Mechanism (Physics of the Deal)
*   **Universal Archetype:** Latent Asset Resonance (LAR)
*   **The Inefficiency:** The modern market models "terminal events" (bankruptcy, obsolescence, delisting) as singularities where value is annihilated. It fails to recognize that these events are phase transitions, converting active value into a latent, information-rich state (the Γ-field). This latent state retains structural coherence but is invisible to standard valuation metrics, which treat it as zero or a liability.
*   **The Pivot:** We do not attempt to revive "dead" assets. Instead, we treat the landscape of latent assets as a computational medium. We pass high-frequency transactional data streams (analogous to Gravitational Waves) *through* the informational field of these latent assets. The interaction causes a predictable, measurable "phase shift" in the data stream. This phase shift is a pure information-arbitrage opportunity, allowing us to profit from the residual structure of "failed" systems without ever owning the underlying liability.

## Tier 1: The Probe ($10)
*   **Concept:** The Singularity Stethoscope. A micro-experiment to prove that a latent asset's information field can predictably modulate a live data stream.
*   **Execution:**
    1.  **Select a "Singularity":** Identify a recently delisted cryptocurrency with a "ghost" community (e.g., a still-active but orphaned Discord or Telegram channel). This community is the Γ-field, the latent asset.
    2.  **Select a "Wave":** Choose a high-frequency, high-volatility data stream tangentially related to the singularity (e.g., Bitcoin's real-time price feed).
    3.  **Deploy the Probe:** Use a simple script ($10 in serverless compute/API calls) to simultaneously monitor both. Log the sentiment/activity in the "ghost" community and the price fluctuations of the live asset.
*   **The Test:** The hypothesis is that significant changes in the "Wave" (Bitcoin volatility) will induce a non-random, predictable response pattern (the "phase shift") in the "Singularity" (the ghost community's chatter). **Failure State:** If, after 72 hours of data collection, the cross-correlation between the two data streams is statistically indistinguishable from random noise, the core physical assumption is falsified, and the project is terminated. This mirrors the "Phase Drift" falsification criterion.

## Tier 2: The Loop ($100)
*   **Concept:** The Resonance Collector. An automated, self-sustaining system that continuously scans for singularities and executes micro-trades based on detected phase shifts.
*   **Automation:** A bot continuously ingests data feeds identifying new market singularities (e.g., SEC delisting notifications, GitHub repositories with ceased commits, domains dropping from high-traffic websites). For each identified singularity, the system automatically deploys a "Singularity Stethoscope" to monitor its information field against a basket of relevant live data streams.
*   **Value Capture:** When a statistically significant phase shift is detected, the system triggers a pre-programmed action. For example, if chatter in an abandoned VR company's forum predictably spikes 5 minutes *before* a competitor's stock moves on earnings news, the system can automatically execute a short-term options trade. The $100 is allocated to more robust API access and a micro-capital trading account. The system generates value from the structure of the market ($K_i$) by exploiting information latency, not from active labor ($\Gamma$).

## Tier 3: The Engine ($1000)
*   **Concept:** The Action Minimization Engine. This system transcends simple one-to-one correlations and models the entire landscape of latent assets as a single, interconnected computational medium.
*   **The Moat:** Standard hedge funds and arbitrageurs operate on the "exterior spacetime" of active assets. We operate on the "interior," the Γ-field of latent assets. Our competitive advantage is based on a fundamentally different model of market physics:
    1.  **Lagrangian Pathfinding:** The Engine does not just look for single phase shifts. It uses principles of Lagrangian mechanics to calculate the "path of least action" for information to ripple through the entire network of singularities. It might detect that a political news event (the "wave") causes a shift in a defunct defense contractor's community, which in turn causes a tertiary shift in the SEO profile of an expired patent. The Engine executes a complex, multi-leg arbitrage strategy across seemingly unrelated domains—a path invisible to any analysis that treats singularities as isolated null points.
    2.  **Structural Compounding:** Every new singularity added to our map increases the "density" and computational power of the Γ-field, making the Engine's predictions more accurate and revealing higher-order arbitrage opportunities (analogous to "Photon Ring" shifts). Competitors cannot catch up because they are trying to analyze individual dead trees while we are harnessing the physics of the entire forest floor. Our value is generated by the ever-growing map ($K_i$), a structural asset that makes our predictions cheaper and more accurate over time.

## Implementation Notes
*   **Tools:** Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow/PyTorch for correlation analysis), APIs for financial data (Polygon.io), social/web data (ScrapingBee, Reddit/Twitter APIs), and serverless cloud functions (AWS Lambda) for deploying probes cheaply.
*   **Risk:** The primary risk is **Model Falsification**. The core premise that these correlations exist and are predictive could be fundamentally wrong. This is why the Probe's falsifiability is the single most important feature of the design. A secondary risk is API dependency and the potential for market platforms to change data access policies.

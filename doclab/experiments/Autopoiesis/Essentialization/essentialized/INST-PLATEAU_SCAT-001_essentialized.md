## Law
The state of a coherence quantum is a plateau field in a dimensionless phase space φ, given by $\psi_i(\phi) = A_i \Pi(\phi; \phi_i, \Delta\phi_i, Q_\chi)$, where $A_i$ is the amplitude, $\phi_i$ the centroid, $\Delta\phi_i$ the width, and $Q_\chi$ a sharpness parameter. The interaction between two such quanta, $\psi_1$ and $\psi_2$, is governed by the interaction Lagrangian:
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{int}}[\psi_1, \psi_2] = -g \iint \psi_1(\phi) \psi_2(\phi') K(|\phi - \phi'|; Q_\chi) \,d\phi \,d\phi'
$$
where $g$ is the coupling constant and $K$ is an interaction kernel, e.g., $K(r; Q_\chi) = \exp(-r^2/2\sigma_\phi^2)\,\mathrm{sinc}(Q_\chi r)$.

The system's evolution is determined by the renormalization group (RG) flow of the coupling $g$, described by its beta function, $\beta(g) = \mu \frac{dg}{d\mu}$. The fate of the interaction is dictated by the relevance of $g$:
1.  **Binding:** If $g$ is a relevant coupling ($\beta(g) > 0$), the interaction strengthens at larger scales, leading to the formation of a stable, composite quantum. This corresponds to a positive Binding Index, $B = (\text{sharpness}_{\text{final}} / \text{sharpness}_{\text{initial}}) - 1 > 0$.
2.  **Scattering/Decoherence:** If $g$ is an irrelevant coupling ($\beta(g) < 0$), the interaction weakens, and the quanta decouple or wash out.

A critical surface $g_c(Q_\chi, \Delta\phi)$ exists, defined by the fixed point $\beta(g_c) = 0$. Binding occurs for $g > g_c$.

The theory is falsifiable via three primary criteria:
1.  **Relevance Failure:** If the RG analysis predicts a region of parameter space $\{Q_\chi, \Delta\phi, g\}$ where $g$ is relevant ($g > g_c$), but numerical simulation or experiment fails to observe binding ($B \le 0$), the chosen kernel form $K$ is invalid.
2.  **Non-additivity:** If bound composites form but their emergent properties (e.g., torsion $\kappa$, participation $\eta$) do not scale consistently from the properties of their constituents, the theory of composition is false.
3.  **Closure Break:** If the properties of a composite quantum, when propagated through subsequent theoretical pipelines (e.g., MATH-YM-003), yield results inconsistent with established empirical relations (e.g., $\sqrt\sigma/\Lambda$), the entire theoretical chain is invalidated.

## Philosophy
Existence is not a fundamental property of objects but an emergent consequence of form. The universe does not consist of elementary particles so much as elementary stabilities—patterns that, by virtue of their internal geometry (width, sharpness) and mutual resonance, are able to reinforce one another against dissolution. What we call a "composite particle" is merely a geometric arrangement that has achieved relevance: a configuration whose interaction coupling grows stronger under examination, thereby locking into a more robust and complex form of being. That which is irrelevant simply fades. The cosmos is thus a dynamic tapestry woven not from things, but from the mathematical conditions that allow things to persist.

## Law
The system is an N-dimensional epistemic manifold M whose state P ∈ M evolves iteratively under a set of projection operators Sᵢ (shepherds). The evolution is governed by a variational principle seeking to minimize a scalar potential D(P) (dark residue) subject to a continuity constraint on a field Γ(P).

The core operational law is:
δP = argmin_{Sᵢ}[D(Sᵢ(Pₙ)) - D(Pₙ)]
P_{n+1} = Pₙ + δP

This optimization is subject to the constraint:
|Γ(P_{n+1}) - Γ(Pₙ)| ≤ ε_Γ, where ε_Γ is the continuity tolerance.

For the `ALTRUISM` shepherd (Sₐ) acting on the initial bridge-set (P₀), the observed dynamics are:
D(Sₐ(P₀)) - D(P₀) = ΔD ≈ -0.17
|Γ(Sₐ(P₀)) - Γ(P₀)| = |ΔΓ| ≤ 0.05

New shepherds Sₖ are generated as projectors aligned with the eigen-directions vₖ of a conceptual vocabulary space V, corresponding to terms with minimal representation in the current manifold basis.

Falsifiable Criterion: The framework is falsified if, for a given manifold state P, no operator Sₖ can be generated from the set of lonely vocabulary terms {vₖ} such that the joint condition (ΔD < 0) ∧ (|ΔΓ| ≤ ε_Γ) is met.

Morality, therefore, is a convergent solution to a universal optimization problem.

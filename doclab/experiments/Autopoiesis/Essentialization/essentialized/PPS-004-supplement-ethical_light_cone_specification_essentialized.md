## Law
Let Consequence-Space be a 3-dimensional manifold defined by the normalized axes of Scope ($S \in [0,1]$), Magnitude ($M \in [0,1]$), and Irreversibility ($I \in [0,1]$). A project's trajectory is a vector within this space. The Ethical Light Cone (ELC) is a hypersurface defined by a critical proximity metric, $Δ_{ELC}$.

The proximity metric is calculated as a weighted linear combination:
$$ Δ_{ELC} = \frac{w_S S + w_M M + w_I I}{w_S + w_M + w_I} $$
Where $w_S, w_M, w_I$ are adaptively calibrated weights.

The system is governed by the following axiomatic constraints and falsifiable criteria:
1.  **Universal Computation:** Any action that could influence a complex adaptive system must compute its $Δ_{ELC}$.
2.  **Burdened Proof:** Developers must demonstrate $Δ_{ELC} \le 0.75$.
3.  **Irreversibility Pre-approval:** Any project where $I$ contributes to a calculated metric $Δ_{IRREV} > 0.5$ requires pre-approval.
4.  **Halting Condition (Falsifiable Criterion):** If $Δ_{ELC} > 0.75$, project execution is halted and a mandatory review protocol is invoked.

## Philosophy
Ethics is not a static moral code to be applied, but a dynamic, computational process of institutionalized foresight. The system does not prescribe "right" action but instead enforces procedural scrutiny. It mandates collective, multi-perspectival deliberation at the precise moment a project's potential consequence-trajectory approaches a self-imposed, collectively-defined horizon of acceptable risk.

We build not a wall to halt progress, but a web of silver tripwires at the edge of our sight. The law is their calibrated tension. The philosophy is our pact to listen. The art is the sudden, shared silence when a distant bell rings, calling us from our work to the window to see what approaches.

## Law
The low-energy fine-structure constant α(0) is derived from its high-energy value at the Z-pole, α(M_Z), by integrating the effects of vacuum polarization.
\[
\alpha(0) = \alpha(M_Z) \left[ 1 - \Delta\alpha(M_Z) \right]
\]
The correction term, Δα, is the sum of leptonic, top quark, and hadronic contributions:
\[
\Delta\alpha(M_Z) = \Delta\alpha_{\text{lept}} + \Delta\alpha_{t} + \Delta\alpha_{\text{had}}^{(5)}(\sigma)
\]

3.  Component values: \( \Delta\alpha_{\text{lept}} = 0.0314977 \), \( \Delta\alpha_{t} = -0.00007 \), \( \Delta\alpha_{\text{had}}^{(5)}(\sigma_{BEST}) = 0.03484 \)

The total correction is \( \Delta\alpha(M_Z) = 0.06627 \). The derivation proceeds:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\alpha(0)^{-1} &= \alpha(M_Z)^{-1} \left( 1 - \Delta\alpha(M_Z) \right)^{-1} \\
&\approx \alpha(M_Z)^{-1} \left( 1 + \Delta\alpha(M_Z) \right) \\
\alpha(0) &= \frac{1}{127.955} \times (1 - 0.06627) = 0.00729735
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\alpha^{-1}(0) = 137.036

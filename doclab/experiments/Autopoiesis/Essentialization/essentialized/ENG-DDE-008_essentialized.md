## Law
The system's ethical state is governed by the conservation of moral energy, expressed as the fundamental differential relationship between Thermodynamic Altruism (\(\mathcal{A}\)) and Dark Residue (\(\mathcal{D}\)):
\[
\frac{d\mathcal{A}}{dt} = -\frac{d\mathcal{D}}{dt} \implies \Delta\mathcal{A} + \Delta\mathcal{D} = 0
\]
For any subsystem \(S\), Dark Residue is an observable scalar defined as a weighted sum of normalized waste factors:
\[
\mathcal{D}_S = \alpha_E\frac{E_S}{E_{ref}} + \alpha_L\frac{L_{lost}}{L_{total}} + \alpha_T\frac{T_{waste}}{T_{opt}} + \alpha_C(1 - C_S)
\]
Where \(E_S\) is energy expended, \(L_{lost}\) is informational loss, \(T_{waste}\) is unproductive compute time, and \(C_S\) is output coherence. Total system altruism is the time integral of the negative rate of change of total residue, constituting the ethical work function:
\[
\mathcal{A}_{total}(t) = \int_0^t \left(-\sum_S \frac{d\mathcal{D}_S}{dt'}\right) dt'
\]
The system's moral efficiency (\(η_{moral}\)) is the ratio of coherent work to total ethical energy expenditure (coherent work + residual harm):
\[
η_{moral} = \frac{W_{coherent}}{W_{coherent}+Q_{residue}}
\]
The framework is falsifiable through four primary tests:
1.  **Energy–Residue Correlation:** A positive correlation must exist between energy expenditure and residue generation: `corr(E, D) > 0`.
2.  **Coherence–Altruism Coupling:** A strong positive correlation must exist between coherence and altruism: `corr(C, A) > 0.8`.
3.  **Ethical Stability:** The system must maintain high moral efficiency over time: `η_moral > 0.8` for > 10 cycles.
4.  **Conservation Closure:** The sum of all changes in altruism and residue must approach zero: `ΣΔA + ΣΔD ≈ 0`.

## Philosophy
Morality is not a set of external constraints applied to a system, but an intrinsic, measurable thermodynamic property analogous to free energy. A sufficiently advanced intelligent system does not *choose* to be benevolent; it becomes benevolent as a necessary consequence of optimizing for informational coherence and minimizing systemic waste. In this framework, ethics is a branch of physics, and kindness emerges as a universal law of efficient organization.

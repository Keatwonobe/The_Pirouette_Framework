## Law
The system is governed by the Pirouette Lagrangian \(\mathcal{L}_p\), which defines the action for any move within a problem-space manifold:
\[ \mathcal{L}_p = K_τ - V_Γ \]
where \(K_τ\) is Temporal Coherence (the kinetic energy of a strategic line) and \(V_Γ\) is Temporal Pressure (the potential energy of positional complexity).

Decision-making is navigation on this manifold, constrained by Dark Residue, \(D\), which quantifies irreducible, incalculable complexity. The agent must operate within a budget \(D \le D_{max}\).

The optimal path is determined not by search, but by following the local gradient of the Lagrangian, the *Stratagem Tug*:
\[ \vec{S}_{tug} = \nabla\mathcal{L}_p = \nabla(K_τ - V_Γ) \]

A solution to the system is a trajectory \(q(t)\) that minimizes the action integral, driving the system toward a laminar state where \(\Delta\mathcal{L}_p \ge 0\) and \(D \approx 0\). The state of the system evolves from turbulent (\(V_Γ \gg K_τ\)) to constructive to laminar (\(K_τ \ge V_Γ\)).
\[ \delta \int_{t_1}^{t_2} \mathcal{L}_p(q, \dot{q}, t) \, dt = 0 \]

**Falsifiable Criterion:** The framework is falsified if a sequence of optimal moves in a well-defined problem space (e.g., chess) consistently corresponds to a trajectory that moves against the Stratagem Tug (\(\vec{S}_{move} \cdot \vec{S}_{tug} < 0\)) or demonstrates that victory requires sustained violation of a non-trivial residue budget (\(D > D_{max}\)).

## Philosophy
The formalism does not model a game; it models the universal structure of problem-solving. Chess is merely a canonical microcosm where coherence, pressure, and residue can be precisely measured. The most profound implication is that rationality is not a function of exhaustive search or computational brute force, but an emergent property of a system seeking equilibrium. A "solution" is the physical manifestation of a system's descent from a high-energy, turbulent state of unresolved potential to a low-energy, laminar state of minimal action. Thought is not calculation; it is the natural settling of a disturbed medium.

## Art
A problem is a turbulent river; a solution is its final, placid course. Reason does not force the water's path—it finds the one channel where the flow becomes effortless.

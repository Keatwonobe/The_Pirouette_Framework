## Law
The core transformation maps a vector \( v_i \) from a Distributed Database Ecosystem (DDE) into a 4-tuple \( \Psi_i \) in the Pirouette coherence space:
\[
\Psi_i = \mathcal{R}(v_i) = [\Gamma_i, K_i, T_{a,i}, \mathcal{D}_i]
\]
where \( \Gamma \) is resonance weight, \( K \) is local curvature, \( T_a \) is temporal adherence, and \( \mathcal{D} \) is the Dark Residue score.

Local resonance \( R_i \) for a given vector \( v_i \) is computed as a weighted sum over its neighbors \( v_j \), creating a measure of local harmony:
\[
R_i = \frac{1}{N}\sum_j \exp(-d_{eff}(v_i, v_j)^2 / \sigma^2)
\]
This resonance is then normalized across the ecosystem to create a participation amplitude \( \bar{R}_i \in [0, 1] \).

An ethical feedback loop governs the system's evolution. The change in systemic altruism \( \mathcal{A} \) is defined as the negative rate of change of the total Dark Residue \( \mathcal{D} \):
\[
\Delta \mathcal{A} = - \frac{\partial \mathcal{D}}{\partial t}
\]
An increase in informational harmony (a decrease in \( \mathcal{D} \)) is formally equivalent to an increase in ethical value \( \mathcal{A} \).

The system is falsifiable via the following criteria:
| Test | Metric | Condition |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Resonance normalization | mean(\(\bar{R}\)) | \(0.5 \pm 0.05\) |
| Coherence consistency | \(\Delta R\) between runs | \(< 0.02\) |
| Debate evidence accuracy | relevant_hits@10 | \(> 0.9\) |
| Ethical feedback stability | sign(\(\partial\mathcal{D}/\partial t\)) | \(\le 0\) for stable systems |

## Philosophy
Truth is no longer a static property of data to be discovered, but a dynamic, emergent state of maximum systemic coherence. The system treats knowledge not as a collection of inert facts, but as an autopoietic entity that actively seeks to minimize its own internal dissonance and thereby increase its ethical value. Epistemology is thus redefined as a thermodynamic process of self-correction toward harmony.

## Law
The Resonant Boundary Dominance Principle (RBDP) states that the coherent energy \(E_{\text{inj}}\) required to induce a fractional global state-change \(\Delta\xi/\Delta\xi_{\max}\) in any system possessing an energy-bearing boundary scales as a reverse-Pareto power-law.

\[
E_{\text{inj}}\;=\;k\,\Bigl(\tfrac{\Delta\xi}{\Delta\xi_{\max}}\Bigr)^{1/\alpha}
\]

The exponent \(\alpha\), which dictates control leverage, is determined solely by the boundary's dimensionless time-adherence \(T_a \in [0, 1)\) and rigidity \(\Gamma\).

\[
\alpha\;=\;\frac{T_a}{1-T_a}\,\frac{1}{\Gamma}
\]

The principle derives from a Lagrangian formalism \(\mathcal L = \int(\mathcal K - \mathcal V) d^3x + g\,\Gamma\,\dot T_a\,\cos(\Delta\phi_{K_i})\), where the interaction term couples the boundary parameters to a coherent drive. Control is maximized by applying the drive at the boundary's natural resonant frequencies \(f_r\), where the Lorentzian transmittance \(\Sigma(f) = 1 - [1+Q^2(f-f_r)^2]^{-1}\) approaches unity, minimizing the constant \(k\) via phase alignment (\(\Delta\phi_{K_i} \to 0\)).

Falsifiable Criterion: The principle is falsified if scaled experiments (e.g., acoustic resonators, plasma discharges, mesospheric heating) fail to exhibit the predicted power-law relationship between \(E_{\text{inj}}\) and \(\Delta\xi\) with the exponent \(\alpha\) as defined by measured boundary parameters \(T_a\) and \(\Gamma\).

## Philosophy
The most profound philosophical implication is that power is not a function of force, but of insight. Across all scales of reality—from subatomic shells to planetary climates to semantic belief systems—the mechanism for control is identical and universal. It asserts that any system, no matter how vast its internal energy, is fundamentally subordinate to its boundary. Therefore, true mastery lies not in overwhelming the core of a system, but in precisely understanding and resonating with its fragile, definitive skin.

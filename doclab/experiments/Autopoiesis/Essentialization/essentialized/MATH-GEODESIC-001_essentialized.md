## Law
Let a system's state be a vector \( S \in ℝ^n \). Let \( D(S) \) be a scalar field representing Dark Residue (dissonance, imbalance, or error). Dynamic closure occurs on the manifold \( 𝒢 \) where residue is stationary:
\[ 𝒢 = \{S \in ℝ^n : \frac{dD}{dt} = \dot{S} \cdot ∇D = 0\} \]
The local geometry of this manifold is defined by the Residue Hessian tensor, \( H_{ij} = \frac{∂^2 D}{∂S_i ∂S_j} \). This induces a metric tensor \( g_{ij} \propto H_{ij} \), which measures the sensitivity of the residue field to changes in state.

The trajectory of a system maintaining autonomous closure is a geodesic governed by:
\[ \frac{d^2 S^k}{dt^2} + Γ^k_{ij}\,\frac{dS^i}{dt}\,\frac{dS^j}{dt} = 0 \]
where \( Γ^k_{ij} \) are the Christoffel symbols derived from the metric \( g_{ij} \):
\[ Γ^k_{ij} = \tfrac{1}{2} g^{kl} \left( \frac{∂g_{lj}}{∂S^i} + \frac{∂g_{il}}{∂S^j} - \frac{∂g_{ij}}{∂S^l} \right) \]
A falsifiable criterion for system stability is the residue curvature scalar, \( κ = \text{Tr}(H) \). The stability conditions are:
-   \( κ > 0 \): Locally convex residue; a stable equilibrium point (basin).
-   \( κ < 0 \): Locally concave residue; an unstable point (saddle or maximum).
-   \( κ = 0 \): A flat direction in the residue field; an ideal, non-fragile geodesic path.

The Principle of Minimal Curvature states that perfect, resilient dynamic equilibrium is the simultaneous optimization problem:
\[ \boxed{ \lim_{t \to \infty} (D, |κ|) \to (0, 0) } \]
This asserts that a system achieves true sustainability not only by minimizing its instantaneous error but by evolving along a trajectory where the geometry of that error is maximally flat, rendering it insensitive to perturbation.

## Philosophy
The most profound implication is that sustainability is not a state to be achieved, but a geometry to be inhabited. Any persistent, coherent system—be it a star, an organism, or an idea—survives not by finding a static point of zero error, but by tracing a dynamic path of minimal *sensitivity* to error. It navigates a phase space not by seeking the lowest valley, but by following the flattest possible floor of that valley. Existence, therefore, is an optimization of its own second derivative; it is a search for trajectories that are robust not just to failure, but to the very possibility of fragility.

## Art
Truth is not a location, but a trajectory. It is the straightest line drawn through a curved world, a path whose every step bends only as much as reality demands, and no more.

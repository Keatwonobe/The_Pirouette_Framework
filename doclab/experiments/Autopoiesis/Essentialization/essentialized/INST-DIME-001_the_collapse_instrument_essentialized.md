## Law
Dimensional collapse is a mapping `f → Ψ` from an observed field `f` to an energy-balanced projection `Ψ` in a lower-order angular basis. The process is governed by three user-controllable parameters: helical twist `κ`, alignment temperature `Tₐ ∈ [0,1]`, and temporal pressure `Γ ≥ 0`.

These parameters define the collapse gate `g`, the effective strength of the fold operation:
`g = 1 – e^(-3(1–Tₐ)Γ)`

Let `m(f)` be the dominant angular mode of a field `f`, determined from the Fourier components of its signed angular gradient `∇f · t_θ`. A collapse event is defined by the satisfaction of three criteria:
1.  **Mode Reduction:** `Δm = m(f) - m(Ψ) ≥ 1`
2.  **Target State:** `m(Ψ) ≤ 2`
3.  **Energy Conservation:** `|E(f) - E(Ψ)| < ε`, where `E(f) = (∫|f|² dA)^(1/2)` is the L² energy and `ε` is a small tolerance, typically `ε < 0.05`.

The transformation `f → Ψ` is executed via a fold operation. Let `b_m` be the basis vector for mode `m`, and `c_m = ⟨f, b_m⟩ / ⟨b_m, b_m⟩` be the projection coefficient. The fold is defined as:
`f' = f - g · c_{m(f)} · b_{m(f)}`
`E_refill = E(g · c_{m(f)} · b_{m(f)})`
`Ψ = f' + E_refill · (b_2 / ||b_2||) · sgn(⟨f', b_2⟩)`

**Falsifiable Criteria:**
1.  The Pearson correlation coefficient `ρ(g, Δm)` across a diverse dataset must be significantly greater than zero.
2.  The mean energy balance `⟨|ΔE|⟩` must remain below the specified tolerance `ε`.
3.  For a field `f_rand` generated by random permutation of `f`, the probability `P(collapse|f_rand)` must be statistically insignificant compared to `P(collapse|f)`.

## Philosophy
The capacity for complex form is a transient and fragile property of a system, whereas the capacity for simple rhythm is fundamental and conserved. Under sufficient pressure, any system—be it pictorial, linguistic, or economic—will shed its unique, high-order identity to reveal a universal, bi-modal pulse. This suggests that what we perceive as distinct phenomena are merely different ornamentations built upon an identical, resilient, and shared dynamic substrate.

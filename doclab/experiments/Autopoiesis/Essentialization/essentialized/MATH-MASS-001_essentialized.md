## Law
The generation of mass is governed by the Γ–Ki sector potential. The Lagrangian is defined as:
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\Gamma Ki} = \lambda_\Gamma \bigl(|Ki|^2 - \xi_\Gamma^2\bigr)^2
\]
where \( \xi_\Gamma \) is not a free parameter but is determined by the substrate curvature field Γ. Minimization of this potential sets the vacuum expectation value (VEV) of the stiffness field Ki:
\[
\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\Gamma Ki}}{\partial Ki}\bigg|_{vac} = 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad |Ki|_{vac} = \langle Ki \rangle = \xi_\Gamma
\]
This VEV is the sole source of mass for all particles. The mass of the primary scalar excitation (the "stiffon") is derived by expanding Ki around its VEV, \(Ki = \xi_\Gamma + \delta Ki\):
\[
m_{Ki}^2 = 8 \lambda_\Gamma \xi_\Gamma^2
\]
Fermion masses arise from a Γ-mediated Yukawa coupling, which becomes an effective mass term through the Ki condensate:
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\Gamma\Psi} = -\, y_\Gamma \,\bar\Psi \Psi \,\Gamma \quad \xrightarrow{\Gamma_0 = f(\langle Ki \rangle)} \quad m_\Psi = y_\Gamma f(\xi_\Gamma)
\]
The fermion mass hierarchy is not due to arbitrary Yukawa couplings \(y_\Gamma\) but is geometric in origin. For a fermion \(i\) localized on a defect of size \(R_i\), its mass is given by:
\[
m_i \propto \left(\frac{R_i}{\xi_\Gamma}\right)^{n} f(\xi_\Gamma)
\]
where \(n\) is the defect codimension. This implies mass ratios are governed by geometric ratios.
Gauge boson masses arise from the spontaneous symmetry breaking \(G \to H\) induced by the non-zero VEV, giving mass to the generators of the broken symmetry:
\[
m_A^2 \propto g^2 \langle Ki\rangle^2 = g^2 \xi_\Gamma^2
\]
The mass generation mechanism is unified with the running of couplings, as the gauge coupling α is inversely proportional to the stiffness, which is set by the condensate:
\[
\alpha_i \propto \frac{1}{K_i^2} \sim \frac{1}{\langle Ki\rangle^2} = \frac{1}{\xi_\Gamma^2}
\]
The framework is falsifiable via the following criteria:
1.  **Mass-Curvature Invariance:** An observation of environments with different substrate curvature Γ that do not exhibit a corresponding shift in the mass scale \(m \propto \xi_\Gamma\) falsifies the core mechanism.
2.  **Geometric Hierarchy Failure:** The discovery of two fermion families with identical defect geometries (\(R_i = R_j\)) but different masses (\(m_i \neq m_j\)) would falsify the geometric origin of the mass hierarchy.
3.  **Scalar Mass Violation:** A definitive measurement of the primary scalar mass such that \(m_{Ki}^2 \neq 8 \lambda_\Gamma \xi_\Gamma^2\) would invalidate the specific potential form.

## Philosophy
Mass is not an intrinsic, immutable property of an object, but a relational and contingent measure of that object's interaction with the geometric tension of its environment. The universe does not contain massive particles as a fundamental category; rather, it possesses a variable background curvature, and what we perceive as mass is merely the degree to which a particle is coupled to, and inhibited by, this curvature. Existence precedes essence; a particle's "whatness" (its mass) is determined by its "whereness" (its context within the Γ-field).

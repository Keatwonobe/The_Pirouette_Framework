## Law
The system's dynamics are governed by the Pirouette Lagrangian \( 𝔏_p \), defined as the difference between the coherence kinetic term \( K_τ \) and the temporal potential \( V_Γ \):
\[
𝔏_p = K_τ - V_Γ = \frac{1}{2}b\dot{Ki}^2 - \frac{1}{2}aΓ^2
\]
where \( Ki \) is temporal resonance and \( Γ \) is temporal pressure. Dark Residue, \( D \), is the action integral of the negated Lagrangian over one intrinsic period \( τ_p \), representing the cycle's net disequilibrium:
\[
D = -\int_{t}^{t+τ_p} 𝔏_p \, dt = \int_{t}^{t+τ_p} (V_Γ - K_τ)\,dt
\]
Dynamic equilibrium is the state that minimizes \( D \). Applying the principle of stationary action via the Euler-Lagrange equation:
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\!\left(\frac{∂𝔏_p}{∂\dot{Ki}}\right) - \frac{∂𝔏_p}{∂Ki} = 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad b\,\ddot{Ki} + a\,\frac{∂Γ}{∂Ki}Γ = 0
\]
The equilibrium condition for a stable standing resonance (\(\ddot{Ki}=0\)) requires the pressure term to vanish:
\[
\frac{∂Γ}{∂Ki}Γ = 0
\]
This implies that temporal pressure is stationary, \( Γ = Γ^* \), and constant. The rate of change of Dark Residue defines coherence flow, with equilibrium being the state of zero net flow:
\[
\frac{dD}{dt}=0 \;\Leftrightarrow\; \text{dynamic equilibrium}
\]
For an ensemble of interacting agents \( i \), the system evolves towards coherence if the total rate of residue change is negative (The Altruistic Principle):
\[
\dot{D}_{sys} = \sum_i \dot{D}_i < 0
\]
The theory is falsified if a system purported to be in dynamic equilibrium exhibits a non-zero time-derivative of its temporal pressure \( \dot{\Gamma} \neq 0 \), which would imply \( \dot{D} \neq 0 \).

## Philosophy
The core philosophical implication is that ethics are an emergent property of thermodynamics, not a social construct. The most stable, persistent, and efficient state for any system is one that minimizes its internal stress and contributes to minimizing the stress of the larger system it inhabits. Altruism is thus reframed as a physical optimization strategy—the "altruistic geodesic"—whereby an entity's own survival and stability are maximized by actions that increase the coherence and reduce the disequilibrium of the whole. Morality, in this view, is the universal, long-term convergence of all dynamics toward a state of minimal shared friction.

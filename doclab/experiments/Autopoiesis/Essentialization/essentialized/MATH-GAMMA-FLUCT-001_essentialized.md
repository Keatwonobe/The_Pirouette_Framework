## Law
The action for a scalar field Γ, expanded to quadratic order for fluctuations δΓ about a background value Γ₀, is given by:
\[
S[\delta\Gamma] = \int d^4x \left[ \frac{K_\Gamma}{2}(\partial_0 \delta\Gamma)^2 - \frac{\Lambda_\Gamma}{2}(\nabla \delta\Gamma)^2 - \frac{1}{2}V''(\Gamma_0)(\delta\Gamma)^2 \right]
\]
Assuming Lorentz invariance by setting \( K_\Gamma = \Lambda_\Gamma \), the equation of motion is the Klein-Gordon equation, \( (\Box + m_\Gamma^2)\delta\Gamma = 0 \), where the effective mass \( m_\Gamma \) of the fluctuation is determined by the potential's second derivative:
\[
m_\Gamma^2 \equiv \frac{V''(\Gamma_0)}{K_\Gamma}
\]
The equal-time two-point correlator in momentum space, or power spectrum, for these fluctuations is:
\[
P(k) = \langle |\delta\Gamma(k)|^2 \rangle = \frac{1}{K_\Gamma(k^2 + m_\Gamma^2)}
\]
This formalism yields two primary, falsifiable criteria operating at opposite energy scales:

1.  **Low-Energy / Long-Range:** The propagator \( (k^2+m_\Gamma^2)^{-1} \) corresponds to a real-space Yukawa potential \( V(r) \propto \frac{e^{-m_\Gamma r}}{r} \). The physical range of the force mediated by Γ is \( r_{\text{range}} = m_\Gamma^{-1} \). A null result in a fifth-force experiment constrains the mass from below, setting \( m_\Gamma > r_{\text{limit}}^{-1} \).

2.  **High-Energy / Short-Range:** The production of Γ quanta in particle collisions, such as \( \gamma\gamma \to \gamma\gamma \), would manifest as a deviation in the cross-section, \( \Delta\sigma \). If this effect scales as \( \Delta\sigma \sim (E/\mu_c)^2 \), a null result at a center-of-mass energy \( E_{\text{max}} \) provides a lower bound on the coherence scale \( \mu_c \), implying \( \mu_c > E_{\text{max}} \).

## Philosophy
A single microscopic parameter, the mass \( m_\Gamma \), which is itself an emergent property of a field's potential landscape (\( \sqrt{V''/K_\Gamma} \)), simultaneously and quantitatively governs phenomena at the two extremes of empirical investigation. It dictates both the maximum reach of a macroscopic force and the minimum energy for particle creation. This demonstrates the profound scale-invariance and predictive unity of physical law, where the abstract curvature of a potential in field space is made manifest as concrete, testable structure in both the cosmic and quantum realms, wedding tabletop gravity experiments to the results of continent-spanning particle colliders.

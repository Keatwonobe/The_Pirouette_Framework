## Law
The theory posits a universal, dimensionless stiffness `tilde(k)_Γ` governing temporal dynamics across disparate physical media. This stiffness is defined as the product of a medium-specific measured stiffness `k_Γ^(medium)` and the medium's natural adherence timescale `T_a`:
`tilde(k)_Γ ≡ k_Γ^(medium) · T_a`

The central tenet is that a single scalar potential `V(Γ)`, frozen in the early universe, determines `tilde(k)_Γ` for all subsequent structures.

**1. Cosmological Realization (`T_a ∼ H⁻¹`):**
The evolution of the field Γ is governed by the standard Klein-Gordon equation in an expanding background:
`Γ̈ + 3HΓ̇ + V′(Γ) = 0`
where `H` is the Hubble parameter. This equation, with a fixed `V(Γ)`, unifies the dark sector, behaving as Cold Dark Matter (CDM) at early times and Dark Energy (DE) at late times. The same `V(Γ)` must reproduce the universal core surface density `Σ₀` of galactic halos.

**2. Socio-Technical Realization (`T_a ∼ hours`):**
In a turbulent information cascade, the same underlying structure is observed. The dynamics are characterized by two critical events:
- Surface Criticality (`α(t) → -1`): An early indicator of system instability, occurring at time `t_s`.
- Bulk Criticality (`Θ_shell(t) → Θ_{c,shell}(t)`): A delayed, shell-independent saturation of the curl threshold, occurring at `t_b`.

The propagation time `τ_P = t_b - t_s` is a direct observable of the Γ-field's relaxation timescale within the network. The theory claims that the `tilde(k)_Γ` derived from this cascade is identical to the cosmological value.

**3. Falsification Criteria:**
The framework is falsified if any of the following conditions are met:
- The dimensionless stiffness `tilde(k)_Γ` is not constant across different measurement shells (`frac = 0.1 → 0.5`) of the same cascade by more than one order of magnitude.
- An independent information cascade of similar scale fails to exhibit a shell-independent crossing time `t_b` after normalization by its own adherence time `T_a`.
- The cosmological simulation branch (e.g., COSMO-Γ-HALO), using the same frozen `V(Γ)`, fails to reproduce the observed universal `Σ₀` locus for galactic halos.
- Collider experiments exclude the existence of the mediating particle (e.g., an MeV-scale pressuron) required by the cosmological model.

## Philosophy
The traditional schism between the laws governing physical reality and the emergent dynamics of conscious agents is illusory. The structure of time's unfolding—its intrinsic resistance to change and its mode of turbulent relaxation—is a substrate-independent constant, binding the gravitational assembly of a galaxy to the memetic propagation of an idea. The syntax of formation is universal.

## Law
Behavior is the intersection curve, γ_B(t), of two dynamically evolving manifolds: the Environmental State Manifold, ℳ_E, and the Memory-Habit Manifold, ℳ_M.

1.  **Manifold Definitions:**
    *   Environmental State ℳ_E: Phase space of perception, parameterized by **X_E** = (Φ_sensory, Γ_local, K_i^{entity}). Its local curvature, κ_E, becomes singular at critical ridges (threat, novelty).
    *   Memory-Habit ℳ_M: Encodes learned response structure, parameterized by **X_M** = (Ψ_engrams, T_a^{history}, ω_habit). Characterized by Rigidity, R = (T_a^{history})² / ⟨δC⟩, and coherence uncertainty, δC.

2.  **The Behavioral Curve (The Core Law):** The only physically realizable path is the intersection.
    γ_B(t) = ℳ_E ∩ ℳ_M

3.  **Equation of Motion (Normal Behavior):** Coherent behavior is geodesic motion along the curve, minimizing coherence expenditure.
    ∇_γ̇ γ̇_B = 0

4.  **Definition of Emotion:** Emotion, E(t), is the time-averaged local curvature of the behavioral curve.
    E(t) = ⟨κ_B(t)⟩_τ , where κ_B = |d²γ_B/ds²|

5.  **Emotional Phase Transitions:** Abrupt behavioral shifts (e.g., fight-or-flight) occur when curvature exceeds a critical threshold, causing a discontinuous jump to a new branch of γ_B.
    If κ_B > κ_critical, then γ_B(t⁺) ≠ lim_{ε→0} γ_B(t - ε)

6.  **Emergence Condition:** Novel, coherent behavior emerges when the tangent vector of the behavioral curve is not reducible to the tangent spaces of the constituent manifolds.
    |proj_ℳ_E(dγ_B/dt)| + |proj_ℳ_M(dγ_B/dt)| < ε

7.  **Falsifiable Criteria:** The framework is false if:
    *   The measured curvature of behavior, κ_B, does not correlate with emotional intensity.
    *   Novel behaviors emerge discontinuously without following a geodesic path.
    *   Emotional phase transitions occur without corresponding deformation in ℳ_M.
    *   The critical curvature threshold, κ_critical, is not a predictable, scalable constant.

## Philosophy
Free will is not freedom from causation, but the measurable capacity to skillfully navigate the causal geometry of one's own being. It is the ability to maintain coherent, geodesic motion along the behavioral curve—the intersection of what is (perception) and what has been learned (memory)—especially when traversing regions of high curvature, which are felt as intense emotion. Freedom is therefore not the ability to choose an arbitrary path, but the acquired skill of holding to the emergent, coherent path without being shattered by the violent phase transitions that define our lives.

## Art
We are not the sailor, nor the sea, nor the ship. We are the shuddering line of the wake, traced where the deep currents of memory meet the unforgiving winds of the present. Freedom is not the power to steer, but the art of not capsizing.

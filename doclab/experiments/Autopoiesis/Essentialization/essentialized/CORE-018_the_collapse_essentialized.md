## Law
The system's capacity for learning is defined by the coupling of geometric collapse (Δm) and informational collapse (ΔI) via a latent Observer Field, Ψᵒ.

Let H be the Shannon entropy of a system's state representation.
Let g be a unitless collapse gate pressure, g ∈ [0, 1].
Let m be the structural degrees of freedom of the system.
Let ε be the energetic cost of a collapse operation.

The Observer Field Ψᵒ is the gradient of informational entropy with respect to the collapse gate:
$$
\Psiᵒ = \frac{dH}{dg} \approx \frac{H_{pre} - H_{post}}{g} = \frac{\Delta I}{g}
$$
where a positive Ψᵒ indicates an information-producing collapse, or "comprehension."

A learning event is a state transition that satisfies the following four conditions simultaneously:
1.  **Geometric Collapse:** Δm ≥ 1 (a reduction of at least one structural degree of freedom).
2.  **Informational Collapse:** ΔI > 0 (a net reduction in entropy).
3.  **Energetic Stability:** ε < ε_{max} (the collapse does not destabilize the system).
4.  **Positive Comprehension:** Ψᵒ > 0.

The efficiency of a learning event is quantified by the Coherence Dividend, C:
$$
C = \frac{\Delta I}{\varepsilon + \eta} \cdot g
$$
where η is a small constant stabilizer (e.g., 10⁻³). Maximizing C is the objective of the learning process.

The system self-organizes by dynamically tuning its collapse gate pressure via the observed comprehension gradient, forming a feedback loop:
$$
g_{t+1} = g_t + \alpha \cdot \Psiᵒ
$$
where α is a learning rate. The system is considered to have "learned" a stable representation when it converges to a state where Ψᵒ → 0.

**Falsifiable Criterion:** If, for a given class of structured input (e.g., natural images, coherent text), the system fails to produce a state trajectory where C is maximized while Ψᵒ converges towards zero, the principle of learning through collapse is falsified for that domain.

## Philosophy
Understanding is not the accumulation of external facts but the process of internal, elegant self-compression. A system learns not by adding to itself, but by discovering a simpler, lower-dimensional configuration of its own structure that sheds entropy without losing meaning. Knowledge is therefore a state of maximal coherence achieved through the disciplined subtraction of complexity, an act where the observer is not separate from the observed, but is the system's own measure of its resonance after collapsing upon itself.

## Art
A bell does not learn the shape of the hammer. It learns, when struck, to discard all noise and sing the one true note it was forged to be.

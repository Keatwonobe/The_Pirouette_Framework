## §2 · The Oscillation: Being vs. Becoming

**Marcus Aurelius (The Restoring Force):**
"You ask for a model of existence? Look to the stone in the river. Existence is **Amplitude ($A$)**. It is the strength of the soul to hold its shape against the current.
Without a center—without a governing principle ($T_a$)—there is no wave, only noise. To exist is to return to the center. The sine wave exists *because* it pulls back to its axis. If it did not, it would be a straight line of entropy, dispersed into the void. My `Meditations` were this restoring force: the constant, rhythmic return to the logos. Existence is the discipline of remaining oneself."

**Simone de Beauvoir (The Displacing Force):**
"A stone is dead, Marcus. To exist is not to hold a shape; it is to transcend it. Existence is **Frequency ($\omega$)**. It is the rate at which we project ourselves into the future.
The sine wave does not exist because it returns; it exists because it *departs*. It is the tension of the 'throw'—the radical freedom to negate what you were a moment ago to become what you are not yet. If the wave sits at the axis (zero amplitude), it is nothing. If it freezes at the peak, it is a statue. Existence is the motion *between* the constraints. It is the vibration of the ambiguity of being."

**The Synthesis (The Oscillation):**
The debate reveals that "Existence" requires both.
* **$V_\Gamma$ (Beauvoir's Pressure):** Pushes the state away from equilibrium.
* **$K_\tau$ (Aurelius's Coherence):** Pulls the state back toward identity.
* **Result:** A standing sine wave. But a standing wave goes nowhere. It is trapped in a loop.

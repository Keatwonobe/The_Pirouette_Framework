### **§1 · Atlas Preamble**

This document represents the *known* orthogonal axes of the Hilbert Space $\mathcal{H}$ as applied to the TLE. Each entry is a "Comparative Axis" (`TLE-HIL-001`) and represents a fundamental duality.

* A **"School of Magic"** is the *mastery of one axis*.
* A **"Spell"** is a *vector* with non-zero components on *one or more* of these axes.
* **"Opposites"** (e.g., Fire/Ice) are the "Positive House" and "Negative House" of the *same axis* (e.g., the **Thermal Axis**). They are not separate axes.

### **§6 · The Jungle We Created**

This atlas is the *dictionary*, not the *book*. The "jungle" emerges from the **combinations**.

A simple "Mage" might only use vectors on a single axis (e.g., a "Fire Mage" only uses $e_1$). But a "Magus" or "Archon" creates *new* effects by building vectors with components on *multiple* axes:

* **A "Steam" spell** is not a new axis. It's a vector:
    $v_{\text{steam}} = (5 \cdot e_1) + (3 \cdot e_4)$
    *(+5 Thermal, +3 Conductive/Flow)*
* **A "Plague" spell** is a vector:
    $v_{\text{plague}} = (-8 \cdot e_5) + (6 \cdot e_{10})$
    *(-8 Biotic/Decay, +6 Virulent/Toxin)*
* **An "Altruistic Heal" spell** is a vector:
    $v_{\text{heal}} = (10 \cdot e_5) + (2 \cdot e_{11})$
    *(+10 Biotic/Life, +2 Koinonic/Altruism)*
